# Tread - SXS Edition Owner's Manual - Dog Tracking

# Dog Tracking

The dog tracking features allow you to see information transmitted from your handheld dog device on your
Tread® - SXS Edition device. You can navigate to your dogs, view their current status and location, and observe the distance they have traveled.
