# Electric motors for commercial vehicles

#### Compact and scalable electric drive for battery-electric and fuel cell commercial vehicles

The electric motor converts electrical energy into mechanical power and provides electric propulsion. The electric motor transmits its torque directly to the transmission gear based on the application. By converting mechanical to electrical energy, electric motor charges the vehicle's battery.

## Electric motor portfolio



* The electric motors with a nominal voltage of 800 V are designed for the service life requirements of commercial vehicles.
* The compact design is optimized for easy integration into the electric axle.
* The electric motors are available in various power levels between 150 kW and 240 kW of continuous power.
