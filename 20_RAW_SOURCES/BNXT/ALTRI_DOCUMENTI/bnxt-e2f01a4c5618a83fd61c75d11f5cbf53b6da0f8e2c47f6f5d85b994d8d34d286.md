# Tread Owner's Manual - Pairing Additional Bluetooth Devices

# Pairing Additional Bluetooth Devices

1. Place your headset or phone and your
   Bluetooth® device within 33 ft. (10 m) of each other.
2. On your device, enable
   Bluetooth wireless technology.
3. On your headset or phone, enable
   Bluetooth wireless technology and ensure it is visible to other
   Bluetooth devices.
4. On your device, select
    > Wireless Networks > Bluetooth > Pair new device.

   A list of nearby
   Bluetooth devices appears.
5. Select your headset or phone from the list.
6. If necessary, select
   Pair.
