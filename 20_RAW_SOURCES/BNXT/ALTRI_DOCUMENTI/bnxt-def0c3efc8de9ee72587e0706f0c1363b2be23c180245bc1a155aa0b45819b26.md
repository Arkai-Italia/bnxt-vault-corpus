# Tread - Overland Edition Tread XL - Overland Edition Owner's Manual - My device is not acquiring satellite signals

# My device is not acquiring satellite signals

* Verify the location services feature is enabled ([Enabling or Disabling Location Services](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-157A2412-594F-45D8-A5EE-5A5327DA0914/EN-US/GUID-814E0376-A0DD-48B1-AB73-2268666CF053.html)).
* Take your device out of parking garages and away from tall buildings and trees.
* Remain stationary for several minutes.
