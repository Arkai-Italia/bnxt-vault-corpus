# Tread Owner's Manual - BirdsEye Imagery

# BirdsEye Imagery

BirdsEye imagery provides downloadable, high-resolution map imagery, including detailed satellite imagery and raster-based topographical maps. You can download
BirdsEye Satellite Imagery directly to your device when it is connected to a
Wi‑Fi® network.
