# **<u>1. INTRODUCTION</u>**

The Alpinestars’ heated model HT HEAT TECH Vest (hereinafter “Vest”) is designed to offer improved thermal comfort to the motorcycle rider in cold  weather conditions due to the innovative heating technology embedded inside the Vest. This technology is made of flexible, thin and lightweight graphene heating pad elements, which due to their enhanced performances in terms of resistance, heating efficiency and safety, provide unrivalled levels of warmth and comfort without the bulk typically caused by excessive layers of materials.

The Vest’s electronic components, are equipped with safety systems to provide controlled and even heating. Moreover battery is provided with systems to avoid overheating and overcharging anomalies. The Vest can be operated safely in various weather conditions due to the combination of selected materials, premium construction techniques and innovative heating technology.

The Vest is not designed as an outer garment. It should be worn underneath a motorcycle garment such as a jacket or suit. The vest is not waterproof. It may be used in rain conditions provided that the outer garment is water resistant. Heating performance will be impaired if exposed to water or rain conditions.

The Vest is powered by a lightweight, rechargeable and powerful Lithium Polymer battery.

**WARNING! The Vest is not suitable for children under the age of 16 years. Do not allow children to play with the Vest or its accessories.**

- **WARNING! As with any electrical device, misuse of the Vest or failure to follow the instructions may cause overheating, fire or injury to you or your property.**

- **WARNING! Do not attempt to repair any electrical part or component of the Vest. This will void the warranty and can result in temperature control malfunctions, which may cause minor to severe burning.**

- **IMPORTANT!** Damaged heating elements may cause the Vest to heat intermittently or completely fail to heat. If the heating elements do not operate properly contact Alpinestars.

# **<u>2. LIMITATIONS ON USE</u>**

**WARNING! The Vest does not offer any form of protection in case of an accident or fall.**

**The Vest is NOT personal protective equipment nor is it an impact protector .**

Remember that sensible motorcycling requires that the full body must be protected and as such the Vest should be worn with correctly CE certified and fitting motorcycle clothing such as a jacket or suit, gloves, boots and an homologated helmet.

The Vest has been designed exclusively for motorcycling use and is  not for use in other sporting, nonsporting or industrial activities.

Users should be aware that different environmental conditions including high or low temperatures can change the characteristics of the Vest and may reduce its performance.

The Vest and its components, in particular the batteries found on the Vest, may not activate if frozen or stored in extreme cold. Should this happen, allow the Vest and its components to thaw and/ or warm up before attempting to use or activate the heated Vest.

# **<u>5. BATTERY CHARGING INSTRUCTIONS</u>**

To charge the batteries before first use and/or recharge the batteries:

1. Plug the Wall Charger (9) to a standard wall plug using the appropriate adaptor and then connect it to the DC port (7) of the battery (6).

- **IMPORTANT!** Connect the wall Charger (9) to the power source. Do not use outdoors or in damp areas. Do not alter or subject the wall Charger (9) to damage or stress. Never alter the components.

- **IMPORTANT!** Do not turn on or wear the Vest during the charging process. It is essential that the Vest is switched off before starting the charging process.

2. When the Battery LED indicator (10) is RED the battery is not fully charged and is still charging.

- **IMPORTANT!** While charging, always ensure that the wall Charger (9) is properly connected to both the DC port (7) and the AC adapter is securely inserted in the power source.

3. When the LED Battery indicator (10) is GREEN the battery is fully charged and ready for use or storage Approximately 2 hours charging time is required to fully charge the battery, with the exception of the first battery charge which may require a longer time (approx. 4 hours).

The battery will naturally discharge itself over time. If the Vest has not been used for more than a month, battery charging may also require for a longer time; approximately four hours. It is also recommended to recharge the batteries at least every 16 weeks to prevent capacity reduction.

**IMPORTANT!** A fully charged battery is recommended before the first use of the Vest.

For the best performance, Alpinestars recommends to fully charge the battery before each use of the Vest.

**IMPORTANT!** Alpinestars recommends to always use the original Alpinestars wall Charger (9) and battery (6) parts.

# **<u>8. CLEANING AND MAINTENANCE</u>**

**WARNING! The Vest, including its components, are advanced pieces of motorcycling equipment and should not be treated like a normal motorcycle garment. Similar to one’s motorcycle, the Vest and its components must be cared for and maintained, so that they may function correctly.**

## **CLEANING**

To clean the Vest, firstly switch the Vest OFF and remove the battery (6) before starting to clean the Vest. Thereafter follow the below instructions:

The Vest and/or their components should NOT be

- washed in a washing machine

- submerged in water

- tumble dried

- ironed

- cleaned with any chemical or abrasive cleaning agents

Keep batteries, cables and adapters away from water.

Vest should be regularly cleaned using a damp cloth. It is preferable to clean the Vest after each use as such prompt cleaning will avoid the build-up of dirt and/or grease marks over time and facilitate easier cleaning. The longer such dirt is left on the Vest, the harder it will be to remove such dirt. Do not clean under running water. Follow the washing instructions located on the Vest. If deeper cleaning is needed, always use a soft, non-abrasive, damp cloth with neutral soap and use gentle circular movements to remove or reduce the amount of dirt on the Vest. Do not aggressively rub the dirty area and do not use detergents (other than neutral soap) nor chemical agents or solvents for cleaning.

After cleaning the Vest should be allowed to dry naturally in a warm, well ventilated area.

During the drying process do not place the Vest or its components in contact with fire or heaters or other heat sources, as such actions will compromise the qualities of the Vest.

Do not wring the Vest out, as such actions will compromise the qualities of the Vest.

Do not let the Vest or their components come into contact with solvents (including petrol) as this may degrade the Vest materials and/or affect the colors.

# **<u>10. STORAGE AND TRANSPORT</u>**

When not in use, store the cleaned Vest in a clean, dust-free environment at room temperature (15-25°C / 59 – 77 °F). Ensure that the Vest is completely dry before storing. To prevent mildew or other degradation to the Vest, store in a dry and well-ventilated area. Do not store inside a sealed bag or container, or underneath heavy objects.

Store the Vest with the battery (6) disconnected.

When storing the Vest and its components, do not store in places that will expose the Vest and its components to extreme heat sources such as fires or flames and neither to extreme cold sources that could result in the Vest or its components from freezing.

It is also recommended to fully charge the batteries before storing for extended periods of time.

When handling the Vest be careful not to drop or in any manner cut, tear or pierce the Vest. Protect the Vest against sharp objects.

Similarly, when transporting the Vest, the above care should be taken and in particular not to place heavy objects on the Vest that can bend or crease the Vest shape.

# **<u>11. DISPOSAL OF THE VEST AT THE END OF THEIR LIFE SPAN</u>**

At the end of your Vest lifespan be sure to dispose of it following your local/national refuse regulations and dispose it in an appropriate manner.

To dispose of the Vest in the appropriate manner, at the end of your Vest life span follow your local/ national refuse regulations.

Moreover, as the Vest contains electronic components, the Vest must be disposed of in accordance with the appropriate legislative or environmental measures adopted by the public waste disposal services, operating in your territory, for electronic components. ## **PULIZIA**

Il Gilet e/o i suoi componenti NON devono essere

- lavati in lavatrice

- Immersi in acqua

- asciugati in asciugatrice

- stirati

- puliti con qualsiasi detergente chimico o abrasivo

Tenere le batterie, i cavi e gli adattatori lontano dall’acqua.

Il Gilet deve essere pulito regolarmente utilizzando un panno umido. È preferibile pulire il Gilet dopo ogni utilizzo poiché tale pulizia tempestiva eviterà l’accumulo di sporco, segni di insetti e/o macchie di grasso nel tempo e faciliterà la pulizia. Non strofinare con eccessiva forza la zona sporca e non utilizzare detergenti (diversi dal sapone neutro) né agenti chimici o solventi per la pulizia.

Dopo la pulizia, il Gilet deve essere lasciato asciugare naturalmente in un’area calda e ben ventilata.

Durante il processo di asciugatura non mettere il Gilet né i suoi componenti a contatto con fiamme o stufe o altre fonti di calore, poiché tali azioni comprometteranno le qualità del Gilet.

Non strizzare il Gilet, poiché tali azioni ne comprometteranno le qualità.

Non lasciare che il Gilet o i suoi componenti entrino in contatto con solventi (inclusa la benzina) poiché ciò potrebbe danneggiarne i materiali e/o alterarne i colori.

## **LIMPIEZA**

Para limpiar el chaleco, primero apáguelo y retire la batería (6) antes de comenzar a limpiarlo. A partir de entonces, siga las instrucciones a continuación:

El chaleco o sus componentes NO deben ser

- lavados en una lavadora

- sumergidos en agua

- secados en secadora

- planchados

- limpiados con cualquier agente de limpieza químico o abrasivo

Mantenga las baterías, los cables y los adaptadores alejados del agua.

El chaleco debe limpiarse regularmente con un paño húmedo. Es preferible limpiar el chaleco después de cada uso, ya que esta limpieza rápida evitará la acumulación con el tiempo de suciedad, marcas de insectos o de grasa y facilitará la limpieza. Cuanto más tiempo permanezca esa suciedad en el chaleco, más difícil será eliminarla. No limpiar con agua corriente. Siga las instrucciones de lavado que se encuentran en el chaleco. Si se necesita una limpieza más profunda, utilice siempre un paño suave, no abrasivo y húmedo con jabón neutro y realice movimientos circulares suaves para eliminar o reducir la cantidad de suciedad en el chaleco. No frote agresivamente el área sucia y no utilice detergentes (que no sean jabón neutro) ni agentes químicos o solventes para limpiar.

Tras limpiar el chaleco, debe secarse de forma natural en un área cálida y bien ventilada.

Durante el proceso de secado, no coloque el chaleco ni sus componentes en contacto con el fuego o calentadores u otras fuentes de calor, ya que tales acciones comprometerán las cualidades del chaleco. No escurra el chaleco, ya que tal acción comprometerá sus cualidades.

No permita que el chaleco ni sus componentes entren en contacto con disolventes (incluida la gasolina), ya que esto puede degradar los materiales del chaleco o modificar los colores.
