# Tread Owner's Manual - Activating a Power Switch

# Activating a Power Switch

Before you can use the
Garmin PowerSwitch™ app to activate a switch, you must install your
Garmin PowerSwitch device in your vehicle and pair it with the
Garmin PowerSwitch app.

From the
Garmin PowerSwitch app, select a power button to turn a switch on or off.
