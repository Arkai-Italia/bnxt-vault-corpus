# Tread Owner's Manual - Connecting an inReach Device

# Connecting an inReach Device

1. Bring the
   inReach® device within 3 m (10 ft.) of your
   Tread® device.
2. On your
   Tread device, select
   Apps > Adventure > inReach.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions on your
   Tread device to complete the pairing process.

After the pairing process is complete, the
inReach and
Tread devices connect automatically when they are within range.
