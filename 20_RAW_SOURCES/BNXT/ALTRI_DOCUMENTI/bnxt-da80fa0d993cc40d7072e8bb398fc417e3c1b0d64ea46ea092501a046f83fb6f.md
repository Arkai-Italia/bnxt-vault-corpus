# Tread Owner's Manual - Following Your Recent Track

# Following Your Recent Track

The
TracBack feature records the most recent segment of your movement. You can retrace your recent track back to a previous location.

1. Select
   Apps > Adventure > TracBack.

   Your recent track appears on the map.
2. Select
   Go!.
