# Tread - SXS Edition Owner's Manual - Tracking

# Tracking

You can use the tracking feature on your
Tread® SXS Edition device to record track points and transmit them over the
Iridium® satellite network at the specified send interval. Track points appear on the
Garmin Explore™ website and the
MapShare™ tracking page.
