# Tread Owner's Manual - Recording Tracks

# Recording Tracks

Tracking records your path. When you start tracking, your device logs your location and updates the track line. If your device is paired with a
Garmin Explore™ account, your device periodically synchronizes recorded tracks with the app.

1. From the main screen, select
   Track Recorder.
2. Select
   Start.
