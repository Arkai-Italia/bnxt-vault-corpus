# Tread - SXS Edition Owner's Manual - Checking Road Conditions

# Checking Road Conditions

Before you can use this feature, your device must be connected to a supported phone running the
Tread® app ([Pairing with Your Smartphone](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-9983ED7E-888E-4AD6-A449-CBD17B7838C5.html)).

You can view weather-related road conditions for the roads in your area, on your route, or in another city.

1. Select
    > Navigation > Weather.
2. If necessary, select a city.
3. Select
    > Road Conditions.
