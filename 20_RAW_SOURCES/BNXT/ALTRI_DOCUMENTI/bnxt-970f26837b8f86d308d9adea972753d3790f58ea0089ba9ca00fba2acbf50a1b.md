# My battery does not stay charged for very long

* Decrease the screen brightness ([Display Settings](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-16B1D74D-857B-4FFB-8DE2-A0960FE0D090/EN-US/GUID-4C97EE8C-2FE0-407B-A46C-58A06752E93C.html)).
* Shorten the display timeout ([Display Settings](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-16B1D74D-857B-4FFB-8DE2-A0960FE0D090/EN-US/GUID-4C97EE8C-2FE0-407B-A46C-58A06752E93C.html)).
* Decrease the volume ([Adjusting the Volume](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-16B1D74D-857B-4FFB-8DE2-A0960FE0D090/EN-US/GUID-B3D4BA1D-BCD1-41E2-95BB-4B165E88F357.html)).

* Do not leave your device in direct sunlight.
