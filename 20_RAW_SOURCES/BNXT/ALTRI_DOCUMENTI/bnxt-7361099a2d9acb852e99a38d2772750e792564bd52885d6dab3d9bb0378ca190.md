# Tread - Overland Edition Tread XL - Overland Edition Owner's Manual - Receiving Traffic Data Using Your Smartphone

# Receiving Traffic Data Using Your Smartphone

Your device can receive subscription-free traffic data while connected to a smartphone running the
Tread® app.

1. Connect your device to the
   Tread app ([Pairing with Your Smartphone](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-157A2412-594F-45D8-A5EE-5A5327DA0914/EN-US/GUID-9983ED7E-888E-4AD6-A449-CBD17B7838C5.html)).
2. On your
   Tread - Overland Edition device, select
    > Traffic, and verify the
   Traffic option is selected.
