# **1. ENTRIES**

Entry fee for all the riders (Prestige, Junior, Youth) is 120 EUR (transponder pack included) paid to the promoter. All entries must be received before 7 days prior the start of the Event. The entry fees for late entries will be 150 EUR.

The deposit for the transponder is EUR 100 and is paid to the timekeeping company. The full amount of the deposit will be given back when the transponder is returned.

Entries must be made by an electronic entry form available on the site https://superenduro.org/for-riders/. All other information relating to the riders, team, sponsor and the brand of the machine shall be completed.

# **6. RIDERS ADMITTED**

The riders admitted will be established according to Art. 066.4.3 of FIM SuperEnduro World Championship & Cup Regulations.

The maximum number of riders accepted in each class is as follows:

- Prestige: 22 • Youth: 28

- Juniors: 24 • Europe: 28 (where applicable)

# **7. STARTING PROCEDURE**

The starting procedure will be established according to Art. 066.6.9 of FIM SuperEnduro World Championship & Cup Regulations.

# **8. PADDOCK**

Paddock is located inside the Arena, close to starting gates. Dedicated refueling area outside the Arena will be provided and indicated by organizer.

The use of an environmental mat is always mandatory, also in the case of electric generators or other generators. It is compulsory for electric bikes to use an electrical insulation mat. Failure to comply with this obligation is subject to a disciplinary penalty of 100 EUR. In the event of non-payment of the penalty or failure to use the mat after the above-mentioned penalty has been imposed, the rider will be excluded from the event

# **9. BIKE WARM-UP AREA**

This area will be designated near the paddock, outside. It is strictly forbidden to warm-up the bikes in any other location, especially at paddock inside building. When riding during warming-up the rider is obliged to wear a helmet. Riding on the green areas is forbidden.

# **10. THIRD PARTY LIABILITY INSURANCE**

The amount of the coverage for the Third Party Liability insurance must be of CHF 1’000’000 minimum. A copy of the policy must be sent to the FIM Administration no later than 20 days prior to the event.

# **11. SIGNALING ZONE**

The Signaling Zone is only available to team members actively taking part in the race.

# **12. RESULTS**

The classification will be established according to Art. 066.6.10 of FIM SuperEnduro World Championship & Cup Regulations.

# **13. PRIZES AND PARTICIPATION ALLOWANCES**

Payment of the prize money will be made according to Art. 066.12.2.2 of FIM SuperEnduro World Championship &

Cup Regulations.

Sport Up will pay the prize money by wire transfer after having received the necessary  invoices by the riders.

# **14. INTERPRETATION OF THE SUPPLEMENTARY REGULATIONS**

The interpretation of this regulation rests entirely with the Referee. In case of dispute, regarding the interpretation or if there is any difference between the two official texts, the English text will prevail.

## **<u>Helmet Safety and Modification Rules</u>**


Helmets are designed exclusively for protection and must not be used as a platform for attachments. Cameras and all other accessories or foreign objects are strictly forbidden from being attached to the rider’s helmet.

FIM Homologation (FRHPhe-02) Timeline

- Current (From 01/01/2024): FIM homologated helmets (with a valid FRHPhe-02 label) are strongly recommended.

- Mandatory (From 01/01/2026): FIM homologated helmets become mandatory. All other standards will be forbidden.

# **_<u>Exception for 2026 Championships Starting in 2025:</u>_**

FIM has exceptionally decided that for Championships starting in 2025 that span into the 2026 season, the mandatory requirement to wear an FIM Homologated Helmet (FRHPhe-02) will be waived, and it will remain only strongly recommended.
