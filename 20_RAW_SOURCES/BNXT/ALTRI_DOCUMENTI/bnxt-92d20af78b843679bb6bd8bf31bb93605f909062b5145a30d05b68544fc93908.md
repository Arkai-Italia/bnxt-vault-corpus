# Tread Owner's Manual - Receiving Traffic Data Using Your Smartphone

# Receiving Traffic Data Using Your Smartphone

Your device can receive subscription-free traffic data while connected to a smartphone running the
Tread™ app.

1. Connect your device to the
   Tread app ([Pairing Your Phone and Headset](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-16B1D74D-857B-4FFB-8DE2-A0960FE0D090/EN-US/GUID-883143FA-E7E4-4F4A-906A-4FF7FEEBE594.html)).
2. On your
   Tread device, select
    > Traffic, and verify the
   Traffic option is selected.
