## Pagina 1

T5.1 TECHNICAL PASSPORT PROCESS:
REMINDER OF THE KEY POINTS

## Pagina 2

1. SCOPE




The teams who intend to enter T5.1 vehicles that have no valid FIA technical passport
must follow the process to get such a document by the next FIA event the trucks will be
entered.



                                                                                                      Specific Technical Passport
                                                                                              issued directly by the FIA

                                                                                                       Vehicles requiring a proper FIA
                                                                                               Passport based on the new Template

                                                                                                 With valid FIA Technical
                                                                                                            Passport issued before 2022

                                                                      No FIA Technical
                                                                                             Passport required

## Pagina 3

1. SCOPE



It concerns the following trucks entered as T5.1:

❖Any new truck (truck never entered as T5.1 in any FIA event so far):
➢     Trucks with a valid Cabin Safety Cage ASN Certification
➢     Trucks with a Cabin Safety Cage ASN Homologation
Note that there is no limit of validity for a Safety Cage ASN Certification


❖Any truck as T5.1 in a FIA event since 2023 whatever it is fitted with a cabin safety
  cage with an ASN Certification or an ASN Homologation


     The Cabin Safety Cage of those trucks have already
     been identified with a FIA Passport Sticker.

## Pagina 4

2. PROCESS


1. The team/ competitor/ owner of the truck needs first to
  inform his ASN that he wants to apply for a new FIA T5.1
  Technical Passport

2. The technical department of the ASN will then supply him
   with the FIA Technical Passport template to be completed

3. Once completed, the team will submit it to his ASN,
  together with a copy of the safety cage ASN homologation
   certificate (or Declaration of Conformity) of the cabin fitted
   in the truck and any additional info the ASN may required



                       Declaration        Inspection           FIA TP

## Pagina 5

2. PROCESS


4.  After having reviewed all the documents submitted and the content of
    the template filled up by the truck team, the ASN will have to perform
   a technical inspection of the truck to check:
➢The compliance of the truck with the technical regulations
➢the content of the draft T5.1 technical passport is fully in line with what
   the truck actually is

5.  Once all OK, the ASN will submit the FIA T5.1 Passport request to the
   FIA Technical Department, including the pre-filled passport, that is to
   say the passport template fully completed together with the cabin
    safety cage certificate.

Reminder of the deadline for Dakar 2026: 12th of November 2025


                         Declaration        Inspection           FIA TP

## Pagina 6

2. PROCESS



6. The pre-filled passport will be then checked by the FIA.

7.   If checked OK, it will be printed on FIA blue paper.

8.  At the first FIA event the truck will be entered as T5.1, during
    the pre-event scrutineering, the truck (actually the cabin safety
   cage) will be identified (if not already marked within the 3 past
    years) by means of FIA passport stickers and, if all checked
   OK, the passport will be validated and provided to the team
    representative.





                         Declaration        Inspection           FIA TP

## Pagina 7

3. PASSPORT FEE



A. A fee will be invoiced by the FIA to the ASN – as per the
   FIA Sporting Fee list published on the FIA website.

B. The team/ competitor will be then charged directly by his
   ASN.





                         Declaration        Inspection           FIA TP
