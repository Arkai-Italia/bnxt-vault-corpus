# Tread - SXS Edition Owner's Manual - Tracking Your Dogs

# Tracking Your Dogs

1. Select
    > Adventure > Dog Tracker.

   The dogs connected to your handheld dog device appear on the screen.
2. Select a dog.

   The dog's location and track log appear on the map.
3. Select
   Go! to navigate to your dog.
