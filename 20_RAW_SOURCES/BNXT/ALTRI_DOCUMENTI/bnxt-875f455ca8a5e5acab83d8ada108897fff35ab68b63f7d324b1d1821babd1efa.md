# Tread Owner's Manual - Selecting a Roger Tone

# Selecting a Roger Tone

You can select the tone other devices play when you operate your radio.

1. Select
   Radio >  > Roger Tone.
2. Select a roger tone.

   The device plays the selected tone.
3. Select
   Save.
