# Showing or Hiding Notification Categories for Your Apple Device

If you are connected to an
Apple® device, you can filter the notifications shown on your
Tread® - SXS Edition device by showing or hiding categories.

1. Select
    > Tools > Smart Notifications > .
2. Select the check box next to each notification to show.
