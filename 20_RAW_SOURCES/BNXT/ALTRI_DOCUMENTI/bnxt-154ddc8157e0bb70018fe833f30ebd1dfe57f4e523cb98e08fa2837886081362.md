30 YEARS MYTH
OF RED BULL ERZBERGRODEO




Even after three centuries, the MYTH OF RED BULL ERZBERGRODEO
has not lost it‘s strong attraction. The internationally unique event
is regarded as the world‘s most renowned Extreme Enduro.
2026 will see the 30th edition of Red Bull Erzbergrodeo.

Each year, thousands of ambitious dirtbike athletes from all
over the world get on their way to make a picturesque town
in the heart of Austria become the pulsing epicentre of the
international motorcycle offroad sports community.

They trade thousands of miles of travel for four days
of adrenaline, sweat, party and - disappointement.
Because at the end of the day only a handful of
riders will conquer the superior „Iron Giant“.

TOUGHER THAN IRON is way more than just a phrase -
this motto perfectly describes the motivation of
thousands of competitors, tens of thousands of spectators
and up to 800 crew workers behind the event to
enthusiastically take on the challenges provided by
Red Bull Erzbergrodeo since everything started in 1995.
