# My battery does not stay charged for very long

* Decrease the screen brightness ([Display Settings](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-157A2412-594F-45D8-A5EE-5A5327DA0914/EN-US/GUID-7EEF8CDE-4C38-44EF-859C-D99ED6535E8C.html)).
* Shorten the display timeout ([Display Settings](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-157A2412-594F-45D8-A5EE-5A5327DA0914/EN-US/GUID-7EEF8CDE-4C38-44EF-859C-D99ED6535E8C.html)).
* Decrease the volume ([Adjusting Sound and Notification Volume](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-157A2412-594F-45D8-A5EE-5A5327DA0914/EN-US/GUID-A90E39DE-1BBE-43E3-940C-993CB62FEE02.html)).
* Turn off the wi-fi radio when it is not in use ([Connecting to a Wireless Network](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-157A2412-594F-45D8-A5EE-5A5327DA0914/EN-US/GUID-68D0B0AA-8293-4BB6-A338-22AB76722DE2.html)).
* Put the device in power saving mode when not in use ([Turning the Device On or Off](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-157A2412-594F-45D8-A5EE-5A5327DA0914/EN-US/GUID-BE66547E-AE97-45E0-891D-F8ADB2983BC5.html)).
* Keep your device away from extreme temperatures.
* Do not leave your device in direct sunlight.
