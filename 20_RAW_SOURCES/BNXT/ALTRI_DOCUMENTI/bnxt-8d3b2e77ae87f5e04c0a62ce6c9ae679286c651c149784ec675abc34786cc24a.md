# Tread Owner's Manual - Deleting a Paired Phone

# Deleting a Paired Phone

You can delete a paired phone to prevent it from automatically connecting to your device in the future.

1. Select
    > Wireless Networks.
2. Select the phone, and select
   Forget Device.
