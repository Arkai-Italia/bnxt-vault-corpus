# Tread Owner's Manual - Resetting the Fuel Tank Mileage

# Resetting the Fuel Tank Mileage

When you refill your fuel tank, you should reset the fuel tank mileage on your device to more accurately track your remaining fuel.

Select
 > Fuel Settings > Reset Fuel Tank.
