# GBM air conditioning blower motor

#### Efficient air conditioning for passenger and commercial vehicle interiors

With the Global Blower Motor (GBM), Bosch is offering a DC motor platform for heating and air conditioning systems that is suited for use all over the world. The vehicle air conditioning product portfolio includes both the motor and the blower module. Optionally, the blower module can be equipped with either a linear or a PWM controller, and the standardized modular components can be combined as needed for the application-specific requirements. This way, the system provides a flexible solution to meet customer requirements. The GBM is also suitable for use in commercial vehicles and off-highway applications with 24 V electrical system voltage.

## Compact blower module with scalable motor power

### Air conditioning blower motor

The GBM air conditioning blower motor is a 2-pole DC motor with ferrite magnets. The motor is offered in our product portfolio in three different lengths (S, M and L), all four of them featuring identical outer diameters. The position and length of the output shaft and the electrical connection can be customized to meet the application-specific requirements.

### Air conditioning blower module

The air conditioning blower module comprises the GBM air conditioning blower motor, the module housing, cover, fan wheel and optionally a blower controller. The standard radial fan wheel as well as the mechanical and electrical interface can be adapted flexibly to the HVAC system.
