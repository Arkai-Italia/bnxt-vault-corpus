# Red Bull Romaniacs | Prologue: Kabakchiev wins Gold as Mario Roman makes history in Adventure Ultimate

Published on July 27th, 2026 by Nicki Martinez

**Teodor Kabakchiev takes an incredibly tight Gold class victory in the opening Red Bull Romaniacs 2026 Prologue ahead of Mitch Brightmore and Mani Lettenbichler, while Mario Roman scores his first-ever win in Sibiu after beating Pol Tarres in Adventure Ultimate.**

The 23rd edition of the Red Bull Romaniacs, carrying the “UNSTOPPABLE” title this year, is officially underway.

As tradition dictates, the action kicked off with the urban Prologue right in the centre of Sibiu, on an artificial course just six metres wide designed by Andy Fazekas to accommodate all nine Enduro, Adventure and eMoto classes.

It was a short but intense opening day, with obstacles coming thick and fast and thousands of fans packed around the course, before the real business begins with four Off-Road days from Wednesday.

**Gold: Kabakchiev takes the urban Prologue win**

Mani Lettenbichler came out firing from the very first metre of the Gold final and quickly took control at the front, managing much of the race with his trademark efficiency.

Teodor Kabakchiev, however, never let him get away and stayed right on the German’s wheel, waiting for his chance.

That opportunity came after Lettenbichler chose a less favourable line. Kabakchiev closed the gap and, just before receiving the last-lap flag, made his move to take the lead.

Behind them, Mitch Brightmore was steadily working his way forward and also found a way past Lettenbichler in the closing stages.

Kabakchiev crossed the line first in 11m23.657s, just 0.496 seconds ahead of Brightmore, with Lettenbichler finishing third, exactly two seconds behind the winner.

**Adventure Ultimate: first Romaniacs win for Mario Roman**

The Adventure Ultimate class provided another major talking point as the big multi-cylinder machines took on Sibiu’s man-made obstacles.

Pol Tarres and Kevin Gallas set the early pace, with Mario Roman close behind. Tarres made several mistakes before a crash finally opened the door for the Spaniard.

Roman made the most of it, using his smooth and consistent riding to move into the lead and stay there all the way to the chequered flag.

After 10 years racing in Gold and five Red Bull Romaniacs podiums, Roman finally claimed his first victory at the event – this time aboard a CFMoto 450 Adventure bike.

Tarres finished second, with Dieter Rudolf completing the podium.

**Silver: Jiawei Sun dominates, Sandra Gomez fifth**

Jiawei Sun was the standout rider in the Silver class. The Chinese rider controlled the race from start to finish, setting a pace comfortably faster than his rivals while keeping things clean through the obstacles.

Spain’s Sandra Gomez also put together a strong ride to finish fifth.

Another notable name on the Silver start list was 2021 Moto2 World Champion and current WorldSBK rider Remy Gardner, who completed the Prologue in 16th position.

**Bronze: family battle between the Zavalas**

The Bronze final delivered one of the stories of the day with father and son Jesus and Stanislas Zavala battling for victory.

Jesus led early on before crashing on one of the wet obstacles, allowing Stanislas to move into first place.

Experience eventually won out, though, as Jesus recovered the lead to take victory.

The two Zavalas were also the only riders to complete six laps, while Sébastien Garreau rounded out the podium on five.

**eMoto: Rehrl fastest**

Six riders lined up in the eMoto class, posting competitive lap times even when compared with the Silver and Adventure Ultimate categories.

Valentin Rehrl claimed victory ahead of Karl Jörg Mittendorf and Martin Dosch.

The first of four Off-Road days gets underway on Wednesday, July 29, with stages of around 150 kilometres taking the race out of Sibiu and into the Romanian mountains.

That’s where the fight for the 2026 Red Bull Romaniacs really begins.
