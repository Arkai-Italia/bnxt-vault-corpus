# 2027 Beta RR Race line-up: Tech tweaks and motor revisions across the eight-bike range

Published on September 2nd, 2026 by Nye Davis

-2.jpg)

#### **Beta has pulled the covers off its 2027 RR Race enduro range, festooning its sportiest model range with a raft of engine, electronics, suspension and chassis updates, across the full eight-bike selection.**

As with the mid-range X-Pro range we saw earlier this year, Beta has not taken to reinventing the formula here but has instead weaved in a curated selection of tweaks and improvements. As before, the MY27 range continues to span four-strokes – the RR Race 350, 390, 430 and 480, as well as an equal number of smoky counterparts - 125, 200, 250 and 300.

**Launch control and quick shift for the four-strokes**

Electrical wizardry is now bang up-to-date with the best of ’em, with the introduction of five-level launch control and up-only quick shifting across the four-stroke models - technologies first rolled out on Beta’s new X-Pro models.

Both systems join Beta’s existing two-level traction control and dry- and wet-condition engine maps. All of which is accessed and toggled via redesigned switchgear nestled inside the handlebar protection.

Charging systems on these models has also been upgraded with a new voltage regulator, said to provide a higher and more stable voltage to the battery. Beta reckons the change improves reliability across the electrical system while increasing battery charging capacity.

Alongside the electronic tweaks the biggest pair in the four-stroke catalogue – the 430 and 480 – both receive newly finished titanium intake valves. A new cam chain tensioner, meanwhile, is lifted directly from Beta’s RX 450f motocross bike. It swaps the previous hybrid mechanical-hydraulic arrangement for a purely mechanical system with a newly calibrated spring.

### **Two-stroke changes**

The two-strokes get less to shout about in terms of outright add-ons (in fact there are no new features as such for 2027). Instead Beta’s engineers have invested their time on motor refinement.

-2.jpg)The RR Race 125 receives an updated piston, aimed at “improving smoothness of the overall power delivery,” so Beta says, though no more specifics than that are given.

Both the 250 and 300 2T engines gain new main bearings said to reduce internal friction, but the lion’s share of the revisions are reserved for the 300 alone. Beta has revised the cylinder head geometry and exhaust valve timing with the specific aim of making the engine’s response at low and medium revs more progressive.

### **Add-ons across the range**

Beyond the individual engine updates, all eight RR Race models receive a number of shared tweaks.

The chain guide gets a reinforced structure designed to better withstand the usual abuse – think mud, rocks and impacts - while reducing the chances of the chain derailing.

-2.jpg)The grips are now made from a softer compound, the output sprocket guard has been redesigned with wider coverage and increased strength, while the front end gets a new mask incorporating a latest-generation LED headlight, again debuted earlier this year on the X-Pro family.

Suspension settings have been revised across the bunch, too, meaning the KYB sealed-cartridge front fork and C46 RCU rear shock receive updated internal valve settings developed by Beta’s engineers and race team.

The dashboard is another obvious addition. A larger Trailtech instrument replaces the previous unit, retaining broadly the same information but offering improved readability and greater durability. It’s paired with a new wheel-speed pick-up lead.

-2.jpg)The 2027 RR Race models are due to arrive in dealers worldwide from October, though global pricing is yet to be confirmed.
