BEFOREand after

**Before**Registration in a Google form, payment by transfer to whoever's convenient, lists merged by hand the night before the start.

**After**Registration in the app: classes, entry fees, spot limits, deadlines, and discounted entries for women and kids — availability is calculated automatically.

**Before**Medical certificates and insurance arrive as photos in DMs, and by morning nobody remembers who has already been checked.

**After**Participants upload documents in the app, and the moderator sees a queue with filters and statuses. Checked means checked.

**Before**Results mean two people with stopwatches and Excel until 3 a.m. One mistake — and there's a scandal at the awards ceremony.

**After**Marshals log the start, checkpoints, and finish from their phones. Splits, classes, and the overall standings are calculated automatically — live results are ready right at the finish.

TOOLKITin the app

### A race in one evening

One screen: classes, entry fees, discounts, required documents, the rulebook as a file, official chats. The listing goes straight into the season calendar.

### Start list

Reorder classes by drag and drop, choose start formats, and every participant has the start list on their phone — no A4 printouts.

### Qualifying and timing

Qualifying runs with a photo barrier, checkpoint logging, splits, and full results by class.

### Marshals connected

Every marshal gets a workstation on their phone: checkpoint assignment, START → CP → FINISH logging, and everything flows into the results instantly.

### Live course map

Participants on the map in real time, plus GPS course-compliance monitoring: if someone cuts the course, you see it.

### HQ SOS panel

An alert with coordinates reaches race staff immediately — for yourself or by bib number. Safety is built in, not bolted on.

ROUTEhow it works

#### Get the role

Apply for the organizer role right from the app. We review and confirm it.

#### Build the race

Classes, entry fees, documents, the rulebook — one builder screen instead of a week of back-and-forth.

#### Take entries

Registrations, discounts, and document checks run on their own. You see a summary, not chaos.

#### Run race day

Start list, marshals, qualifying, the live map, and the SOS panel — all on your team's phones.

#### Publish the results

Results by class and overall standings are ready at the finish. Participants see them in the app right away.

What does it cost the organizer?

Zero. Creating and running races is free — no commission on the event. We make money on rider subscriptions, so more races from you means more value for us.

My race is already in the calendar — how?

We maintain the season calendar (195+ races) from open sources so riders never miss anything. Write to us or apply as an organizer — we'll verify your rights to the listing and hand you control of it.

Do I need a computer?

No. The whole cycle runs from a phone. For big events there's a web dashboard on this site: the same data on a wide screen.

How fast is the role confirmed?

The application goes to the platform administrator from the app. Usually within a day or two; if your race is already announced, we'll speed it up.
