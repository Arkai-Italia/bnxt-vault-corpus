# XEF 450

XEF 450

The XEF450 represents the ultimate expression of enduro according to Fantic: a completely new motorcycle designed to deliver top-level performance on any terrain. The new engine, new frame and Euro 5+ homologation define a modern, advanced platform capable of combining the strength of the 450 cc single-cylinder with sophisticated electronics that make power always manageable, smooth and perfectly controllable.

A motorcycle created to overcome any obstacle with ease, providing the rider with stability, traction and confidence even in the most demanding situations.

**Advanced Electronics, Tailored Power**

The **electronic heart** of the XEF 450 is **entrusted to the Athena-GET ECU**, developed in collaboration with the Fantic racing department. **Two engine maps (Hard/Soft),** fully customizable via the WiGET app, allow the power delivery to be adapted to any terrain or riding style. Selection is made directly from **the new multifunction handlebar switch, which also manages traction control,** giving the rider immediate and intuitive command in any situation.

**Advanced Components for Maximum Performance**

Among the most significant innovations on the XEF 450 **is the new hydraulic clutch**, designed to ensure constant and precise modulation even in the most demanding conditions. **The new ARROW exhaust system also debuts**, optimised to increase mid-range thrust and easily derestricted in racing configuration to exploit the full power of the 450 cc engine.

Completing the package is the new-generation integrated start/stop control, redesigned with improved ergonomics for more immediate and intuitive operation.

**Next-Generation Frame, Instant Feeling**

The chassis of the XEF 450 **evolves with a new aluminum perimeter frame,** updated in terms of rigidity to enhance stability and precision in every riding phase. **Completing the package are the fully adjustable Kayaba SSS suspensions,** an absolute benchmark for sensitivity and support.

The result is an immediate feeling: total control, predictable response and unwavering confidence everywhere — from the toughest timed special of a World Championship to weekend mountain trails.

**Design: Renewed Identity, Unmistakable DNA**

The aesthetics of the XEF 450 follow the design language of the XXF range, introducing new graphics, a redesigned headlight mask and a completely new airbox for a modern, aggressive and unmistakably racing look.

**The new Fantic liveries** recall the brand’s golden age, reinterpreting the iconic red, white and blue in a contemporary style. The chrome base adds a distinctive, visually striking touch, enhancing the bike’s lines and making it instantly recognisable on any terrain.
