This is a complete kit for outfitting an enduro bike with a full handlebar mounted navigation system.  If you're starting from scratch and want a simple, low-profile installation without the need for a fairing then this is for you.

Includes:

* TowerOne Device with GPS antenna and Beeper.
* Mounting hardware including isolators, baseplate, crossbar clamps, and handlebar clamp crossbar kit.
* 5 button handlebar switch.
* Power switch with single output and power lead.
