# 2027 KTM 65 SX - STEP ON UP! | KTM United States

## 01. Before The Gate

### Adjustablity

The KTM 65 SX is designed to grow with young racers thanks to multiple adjustability options. Different adjustment possibilities were built into the KTM 65 SX construction using scientific statistical data and ergonomic mannequins. Finding the perfect fit for your junior ripper is easy thanks to height-adjustable suspension, adjustable handlebar positioning, subframe, and bodywork. This allows the seat height to be adjusted from a low setting of 715 mm up to 760 mm, while the KTM 65 SX comes standard from the factory with a seat height of 730 mm.

### Ignition

A digital ignition from Hidria finds its way onto the KTM 65 SX, with improved processing power and ignition curve, making for a cleaner burn and smoother power delivery. The throttle assembly benefits from a stabilization cap that extends the lifetime of the cable by reducing the risk of kinking and damage.

### Kickstarter

A lighter, more ergonomic kickstart lever makes firing the KTM 65 SX a cinch for little legs. Not only has the kickstart lever been designed to match the frame and engine design perfectly. It also ensures easy access for quick restarts while being perfectly packaged to be out of the way when tearing up the berms.

### Carburetor

A proven Mikuni TM 24 carburetor takes a prime position in producing the goods in the KTM 65 SX, with settings that work perfectly in harmony with the engine, exhaust, and air intake design.
