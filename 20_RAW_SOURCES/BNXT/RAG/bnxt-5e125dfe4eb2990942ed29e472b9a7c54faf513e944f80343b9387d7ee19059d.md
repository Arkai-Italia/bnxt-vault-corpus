# Stark reveals limited-run VARG Factory Editions

Published on July 21st, 2026 by Nye Davis

#### **Stark Future has lifted the covers off new Factory Edition versions of its VARG MX and VARG EX, with just 500 bikes set to be built worldwide – 200 motocross models and 300 enduro machines.**

While both bikes receive the same fundamental upgrades, it's the EX model that has naturally grabbed our attention.

The headline change is a full KYB Factory A-Kit suspension package – an addition Stark claims is the first time a complete factory-spec KYB setup has been fitted to a production motorcycle.

The package includes 48mm closed-cartridge coil-spring forks with Kashima-coated outer tubes, DLC-coated internals and revised valving, paired with a triple-adjustable KYB rear shock featuring a Kashima-coated body, DLC-coated piston rod and X-Trig preload adjuster.

The EX receives type-specific suspension settings with 300mm of fork travel, compared to 310mm on the MX, while both bikes retain 303mm of rear wheel travel.

Beyond the posh suspension additions, the Factory Editions gain 3D-printed titanium footpegs and a titanium bolt kit, trimming just over 1kg in total, alongside a high-grip seat, upgraded Bio PA6 radiator fan holder, and an exclusive CNC-machined aluminium triple clamps numbered one through to 500.

The EX also comes equipped with Dunlop Geomax EN91 tyres, front and rear disc guards, and flag-style handguards.

Power comes from the same 79bhp electric powertrain found in the standard VARG EX, including the same 7.2kWh battery. The recently announced Dynamic Traction Control, available to existing VARG Alpha owners (but not those with the 59bhp version) also features here.

Pricing starts at £13,990 for the VARG MX Factory Edition, while the road-legal VARG EX Factory Edition is priced at £14,990.

Stark Future Racing Director and former MX world champion Sébastien Tortelli said:

*"Racing and developing championship-winning motorcycles has been my life's work,"* said Tortelli.

*"Since joining Stark, we have continued to improve the VARG through testing and competition at the highest level. Every race has taught us something, and everything we have learned has gone back into the motorcycle.*

*"The VARG already has the performance. With the Factory Editions, the focus was to give riders more grip, more precision, and more confidence to unlock more of that performance. These are built exactly how I would build my own."*
