# Tread Owner's Manual - Smart Notifications

# Smart Notifications

While your device is connected to the
Tread™ app, you can view notifications from your smartphone on your
Tread device, such as text messages, incoming calls, and calendar appointments.

NOTE:
You may need to wait a few minutes to receive notifications on your navigation device after it connects to the
Tread app.  appears colored in the
Bluetooth® settings when smart notifications are connected and active ([Bluetooth Feature Status Icons](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-16B1D74D-857B-4FFB-8DE2-A0960FE0D090/EN-US/GUID-AB281DFD-A5E5-4ED9-9C86-886153F5CC1E.html)).
