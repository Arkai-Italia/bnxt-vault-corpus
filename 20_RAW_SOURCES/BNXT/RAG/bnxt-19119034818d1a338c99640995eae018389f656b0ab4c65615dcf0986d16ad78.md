# AMA Saddened by Passing of Amateur Flat-Track Racer Alyssa Kruppenbacker

* by [Joy Burgess](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/jburgess/)
* July 6, 2026
* 0 Comments
* 167684 Views

PICKERINGTON, Ohio (July 6, 2026) — The American Motorcyclist Association is saddened and heartbroken to share the news of the passing of AMA member and amateur flat-track racer Alyssa Kruppenbacker from Crescent City, Fla. She was 14 years old.

Alyssa passed away following an on-track incident during the Mile program of the AMA Flat Track Grand Championship in Du Quoin, Ill.

We extend our deepest condolences to the Kruppenbacker family and the entire flat-track community.
