# Tread Owner's Manual - Viewing Your Collections on the Map

# Viewing Your Collections on the Map

Before you can view the Collections map layer, you must pair your device with the
Tread™ app and sync your device with your
Garmin Explore™ account.

You can view your
Garmin Explore collections directly on the map. You must enable the collections map layer to use this feature.

NOTE:
If you do not assign a collection category to your
Garmin Explore data, it appears as an unorganized collection.

1. Select
   View Map >  > Collections.
2. Select one or more collection categories from the list.
3. Select
   Save.

   Your collections appear as icons on the map.
