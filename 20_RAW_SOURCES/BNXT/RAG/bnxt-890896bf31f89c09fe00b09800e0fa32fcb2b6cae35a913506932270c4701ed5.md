# Tread - SXS Edition Owner's Manual - BirdsEye Direct Satellite Imagery

# BirdsEye Direct Satellite Imagery

BirdsEye direct satellite imagery provides downloadable, high-resolution map imagery, including detailed satellite imagery and raster-based topographical maps. You can download
BirdsEye direct satellite imagery directly to your device while it is connected to a
Wi‑Fi® network.

## Downloading BirdsEye Direct Satellite Imagery

Before you can download
BirdsEye direct satellite imagery directly to your device, you must connect to a wireless network ([Connecting to a Wireless Network](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-68D0B0AA-8293-4BB6-A338-22AB76722DE2.html)).

If you have an active Outdoor Maps+ subscription, you must download satellite imagery
using the map manager ([Downloading Outdoor Maps+ Content](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-967C4E16-DAAA-48B9-9480-109AE1B69C8A.html#GUID-EC61168E-703F-4FC6-973C-822C9B13CBD3)).
