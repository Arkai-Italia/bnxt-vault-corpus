## [How does it work](https://www.motoklik.com/installation-instructions/)

1. Pick out your Motoklik system from the [“Buy Now”](https://www.motoklik.com/product/motoklik-tw1-0-system/) page.
2. When your Motoklik arrives, you can follow the [installation instructions](https://www.motoklik.com/installation-instructions/) to easily fit the system to your bike.
3. Wait for the light to start flashing green in the handlebar controller, press the button and hit the track. Your data is being recorded!
4. At the end of the session, [connect to your Motoklik, and download the data](https://www.motoklik.com/app-use-instructions/#target-section-HowtousetheApp) to analyse lap-times, section times and your suspension.
5. [AiSetup automatically analyses your data](https://www.motoklik.com/app-use-instructions/#target-section-AiSetup) to recommend how much the clickers should be adjusted for the current track conditions, and your riding style!
