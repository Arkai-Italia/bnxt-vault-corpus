## 01. ENGINE & PERFORMANCE

LIGHT FEEL. BIG DRIVE.

### 4-Stroke Power

At its core, the KTM 350 EXC-F delivers a unique combination of high-revving performance and strong, usable torque. Its lightweight and compact design improves mass centralization, providing excellent stability, agile handling, and versatility across technical and fast-paced enduro terrain.

RACE HARD. WEAR LESS

The KTM EXC drivetrain is engineered for durability and efficient power transfer under extreme conditions.
