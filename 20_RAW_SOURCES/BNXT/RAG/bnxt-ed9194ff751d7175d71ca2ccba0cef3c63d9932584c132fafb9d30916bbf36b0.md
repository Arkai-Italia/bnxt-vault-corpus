# EC motors for electric cooling fans

#### Brushless electric cooling fan drives for continuous speed control

The brushless electric cooling fan drives ECM and FED can be used both in combustion engine thermal circuits and in electric vehicles. The electronic motor commutation allows for continuous speed control. As compared to conventional brush motors, the new drives provide advantages in terms of efficiency, packaging, electromagnetic compatibility, temperature resistance and robustness with regard to environmental influences.

## Efficient and compact drive for electric cooling

### Electric cooling modules

Customer-specific electric cooling modules combine outstanding efficiency at low noise levels while making optimum use of the available installation space. The increased efficiency provides important leverage to reduce fuel consumption and CO2 emissions. The European Energy Efficiency Directive refers to the brushless EC technology as the "best available technology".

### Electric cooling drive

In addition to the motor functions and high efficiency, the ECM and FED drives offer further protective functions. If the fan is blocked or the permissible voltage/temperature range is exceeded, or in case of overcurrent or internal faults, the motor is shut off.
