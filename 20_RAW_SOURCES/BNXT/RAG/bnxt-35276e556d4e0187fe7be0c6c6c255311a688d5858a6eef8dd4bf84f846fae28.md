# Ducati North America to Support AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony as Livestream Sponsor

* by [AMA Tech](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/amatechama-cycle-org/)
* August 13, 2026
* 0 Comments
* 131458 Views

PICKERINGTON, Ohio (Aug. 13, 2026) — Ducati North America will support the AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony presented by Yamaha Motor Company — which will honor seven outstanding motorcyclists on Sept. 10 — as the Official Livestream Sponsor of the event.

“Ducati North America’s support as the event’s livestream sponsor reflects a shared commitment to celebrating the sport’s most influential figures,” said AMA Business Development Manager Michael Kula. “This partnership allows us to extend the reach of the Induction Ceremony to riders and fans across the country, and we look forward to delivering a memorable evening alongside Ducati North America.”

Celebrating 100 years of operations in 2026, the Italian-based Ducati is a globally recognized brand, with 10 subsidiaries spanning across the globe. For more information about Ducati North America, visit [*www.ducati.com*](https://www.ducati.com/us/en/home)*.*

“The AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony celebrates the individuals who have defined motorcycling’s legacy, and Ducati North America is proud to stand behind that mission,” said Jason Chinnock, CEO of Ducati North America. “With Ducati’s own history deeply connected to AMA and championship racing, supporting the event’s livestream is a meaningful way to help share this celebration with enthusiasts around the world.”

The AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame will welcome Gary Sellers, Jake Johnson, Damon Bradshaw, Russell Bobbitt, Eraldo Ferracci and Dale Walker as the Class of 2026, while Broc Glover will also earn the special recognition as an AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Legend during the evening event.

Tickets to the AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony are on sale now. For more details, and to purchase tickets, visit [*MotorcycleMuseum.org/events-experiences/induction-ceremony/*](https://motorcyclemuseum.org/events-experiences/induction-ceremony/).
