### One Yeti to Rule them All

For the first time ever, there will be the ultimate show down to conclude the 2026 Ice Breaker Challenge.

The scores have been tallied up, and the top riders of the northern routes will go head to head with the top riders of the southern routes to determine who will be the ‘SUPREME YETI’!

The course will provide the ultimate challenge, with lots of tricky navigation thrown in there to test your skills.
