## MAIN FEATURES

ENGINE

#### ENGINE HEAD, EXHAUST VALVE AND MAIN BEARINGS

The 300cc 2-stroke engine has taken a decisive leap forward in terms of performance. Beta’s engineers have revised the engine head geometry and exhaust valve timing with the aim of making the engine’s response even more progressive and controllable at low and medium revs, maximising the usability of the torque. Added to this are the new main bearings, which reduce internal friction, delivering a lively, full-bodied power delivery that responds instantly to the throttle control.

Chassis

#### “NO OIL INJECTION” RACING SETUP AND QUICK-RELEASE WHEEL SPINDLE

In keeping with the tradition of the specialist RR RACE range, the 300cc model also dispenses with the automatic oil injection assy in favour of a traditional fuel mixture system. This design choice keeps the bike’s overall weight to a minimum, ensuring maximum responsiveness. The race-ready specification includes a red anodised quick-release front wheel spindle, essential for speeding up tyre changes during the most intense stages of the race.

suspension

#### KYB SUSPENSION WITH NEW MY27 RACING SET-UPS

The top-of-the-range suspension system features a 48 mm KYB sealed-cartridge front fork and a KYB C46 RCU monoshock. For the Model Year 2027, the suspension benefits from new hydraulic settings developed directly in the field by the official Beta teams, ensuring superior riding feel, maximum traction on rough terrain and absolute stability during hard braking at the limit.

#### NEW LED HEADLIGHT AND HEADLIGHT MASK

A redesigned headlight mask incorporating a powerful, latest-generation LED headlight, delivering a superior light beam and lower power consumption.

#### INSTRUMENTATION TRAILTECH AND REED SENSOR

New Trailtech digital dashboard – larger, more robust and easier to read – paired with a high-precision wheel reed sensor.

#### REINFORCED CHAIN GUIDE AND PINION GUARD

High-impact-resistant chain guide for use in channels, and a new, enlarged pinion guard for maximum guarding and compliance with regulations

#### SOFT HANDLEBAR GRIPS

New handlebar grips made from a soft, anti-vibration compound to reduce strain on the forearms.

#### ERGAL FOOTPEGS AND ZF SPROCKETS CRANKSET

Ergal racing footpegs with interchangeable steel pins for maximum grip, and a red anodised bi-material sprocket with steel teeth.

#### RACING SEAT AND BI-COMPONENT HANDGUARDS

High-support seat with non-slip covering and a document pocket, complemented by high-strength bi-component handguards.

#### ANODISED DETAILS AND FACTORY GRAPHICS

Black gear shift levers, red anodised oil caps, a red Ergal chain tensioner and official racing graphics.

## POWER DELIVERY, TORQUE AND ABSOLUTE CONTROL

The RR RACE 2T 300 MY27 strikes the perfect balance between the raw power of the largest engine capacity and surgical control over the driving torque. Thanks to refinements to the engine head and exhaust valve, combined with a racing chassis set-up featuring KYB suspension, the bike offers a top-of-the-range engine and chassis package.
