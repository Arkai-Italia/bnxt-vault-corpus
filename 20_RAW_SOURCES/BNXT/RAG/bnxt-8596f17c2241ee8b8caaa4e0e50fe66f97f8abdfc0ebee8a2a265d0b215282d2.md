# Tread - SXS Edition Owner's Manual - Audio Zone Settings

# Audio Zone Settings

Select
 > Stereo Settings > Audio Zones, and if necessary, select a zone.

Zone Name
:   Sets the name of the zone.

Loud
:   Boosts the high and low frequencies.

Subwoofer Filter
:   Sets the low-pass frequency of the subwoofer.

Bass
:   Sets the relative volume of low-frequency audio.

Middle
:   Sets the relative volume of middle-frequency audio.

Treble
:   Sets the relative volume of high-frequency audio.
