# Tread - Overland Edition Tread XL - Overland Edition Owner's Manual - Wireless Remote Overview

# Wireless Remote Overview

WARNING

See the
Important Safety and Product Information guide in the product box for product warnings and other important information.

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Back button | Radio sources: Press to tune to the previous station.  AUX sources: Press to decrease gain.  Other sources: Press to skip to the beginning of the track or to the previous track. |
| Forward button | Radio sources: Press to tune to the next station.  AUX sources: Press to increase gain.  Other sources: Press to skip to the next track. |
| Minus button | Press to decrease the volume.  Hold to quickly decrease the volume. |
| Plus button | Press to increase the volume.  Hold to quickly increase the volume. |
| Source button | Press to cycle through the available sources.  Hold to pair with a stereo ([Connecting the Wireless Remote to your Tread Audio Box](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-157A2412-594F-45D8-A5EE-5A5327DA0914/EN-US/GUID-1E704B6F-2E08-41D0-9BAB-76251A70A1DD.html)). |
| Play and pause button | Radio and AUX sources: Press to mute or un-mute.  Other sources: Press to pause or resume. |
