# Pitbike Motoscrambles

###### The most fun on two wheels!

Pitbike motoscrambles combines pitbike motocross with a shorter trail loop, sized just right for 110cc machines. Starts are from the motocross track’s starting gate, and races last 30 minutes (timed off the leader) plus one lap. (Youth 4-8 class may be shortened.) Gate pick order is based on sign up order. Second-row starts will be used if necessary.

Required: long sleeves, pants, helmets, boots, chest protectors and eye protection.

## Classes and Tentative Race Order

##### **NOTE:** Except for Youth classes, all riders must be 13 or older on date of event.

**Trailbike**
Vertical four-stroke minibikes, such as TTR 125, CRF 125F, XR/CRF 100, DRZ 125, KLX 140.
**No 110cc or 12-inch wheels.**

**Women**
Stock 110cc and Stock Limited 110cc bikes permitted.  **No 12-inch Open or Trailbike.**

**Stock 110cc**
Horizontal 109-112cc four-strokes. Only modifications for comfort, durability and safety permitted.  **Stock exhaust, Swingarms and intake required.**

**Youth 9-12**
Stock 110cc and Stock Limited 110cc bikes permitted.  **No 12-inch Open or Trailbike.**

**12-inch Open**Horizontal four-strokes, with all Stock and Stock Limited modifications allowed as well as aftermarket engines and/or bores up to 212cc. MX swingarms and inverted forks allowed.

**Open Electric**Riders must be 13 years or older. Front-wheel max 19 inches. Example models include: Surron, E-Ride, Talaria, Ventus One, etc.

**Vet (30+)**
Riders must be 30 years or older. Stock 110cc and Stock Limited 110cc bikes permitted.
**No 12-inch Open or Trailbike.**

**Youth 4-8**

A 50cc class for riders 4-8 years old. Eligible models include: PW50, JR50, XR/CRF 50, TTR50.  **May take place on separate or limited course.**

**Stock Limited 110cc**

Allows Stock 110cc modifications, as well as aftermarket exhausts and intakes, aftermarket camshafts, porting, A-style swingarms, and manual clutch and disc brake conversions. **Stock bore and wheel sizes must be retained.**Permits “L” versions of base models (example: KLX110L).
