# Costwolds Christmas Breakfast Adventure Rally Raid – now Jan 25th

by [Gabriella Linford](https://rallymoto.co.uk/author/gabriella_l/) | Nov 29, 2025 | [UK Rally](https://rallymoto.co.uk/category/news/)

# COTSWOLDS CHRISTMAS BREAKFAST Adventure Rally Raid

### NOW running on 25th January 2026 as 28th December date was postponed.

## *TWO ROUTES!*

**Start Location: The Old Prison Cafe, Northleach, GL54 3JH**

**Time:** 09:30 for 10:00 start
Finish time around 15:00

**Price: £54.00
You must be a RallyMoto Member to enter.**

Entries Strictly Limited to 40

Open to all abilities and skill level, the Cotswolds Christmans Breakfast Adventure Rally Raid will take place on Sunday, December 28.

Shake off the post-Christmas festivities and join us for a fun day on the bikes! We have two routes on offer, one tarmac and one off-road, each around 160km.  Get ready to explore some of the most picturesque and enjoyable riding lanes and roads throughout the Cotswolds.

Open for newcomers to come and see what it’s all about, to learn how to roadbook, shake down your navigation equipment and to have a great ride out with like minded people.

We will be running a training session and briefing session for the event.

Coffee and breakfast available in the cafe on site.

A full scoring system will be in operation and results on the day.

DIGITAL & PAPER ROADBOOKS AVAILABLE

[ENTRIES CLOSED](https://rallymoto.co.uk)

### Go for ZERO!

Those who receive zero points by hitting all of the waypoints along the route and not getting any speeding penalties will receive a special ‘Zero Hero’ award at the end of the day
