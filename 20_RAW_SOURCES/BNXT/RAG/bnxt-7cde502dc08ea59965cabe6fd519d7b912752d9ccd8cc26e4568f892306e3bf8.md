# Dunlop Motorcycle Tires to Serve as Cocktail Reception Sponsor of AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony

* by [AMA Tech](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/amatechama-cycle-org/)
* August 31, 2026
* 0 Comments
* 138970 Views

**Photo Credit: Willy Browning**

PICKERINGTON, Ohio (Aug. 31, 2026) — Dunlop Motorcycle Tires — one of the largest suppliers of racing tires in America — will help recognize the next class of motorcycling legends as the Cocktail Reception Sponsor of the AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony presented by Yamaha Motor Corporation, which will take place on Sept. 10 at the Event Center at Violet Woods in Pickerington, Ohio.

“The AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony celebrates individuals who have shaped motorcycling in America,” said AMA Business Development Manager Michael Kula. “Given Dunlop’s longstanding relationship with Broc Glover, who will be honored as an AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Legend this year, we appreciate having them as a supporter of the event.”

Dunlop Motorcycle Tires is a premier brand in the competition tire industry, supporting a large number of professional and amateur racers across a wide range of racing disciplines nationwide. For more information about Dunlop Motorcycle Tires, visit [*www.dunlopmotorcycletires.com/*](http://www.dunlopmotorcycletires.com/).

“The AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony is a tribute to the people who built this sport,” said Dunlop Motorcycle Tires Senior Vice President of Sales & Marketing Michael Buckley. “We’re proud to celebrate our Senior Manager of Off-Road, Broc Glover, whose impact on motorcycling is undeniable, and honored to support this year’s ceremony as cocktail reception sponsor.”

The AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame will welcome six esteemed individuals into its hallowed halls on Sept. 10, with Russell Bobbitt, Damon Bradshaw, Eraldo Ferracci, Jake Johnson, Gary Sellers and Dale Walker set to be inducted during the event. Additionally, Broc Glover — a 2000 inductee into the AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame — will be honored as an AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Legend, which is reserved for those whose accomplishments transcend their induction category in impactful ways throughout the motorcycle community.

Tickets to the AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony are on sale now. For more details, and to purchase tickets, visit [*MotorcycleMuseum.org/events-experiences/induction-ceremony/*](https://motorcyclemuseum.org/events-experiences/induction-ceremony/)*.*
