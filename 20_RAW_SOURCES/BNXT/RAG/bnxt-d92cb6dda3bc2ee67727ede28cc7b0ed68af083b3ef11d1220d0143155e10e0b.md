# Tread Owner's Manual - Speaker and LED Wiring Harness Wire and Connector Identification

# Speaker and LED Wiring Harness Wire and Connector Identification

| Wire Group | Wire Color and Description |
| --- | --- |
| Callout number 1 Ignition | Red: Connects to the vehicle accessory line. If the ignition wire is unused, ground the ignition wire or leave it disconnected. |
| Callout number 2 Amplifier on | Blue: Connects to an optional external amplifier to turn it on when the stereo turns on. |
| Callout number 3 Speaker zone 1 | White: Left (+), Connects to the included speaker.  White with a black stripe: Left (-), Connects to the included speaker.  Gray: Right (+), Connects to the included speaker.  Gray with a black stripe: Right (-), Connects to the included speaker. |
| Callout number 4 Speaker zone 2 | Green: Left (+), Connects to an additional speaker.  Green with a black stripe: Left (-), Connects to an additional speaker.  Purple: Right (+), Connects to an additional speaker.  Purple with a black stripe: Right (-), Connects to an additional speaker. |
| Callout number 5 LED RGB1 | Black: Power  Red: Red LED  Green: Green LED  Blue: Blue LED |
| Callout number 6 LED RGB2 | Black: Power  Red: Red LED  Green: Green LED  Blue: Blue LED |
