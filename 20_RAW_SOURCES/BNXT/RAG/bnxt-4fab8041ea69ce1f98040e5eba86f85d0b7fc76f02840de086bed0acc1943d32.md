Company Members are individuals and entities directly linked to STARK FUTURE’s internal structure and operations. This includes:

- i. Board members and other governance bodies of the company;

- ii. Employees, from executive to operational levels;

- iii. Individuals engaged under any contractual arrangement, including temporary, freelance, or project-based contracts.;

- iv. Controlled Investee Companies, meaning subsidiaries or entities where STARK FUTURE holds operational control

- v. Partners or Shareholders.

## 4.2 THIRD PARTIES

The Standards of Conduct at STARK FUTURE are at the heart of our commitment to ethical behavior, integrity, and legal compliance. These standards outline the rules, behaviors, and expectations that guide all interactions within and on behalf of STARK FUTURE. While the Standards of Conduct are designed to be comprehensive, they apply to Bound Persons— Company Members and Third Parties —to the extent that is applicable to their role or relationship with STARK FUTURE.

## 6.1 ETHICAL BUSINESS PRACTICES

The ethical business practices at STARK FUTURE are fundamental to ensuring integrity, transparency, and compliance in all aspects of its operations. Bound Persons are required to uphold the highest standards of ethical conduct, ensuring that every action, decision, and business relationship reflects the company’s commitment to honesty, fairness, and legal compliance.

### 6.1.1 Integrity and Honesty

Integrity and honesty are fundamental principles governing all professional and business conduct at STARK FUTURE. Bound Persons are required to act truthfully, transparently, and in good faith in all interactions, ensuring that any information provided, whether internally or externally, is accurate, complete, and not misleading. This obligation applies to all forms of

Third Parties, including suppliers, contractors, agents, and business partners, are required to adhere to the same principles when acting on behalf of, or engaging in business with, STARK FUTURE.

## 6.1.2 Conflict of Interest

Bound Persons must avoid any situation in which personal, financial, or other interests conflict, or appear to conflict, with the interests of STARK FUTURE. A conflict of interest arises when an individual’s private interests interfere in any way with the interests of the company, potentially impairing objective decision-making, creating unfair competitive advantages, or exposing the company to reputational, financial, or legal risks.

All Company Members are obligated to disclose any actual, potential, or perceived conflicts of interest to the Legal Department or their immediate supervisor as soon as they arise

## 6.1.4 Fair Competition and Antitrust Compliance

STARK FUTURE is committed to maintaining a competitive and fair marketplace in full compliance with applicable antitrust and competition laws. 6.2.3 Gifts, Hospitality and Business Courtesies

STARK FUTURE prohibits the offering, solicitation, or acceptance of gifts, benefits, or hospitality that may improperly influence, or appear to influence, any business decision or professional judgment.

Modest business courtesies that are customary in the industry— such as promotional items bearing the supplier’s logo (e.g., caps, notebooks, t-shirts), or invitations to professional events, trade shows, or product presentations—are generally acceptable, provided they are of symbolic value and aligned with legitimate business interests.

However, any gift, hospitality, or benefit offered to a Company Member with an estimated value exceeding EUR 150 must be reported in advance to the Legal Department and may only be accepted with prior written approval.

Under no circumstances may employees accept:

- Cash, cash equivalents (e.g., gift cards, vouchers), or personal discounts not generally available;

- Benefits or invitations that could create a sense of obligation or unduly influence decision-making;

- Gifts during ongoing procurement, negotiation, or contract award processes.

Company Members must exercise sound judgment and consider the potential reputational, legal, and ethical implications of accepting any gift or benefit. When in doubt, the matter should be escalated to the Legal or Compliance Department for clarification.
