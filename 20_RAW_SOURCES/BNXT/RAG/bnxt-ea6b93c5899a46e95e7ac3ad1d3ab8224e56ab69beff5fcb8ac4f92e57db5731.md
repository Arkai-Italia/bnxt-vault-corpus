# Technical training academy

#### Comprehensive range of technical training services for workshops

The increasing complexity of vehicle systems requires a continuous expansion of technical know-how in order to effectively and efficiently maintain, repair or diagnose modern vehicles in workshops. Bosch supports the training and further education of workshop employees with modern technical and economic training programs and methods. Technical training courses provide extensive system knowledge for all vehicle types and classes. Bosch develops specific training content for manufacturers, organizes the training courses and conducts them in compliance with the highest quality standards.

## Scope of the training

With the technical and commercial training, workshop employees acquire knowledge and skills on current developments in the automotive industry and are optimally prepared for the new requirements.

The training is specifically tailored to the needs of aftermarket professionals and covers a wide range of topics such as:

* Vehicle diagnostics
* Repair and maintenance techniques
* Electronic systems
* Engine management

Highly qualified trainers from Bosch have extensive knowledge and experience in the automotive industry and offer practical training that helps expand technical know-how and increase efficiency in vehicle maintenance and repair. The training includes both theoretical and practical exercises to ensure that what has been learned can be put directly into practice.

The training offer includes both standard training and trainings tailored to the specific requirements of the respective vehicle manufacturer or regional needs. The training is offered in various formats and country-specific languages in around 30 training centers worldwide, both as online courses and as face-to-face training at the Bosch training center or on-site at the customer.

Trainings are offered for all qualification levels, for apprentices to high professional technicians. The training courses are offered for passenger cars and commercial vehicles and can be integrated into customer-specific training programs.
