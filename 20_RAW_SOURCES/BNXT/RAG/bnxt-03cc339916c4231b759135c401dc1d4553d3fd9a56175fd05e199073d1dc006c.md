# Tread Owner's Manual - Installing the Antenna on the Group Ride Mount

# Installing the Antenna on the Group Ride Mount

NOTE:
The mounted antenna must be at least 4 cm (1.57 in.) away from the nearest roll bar or metal surface to avoid radio interference.

For the best range performance, use the external antenna mount. You can mount the antenna directly onto the Group Ride mount when it is not possible to use the external antenna mount ([Mounting the Antenna](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-16B1D74D-857B-4FFB-8DE2-A0960FE0D090/EN-US/GUID-F703933D-99ED-4A12-A920-2325A9CB69C0.html)).

Screw down and tighten the antenna onto the Group Ride mount.
