# Get State-of-the-Art Tools and an AMA Discount at Motool

* by [Jim Witters](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/jwittersama-cycle-org/)
* October 12, 2020
* 0 Comments
* 2878 Views

AMA partner Motool offers next-gen motorcycle tools and accessories, including its Slacker digital suspension tuner.

And as an AMA member, you get a 10 percent discount on Motool products.

Motool’s mission is to make bike setup and maintenance simple so riders can get the most out of their bikes and stay safe. Not only can AMA members get tools and accessories at a discount, but AMA members also benefit from the bike setup videos, recommended sag settings and user guides found at [*motool.co*](https://motool.co/).

Bike setup is key to both performance and safety. The patented Motool Slacker digital suspension tuner makes it fast and simple for any rider to keep their bike on point without any help.

Motool says all of its products come with a 30-day satisfaction guarantee. There is also a 2-year limited warranty on the Slacker V4 with Bluetooth and Slacker Wireless Remote.

To get the 10 percent discount, use the AMA member discount code at checkout at [*motool.co*](https://motool.co/). To get the AMA discount code, go to [*americanmotorcyclist.com/ama-members-deals-and-discounts*](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/ama-members-deals-and-discounts/) and login. Then go to the Motool section of the page.
