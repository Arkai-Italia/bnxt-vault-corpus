# Young Enduro Range – Betamotor S.p.A.

# young enduro

## Your off-road story starts here

Beta believes in the younger generation: it has always made dreams on two wheels come true. Whether you are a young person looking for your first motorcycle, an adult who wants an agile vehicle for fun travel, or an enthusiast who wants a reliable and spirited companion, the Young Enduro range has the right Beta for you. From 50cc 2-stroke to 125 4-stroke, each model is designed to be lightweight, intuitive, and accessible, but also packed with style and character. Now it’s your turn.

design

#### AS BEAUTIFUL AS A BETA

A modern look inspired by Factory models and eye-catching details. Sleek lines and modern proportions, inspired by the Factory models that compete in the World Championships. Lightweight, streamlined plastics, striking graphics and attention to detail throughout: not just any bike, but your first real Beta.

ERGONOMICS AND VEHICLE

#### SUPER EASY TO RIDE

Compact and lightweight frames, with geometries designed to provide agility and safety in every situation: from the first mountain trails to the dirt roads outside home. The suspension is calibrated to offer immediate confidence, while the intuitive riding position and precise controls allow you to feel at ease from the very first ride. Footpegs with optimal grip, adjustable braking systems, and robust components complete a package that combines comfort, control, and durability.

engine

#### THE IDEAL COMPANION

Responsive and brilliant in the 2-stroke models, smooth and efficient in the 4-stroke models: every engine is designed to accompany you every step of the way. The 50 cc two-stroke models are perfect for learning while having fun, thanks to their linear and easily manageable power delivery. The 125 4-stroke engines combine reliability and low fuel consumption with just the right amount of power to grow and tackle longer or more challenging routes. All engines comply with the latest anti-pollution regulations, with practical solutions such as an integrated oil mixer (on 2-stroke engines) and new intake and exhaust systems.

MAINTENANCE

#### INTELLIGENT FUNCTIONALITY

Designed to be ridden every day, Beta Young Enduro bikes make maintenance quick and easy. From the easily accessible air filter to the single-lock system that manages the ignition, seat and fuel tank with a single key, to the reduced fuel consumption of the new 125 4-stroke models: everything is designed to let you spend more time riding and less time in the pits. Because your first bike should above all be a reliable companion that is always ready to go.
