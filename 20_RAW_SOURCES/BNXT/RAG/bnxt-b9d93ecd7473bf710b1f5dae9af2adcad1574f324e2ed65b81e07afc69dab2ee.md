# 2020 AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame calendar now available

* by [Jim Witters](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/jwittersama-cycle-org/)
* December 2, 2019
* 0 Comments
* 2397 Views

Grab your big, fat yellow marker and put a circle on your calendar around the dates of July 10-12, 2020, so you don’t miss the hugely popular AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days event at the Mid-Ohio Sports Car Course in Lexington, Ohio.

You don’t have a calendar, you say? You can fix that now by getting a 2020 AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame calendar that features historic bikes from the Hall of Fame. Just go to [www.amagear.com](http://www.amagear.com).

The collection of motorcycles in the AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame includes bikes ridden by the greatest racers in American motorcycling history, along with iconic models that represent leaps ahead in design or technology.

Beautifully photographed and featured alongside their histories, one of these classics is highlighted each month on the 2020 AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame calendar.

Proceeds from calendar sales help support the AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame in Pickerington, Ohio. Among other things, the Hall of Fame preserves the heritage of motorcycling; educates the public about American motorcycling’s great traditions; maintains archives of publications, photographs, records and other documents relevant to the history of motorcycling; and honors those who have contributed to the heritage of motorcycling in America.

So get your calendar now. Hurry, because supplies are limited. Big, fat yellow marker not included.
