# Tread - Overland Edition Tread XL - Overland Edition Owner's Manual - Pairing with Your Smartphone

# Pairing with Your Smartphone

You can pair your
Tread® - Overland Edition device with your smartphone and the
Tread app to enable additional features and access live information ([Live Services, Traffic, and Smartphone Features](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-157A2412-594F-45D8-A5EE-5A5327DA0914/EN-US/GUID-86A562FA-E457-48DE-A983-0E21D9681578.html)).

1. From the app store on your smartphone, install the
   Tread app.
2. Turn on your
   Tread - Overland Edition device, and place the device and your smartphone within 3 m (10 ft.) of each other.
3. On your phone, open the
   Tread app.
4. Follow the on-screen instructions to sign in to a
   Garmin® account and complete the pairing and setup process.

The main app dashboard appears. After the devices are paired, they connect automatically when they are turned on and within range.
