# 2027 Husqvarna TE 300 Pro and FE 350 Pro revealed

Published on September 10th, 2026 by Nye Davis

#### **Husqvarna bolsters its enduro stable with the addition of the TE 300 and FE 350 Pro models – complete with bolt-on upgrades and an exclusive graphics kit.**

Days after sibling brand KTM debuted its latest farkled-up HardEnduro model for MY27, Husky has lifted the lid on its own special edition duo.

The two Pro models build on the latest TE 300 and FE 350 platforms debuted earlier this summer – themselves going mechanically unchanged from last year's models – but add a list of goodies to provide equal parts bling-factor and added race-readiness.

The standout feature, as ever, is the styling. Both bikes get exclusive white and grey bodywork, a grey ribbed seat cover and white Factory Frame Protectors, versus the white, blue and yellow visuals at play on the standard TE and FE collection.

The 2027 Pro models feature the exact same base specs as the standard 300 and 350s Husqvarna offers, including radiator fans as standard, a fuel line protector and map selector switches – the three fiddy also getting traction control and a quickshifter as part of the package.

The familiarity with the base models extends to the chassis set-up too, meaning Husky’s chromium-molybdenum steel frame and WP XACT suspension package, with Closed Cartridge front forks with base-valve preload adjusters and a WP XACT rear shock features here – not a snazzy WP Pro Component in sight, despite the model naming.

The upgrades continue with a ‘Factory Racing Wheel Set’, comprising black Excel Takasago rims, black spokes and CNC-machined hubs, joined by Galfer discs and a SuperSprox rear sprocket to round things out.

Both models come with a skid plate incorporating linkage protection, front and rear brake disc guards and a clutch slave cylinder protector. The rear brake disc is also safety-wired, and a handy pull strap is located up front.

The 2027 TE 300 Pro and FE 350 Pro will arrive at authorised Husqvarna Mobility dealerships from September 2026, although availability will vary between markets.

Husqvarna is also carrying over the 12-month Premium Manufacturer’s Warranty introduced with the 2027 range. The warranty is subject to servicing and prescribed maintenance being carried out through an authorised Husqvarna Mobility dealer.

UK pricing for the two Pro models has yet to be announced.
