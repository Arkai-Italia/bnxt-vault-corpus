# 2024 AMA Supercross and Motocross Professional Numbers Announced

* by [Joy Burgess](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/joybelle98/)
* October 2, 2023
* 0 Comments
* 11466 Views

PICKERINGTON, Ohio (Oct. 2, 2023) — The American Motorcyclist Association announced the professional competition numbers for pro-licensed riders participating in 2024 Monster Energy AMA Supercross, AMA Pro Motocross Championship and SuperMotocross Championships.

Professional numbers are assigned and used in competition. The reigning champion races with the No. 1 plate when competing in the region or class in which the plate was earned, while the assigned professional number is used when competing outside of the champion’s region or title class.
