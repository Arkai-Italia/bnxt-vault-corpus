# Motorcycle ABS

#### Safer riding instead of locking

The modular and scalable motorcycle ABS (antilock braking system) was especially designed for the braking characteristics of two-wheelers and can be adapted to a wide range of models from small bikes to high-performance bikes. It supports the rider while braking, even in case of hard braking or on slippery roads. It prevents wheel lockup, enhances vehicle stability and supports optimal deceleration while braking. Functional principle motorcycle ABS

The Speed sensors at front and rear wheels detect the rotational speed of wheels by using a non-contacting measurement principle. If a wheel is at risk of locking due to intense braking or slippery road conditions, the motorcycle ABS unit reduces the braking pressure, applied by the rider and controls the wheel speed as well as the vehicle deceleration.Thereby it keeps riding stability, even on varying surfaces.
