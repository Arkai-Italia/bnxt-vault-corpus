# **QUALITY POLICY**

Stark Future SL, a company based in Barcelona and manufacturer of electric motorcycles, assumes the following principles of action that form its Quality Policy.

Implement a robust Quality Management System that adapts to the needs of the organization and is based on continuous improvement.

Periodically review the system to improve it and adapt it to the conditions of the organization. To offer the highest standards of quality and safety with the objective of assuring that our vehicles, systems and components manufactured correspond to those approved and are at the latest legislative level.

Take care of our customers, providing them with an effective and efficient product and service, in accordance with their expectations.

Maintain permanent control of legislative compliance, collaborating with the different administrations, as well as complying with the requirements of the different interested parties. The Top Management is responsible for establishing the principles and objectives regarding quality and assuring the necessary resources to achieve them, as well as the commitment to comply with the requirements and improve the effectiveness of the Quality Management system.

This policy is reviewed periodically to ensure continued suitability with changing circumstances.

This policy applies to all personnel of the organization and is communicated and publicly available.
