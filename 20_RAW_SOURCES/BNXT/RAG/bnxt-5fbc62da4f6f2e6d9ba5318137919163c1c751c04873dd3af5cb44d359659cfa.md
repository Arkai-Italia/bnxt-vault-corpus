Infront's President & CEO Philippe Blatter was named Number 35 of the most influential people in football by ESPN FC. He is the only person from a sports marketing agency in the line-up of 50 Most Influential People in football.

Philippe Blatter said: “It is truly an honour to be recognised alongside so many great and influential individuals. For many years, Infront Sports &Media's strong passion and deep understanding of football and sports have helped shaped the industry. This is only possible because of the incredible dedication, drive and high-quality performance of every single employee at Infront."

Full list available [**here**](http://kwese.espn.com/football/blog/espn-fc-united/68/post/3154248/football-50-most-influential-people-espn-fc-2017-countdown).
