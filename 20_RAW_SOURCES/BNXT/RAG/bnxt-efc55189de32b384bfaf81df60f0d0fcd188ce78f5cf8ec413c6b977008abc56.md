# Street Skills Discount Now Available to American Motorcyclist Association Members

* by [Jim Witters](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/jwittersama-cycle-org/)
* July 21, 2020
* 0 Comments
* 2456 Views

## Online course is based on ‘Cornering Confidence’ book

PICKERINGTON, Ohio — Members of the American Motorcyclist Association are eligible for a 10 percent discount on all online courses offered by Street Skills LLC, a new AMA Member Benefit Partner.

The Street Skills curriculum is based on Jon DelVecchio’s 2018 book “Cornering Confidence: The Formula for 100% Control in Curves,” and includes 109 online lessons.

“This course provides motorcyclists with a simple progression of skills to elevate their cornering control and confidence within a short period of time,” Delvecchio said. “The ultimate goal of this course is mastering trail braking technique.”

The Churchville, N.Y., company also organizes coaching tours and track days.

“AMA members are always seeking ways to improve their riding skills to become more confident on the road,” said AMA Director of Membership Marketing and Services Amanda Donchess. “More confidence leads to more enjoyment on each and every ride. This discount is one more way the AMA provides value to its members.”

For more information about Street Skills offerings, visit [streetskills.net](http://streetskills.net).

To view all of the discounts available to AMA members, go to [americanmotorcyclist.com/ama-members-deals-and-discounts](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/ama-members-deals-and-discounts).
