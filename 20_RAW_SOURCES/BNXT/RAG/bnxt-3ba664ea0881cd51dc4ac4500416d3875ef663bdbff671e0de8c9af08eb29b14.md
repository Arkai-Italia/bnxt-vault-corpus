# Get Your Discounted AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days Tickets…But Hurry!

* by [Joy Burgess](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/jburgess/)
* April 20, 2021
* 0 Comments
* 3131 Views

April 20, 2021

By Joy Burgess

Vintage bike fans have been ecstatic about the news that **AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days** is on for July 23-25 at Mid-Ohio Sports Car Course in Lexington, Ohio. We’ve gotten letters, email and phone calls galore from members who are stoked to be a part of the event for 2021, and we can’t wait to see you all there.

If you’re planning to attend, make sure you buy your tickets soon, as **prices go up April 27!** You can grab tickets for VMD 2021 from Mid-Ohio at [midohio.com/tickets](http://midohio.com/tickets?fbclid=IwAR00fVl3FpDJ-X3jh4MptTCIGAv8obiBjUvWdP1lVdcJxLX47QOoCOs5XsI). They’ll be available beyond that date, but will be more expensive, so hurry!

We’ve already announced our special theme for VMD 2021: A celebration of 50 years of ***On Any Sunday***, which debuted in July of 1971. Hard to believe it’s been that long, and we can’t think of a cooler way to celebrate half a century of Bruce Brown’s epic moto documentary than showcasing some of the people, motorcycles and memorabilia from the film.

Stay tuned in the coming weeks, as we’ve got a huge announcement coming about our Grand Marshal for 2021…and many more exciting details.
