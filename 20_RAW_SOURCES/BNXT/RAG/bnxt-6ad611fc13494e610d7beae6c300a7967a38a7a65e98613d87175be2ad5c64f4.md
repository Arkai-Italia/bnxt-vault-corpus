# Support – ICO Racing

# Support

---

## CheckMate™ Battery Replacement

[CheckMate™ Mail-In battery replacement](https://www.icoracing.com/products/checkmate-battery-replacement) can be [purchased online](https://www.icoracing.com/products/checkmate-battery-replacement). For other repairs, please see our [CheckMate™ Reconditioning](https://www.icoracing.com/products/checkmate-reconditioning) service. Please see our [race schedule](https://www.icoracing.com/pages/about-us) for possible delays.

## CheckMate™ Reconditioning

If your out-of-warranty CheckMate™ requires internal repairs, such as replacing the LCD or the wires, we can now offer [complete reconditioning](https://www.icoracing.com/products/checkmate-reconditioning). This service is [purchased online](https://www.icoracing.com/products/checkmate-reconditioning) and you will receive mailing instructions via email.

## Rallye VRL™ and Dual Sport VRL™ Repairs

For repairs of your Rallye VRL™ or Dual Sport VRL™ computers, please see our [flat-rate repair service](https://www.icoracing.com/products/flat-rate-repair). Please see our [race schedule](https://ico-racing.myshopify.com/pages/about-us) for possible delays.

## Technical Questions

ICO Racing™ [authorized dealers](https://www.icoracing.com/pages/dealer-locator) are experts in rally equipment and preparation. For technical support, it's best to contact the dealer from whom you purchased your ICO Racing™ product.

If you purchased directly from ICO Racing™, please email our [support team](mailto:customerservice@icoracing.com).
