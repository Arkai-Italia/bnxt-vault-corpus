# Getting Started

WARNING

See the
Important Safety and Product Information guide in the product box for product warnings and other important information.

1. Connect to a
   Wi‑Fi® network
    ([Connecting to a Wireless Network](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-68D0B0AA-8293-4BB6-A338-22AB76722DE2.html))(optional).
2. Pair your
   Bluetooth® phone and headset ([Pairing with Your Smartphone](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-9983ED7E-888E-4AD6-A449-CBD17B7838C5.html)) (optional).
3. Update the maps and software on your device ([Product Updates](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-3CCB0850-544E-4983-8D61-3CEB91253BCA.html)).
4. Acquire GPS signals.
5. Mount the device and connect it to power ([Installation](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-722C48BD-8626-490F-B53B-5A1226452999.html#GUID-722C48BD-8626-490F-B53B-5A1226452999)).

7. Navigate to your destination ([Starting a Route](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-8A6627F6-AB1E-4DDE-9CB3-193224CAEDB9.html)).
