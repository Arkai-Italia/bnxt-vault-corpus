# Yamaha Motor Corporation Joins as Presenting Sponsor of AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony

* by [AMA Tech](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/amatechama-cycle-org/)
* August 12, 2026
* 0 Comments
* 130285 Views

**Photo Credit: Willy Browning**

PICKERINGTON, Ohio (Aug. 12, 2026) — Yamaha Motor Corporation will serve as the presenting sponsor of the 2026 AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony, which will take place on Sept. 10 at the Event Center at Violet Woods in Pickerington, Ohio.

“We are so grateful for Yamaha’s support of the AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame and the Induction Ceremony, as they have been a steadfast advocate for the Hall of Fame and its mission over the years,” AMA Business Development Manager Michael Kula said. “We look forward to continuing our relationship with Yamaha Motor Corporation and working together to produce another outstanding AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony.”

Yamaha Motor Corporation began producing motorcycles in 1955 with the release of the YA-1, growing into one of the largest international motorcycle brands while simultaneously emerging as a power in motorsports across a number of racing disciplines.

“The AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame preserves the rich history of American motorcycling and celebrates all of the individuals and groups that have elevated our sport to where it is today,” Yamaha Motorsports Marketing Director Steve Nessl said. “We take great pride in supporting the AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony and celebrating the outstanding individuals that will be forever enshrined amongst the legends.”

For more information on Yamaha Motor Corporation, visit [*https://yamaha-motor.com/*](https://yamaha-motor.com/).

This year, the AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame will welcome six new inductees to its hallowed halls — and recognize Broc Glover as an AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Legend — during the Induction Ceremony. The AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Class of 2026 includes Gary Sellers, Jake Johnson, Damon Bradshaw, Russell Bobbitt, Eraldo Ferracci and Dale Walker.

Tickets for the 2026 AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony presented by Yamaha Motor Corporation are on sale now. For more details, visit [*MotorcycleMuseum.org/events-experiences/induction-ceremony/*](https://motorcyclemuseum.org/events-experiences/induction-ceremony/)*.*
