# Federal Motorcycle Transport Co-Sponsors 2023 Permco AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days Swap Meet

* by [Jack Emerson](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/jemersonama-cycle-org/)
* June 15, 2023
* 0 Comments
* 12647 Views

PICKERINGTON, Ohio (June 15, 2023) — Federal Motorcycle Transport will return to co-sponsor the swap meet at the 2023 AMA Permco Vintage Motorcycle Days presented by Royal Enfield July 21-23 at the Mid-Ohio Sports Car Course in Lexington, Ohio.

“Federal Motorcycle Transport is proud to continue our sponsorship of the AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days swap meet,” said Federal Motorcycle Transport Director of Marketing John Olson. “The AMA and their partners put on an amazing event — we can’t recommend it enough.”

Serving as the premier option for motorcycle shipping, Federal Motorcycle Transport aims to provide fast, secure and reliable shipping for all of its customers. With over 30 years of experience in the industry, it serves more than 2,000 riders each year.

As the largest motorcycle swap meet in North America, the AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days swap meet will boast more than 800 vendor spaces from across the United States and will take place on all three days of the 2023 Permco AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days presented by Royal Enfield.

“We love having Federal Motorcycle Transport back as a co-sponsor of the AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days swap meet,” AMA Editorial Director Mitch Boehm said. “With so much cool stuff available in our swap meet, entire motorcycles included, shipping can be an issue, and Federal is there to help…and we appreciate their support.”

There will be plenty of other exciting happenings at the three-day event, including the AMA Hall of Fame Bike Show and vintage racing opportunities in many disciplines. For those interested in racing, visit [*https://www.vintagemotorcycledays.com/page/racing*](https://www.vintagemotorcycledays.com/page/racing)*.*

Steve Wise, an AMA Motorcycle Hall of Famer and 1982 AMA Pro Athlete of the Year, will be the Grand Marshal for the 2023 Permco AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days presented by Royal Enfield. Wise will be in attendance and be available to sign autographs and speak with enthusiasts from the AMA Soundstage, along with leading the Lap For History event each day.

For more information regarding Federal Motorcycle Transport, visit [*https://funtransport.com*](https://funtransport.com)*.*

To find out more about Vintage Motorcycle Days, visit [*https://www.vintagemotorcycledays.com*](https://www.vintagemotorcycledays.com)*,* sign up for the [AMA VMD newsletter](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/ama-vintage-motorcycle-days-e-news-sign-up-2/) or follow #AMAVMD on social media channels. To purchase tickets, visit [*https://www.vintagemotorcycledays.com/tickets*](https://www.vintagemotorcycledays.com/tickets).
