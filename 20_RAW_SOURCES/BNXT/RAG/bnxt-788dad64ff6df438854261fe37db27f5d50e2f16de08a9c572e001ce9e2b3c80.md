### Dual Front Fuel Tanks

The additional front-mounted fuel tanks add 7 litres, working together with the 13-litre rear tank to bring total fuel capacity to 20 litres. This provides the range required for extended rides and remote terrain.

### Rally Tower & Windshield

A purpose-built rally tower with integrated windshield improves wind protection at higher speeds and over long distances. This reduces fatigue during extended off-road stages and fast liaison sections.

Improved control in off-road conditions

Slim, rally-inspired bodywork is designed for rider movement and control, allowing easy transitions between seated and standing riding in technical terrain.
