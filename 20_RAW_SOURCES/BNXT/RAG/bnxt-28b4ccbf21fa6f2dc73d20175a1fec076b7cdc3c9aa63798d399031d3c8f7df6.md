# 2027 KTM 690 ENDURO R - ALL TERRAIN, ALL THE TIME | KTM United States

## 01. CLUTCH IN, REVS UP!

PLUSH PERFORMANCE

### Adjustablity

Finding the perfect setup when attacking your favorite stretch of dirt or twisty mountain pass is easy, thanks to the KTM 690 ENDURO R's adjustable suspension, brake and clutch levers, and footrests - not to mention a complete PowerPart catalog filled with performance and style enhancing add-ons.

INFOTAINMENT

### TFT Display & Connectivity

The 4.2" TFT dashboard takes prime position ahead of the rider, featuring a unique, specially-designed user interface to suit the compact screen size. Made from bonded glass, the TFT screen also boasts a special coating and polarization to reduce glare and increase visibility in all lighting conditions. Riders can also access music, incoming calls, turn-by-turn navigation, and Ride modes via the dashboard - with a USB-C port for device charging duties.

### Bodywork

With an ergo triangle specifically designed to cater to everything from fast berms, to casual commutes, the KTM 690 ENDURO R boasts aggressive, offroad-ready bodywork. The bodywork is also made from proven, flexible, and long-lasting PP plastic material better suited for off-roading and aggressive riding.
