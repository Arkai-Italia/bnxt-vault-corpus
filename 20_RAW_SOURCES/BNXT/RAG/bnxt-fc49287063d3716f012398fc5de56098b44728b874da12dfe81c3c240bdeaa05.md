# This Month in *American Motorcyclist*

##### **October Digital Issue: Why We Love VMD!**

So, why do we love VMD? Could it be Hall of Fame superstars like Freddie Spencer grand marshal-ing the weekend? Or maybe it’s the nonstop vintage racing, from motocross and hare scrambles to road racing and pit bike mayhem? Or perhaps it’s all the fun off the track that gets our collective motor running? We’d like to think it’s all of the above. Kudos to Kevin Wing for the cover shot.
