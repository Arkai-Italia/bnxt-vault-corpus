# Tread Owner's Manual - Red Light and Speed Cameras

# Red Light and Speed Cameras

NOTICE

Garmin® is not responsible for the accuracy of or the consequences of using a red light or speed camera database.

NOTE:
This feature is not available for all regions or product models.

Information about red light and speed camera locations is available in some areas for some product models. The device alerts you when you approach a reported speed or red light camera.

* In some areas, your device can receive live red light and speed camera data while connected to a smartphone running the
  Tread™ app.
* You can use the
  Garmin Express™ software ([garmin.com/express](http://www.garmin.com/express)) to update the camera database stored on your device. You should update your device frequently to receive the most up-to-date camera information.
