## Shock Springs - Progressive

We offer [multiple brands of shock springs](https://www.teknikmotorsport.com/FAQ/#faq99516) in many different sizes and in many different spring rates - including linear (straight) rate as well as progressive rate which start softer and get firmer during the suspension travel. We also offer a handful of [specialist fork & shock lowering springs](https://www.teknikmotorsport.com/motorcycle-suspension-springs/) when they are in stock.

### Progressive-rate springs for adaptable rear suspension

* Softer initial response for small bumps and traction
* Increasing stiffness through travel to resist bottoming and handle heavy loads
* Available in multiple inner diameters and free heights for exact fitment
* Measured by inner diameter (in millimetres), as they need to be big enough for space around the damper, and their total height with no pre-load applied (also in millimetres)
* Technical support available: learn about coil springs & preload or replacing your spring with our guides

### Recommended Pairings

Consider matching your shock springs with [Suspension Tools](https://www.teknikmotorsport.com/motorcycle-suspension-tools/) for safe installation and setup.
