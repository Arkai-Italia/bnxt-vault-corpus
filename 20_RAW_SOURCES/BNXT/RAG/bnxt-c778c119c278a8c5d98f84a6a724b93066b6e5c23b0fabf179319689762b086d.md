# Pedestrian protection system

#### An electronic guardian angel for pedestrians

Pedestrians are particularly at risk in traffic. In most accidents, the pedestrian collides with the front of the vehicle. Legal requirements and consumer protection tests for pedestrian protection have become significantly stricter in recent years. This presents significant challenges for sensor equipment, which must reliably detect an impact between the vehicle and a pedestrian. Bosch’s electronic system for active impact protection for pedestrians is a safe and cost-effective solution, which fulfils the legal requirements for pedestrian protection on vehicles.

## Operation principle of the pedestrian protection system

The system consists of peripheral sensors in the front part of the vehicle and the airbag control unit, which triggers actuators that can, for example, lift the engine hood within a fraction of a second. This allows for the

pedestrian to impact against a more effective crumple zone, thereby minimizing the risk of injury.
