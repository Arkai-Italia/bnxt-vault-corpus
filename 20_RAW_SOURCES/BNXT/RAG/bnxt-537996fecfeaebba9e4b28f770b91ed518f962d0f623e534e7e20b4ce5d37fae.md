## Using the Measurement Tool

If you don’t know your knee cup measurements, you can use the measurement tool that is supplied with the knee brace. In the standing position, hold the measurement tool parallel to the ground and slide it from the front towards the back over the central part of the knee. You need to find the maximum width of your knee. The arrow will point towards a knee brace size that should fit your knee cup width. This should only be used as an indication and we strongly recommend you try on a knee brace to ensure you purchase the correct size.

# **_4. FITTING PROCEDURE_**

In order to provide the most effective performance it is absolutely essential that the correct size of Bionic Knee Brace is selected AND that it is adjusted correctly to your knee. The fitting is best conducted in conjunction with the other items of clothing you normally wear while riding (undersock, boots, pants). Note that pants must be worn on top of the Bionic Knee Brace. The Bionic Knee Brace is fitted as follows:

1. Referring to table one in the previous section, select the correct size of brace that corresponds to your knee width.

2. The braces are NOT symmetrical. Identify the correct brace (left or right) for the leg that will be fitted. If the label is not visible, the side with 4 Snap Buckles (11), should always face the outside of the leg.

3. Open the Adjustable Straps (2, 3, 7 & 9) and Snap Buckles (11).

4. With your leg completely straight, place the brace onto the knee. The brace should be firmly in contact with (or ‘grab’) the sides of the knee such that when standing upright the brace stays loosely attached even if no other straps are fastened. If the brace does not stay on by itself then the additional Side Padding (5) will need to be fitted. See section 5 for instructions.

5. When closing the Adjustable Straps (2, 3, 7 & 9) the Snap Buckles (11) MUST ALWAYS be done in the following order: First Strap number 1 (7), then Strap number 2 (3), then Strap number 3 (9) and lastly Strap number 4 (2). See diagram 4 on the next page.

# **_6. CARE & STORAGE_**

The soft liners and straps of the Bionic Knee Brace may be detached and machine washed using a cold wash cycle.

The Calf Frame and other parts of the Bionic Knee Brace are NOT machine washable and should only be cleaned using a damp cloth with mild soap and water. Rinse thoroughly under running water afterwards. DO NOT clean the Bionic Knee Brace using a high pressure jet washer.

The use of strong cleaning detergents must be avoided - over time these detergents could weaken the Frame and liners, even if there is no visual sign of damage. After washing it is important that the Bionic Knee Brace is properly dried before it is stored. DO NOT tumble dry.

# **_7. MAINTENANCE_**

The Bionic Knee Brace should be periodically inspected for signs of wear or damage. Check additionally for the presence of foreign objects in the hinges and remove any such objects using water or compressed air.

In the event of an accident, inspect the Bionic Knee Brace carefully. If it is not intact and/or there are cracks or chips in the structure and/or tears in the straps, then the Bionic Knee Brace must be replaced. Check the hinges by bending the Bionic Knee Brace and ensure the hinges operate correctly throughout the full motion. If not the Bionic Knee Brace must be replaced or repaired. In case of doubt check with an authorized Alpinestars Dealer.

Under no circumstances attempt to modify the Bionic Knee Brace or disassemble the hinges or any parts of the Bionic Knee Brace - this includes the application of paint or other stickers which will compromise the material integrity of the Bionic Knee Brace.

At the end of its product life the Bionic Knee Brace must be disposed of in accordance with local refuse regulations. There are no hazardous materials used in the manufacture of the Bionic Knee Brace.

# **_6. NETTOYAGE ET RANGEMENT_**

Après le lavage, il est important que la genouillère Bionic soit correctement séchée avant de la ranger. Ne pas sécher en machine.

Si ce n’est pas le cas, vous devrez remplacer ou faire réparer votre genouillère Bionic. En cas de doute, adressez-vous à un revendeur Alpinestars agréé. N’essayez en aucun cas de modifier la genouillère Bionic ou d’en démonter les charnières ou des pièces, n’appliquez aucune peinture ou autocollants qui compromettraient l’intégrité matérielle de la genouillère Bionic.

À la fin de sa durée de vie, les genouillères Bionic doivent être jetées conformément à la réglementation locale relative aux déchets. Aucune matière dangereuse n’est utilisée dans la fabrication de la genouillère Bionic.

# **_6. PLEJE OG OPBEVARING_**

De bløde foringer og remme på Bionic knæbeskytteren kan tages af og vaskes i maskinen på et koldt vaskeprogram.

Rammen til lægbenet og andre dele af Bionic knæbeskytteren kan IKKE maskinvaskes og bør kun rengøres med en fugtig klud med mild sæbe og vand. Skyl grundigt under rindende vand bagefter. Rengør IKKE Bionic knæbeskytteren med højtryksrenser.

Brug af stærke rengøringsmidler skal undgås - over tid kan disse rengøringsmidler svække rammen og foringer, selvom der ikke er nogen synlige tegn på skade. Efter vask er det vigtigt, at Bionic knæbeskytteren tørres ordentligt, før den opbevares. MÅ IKKE tørretumbles.

# **_7. VEDLIGEHOLDELSE_**

Bionic knæbeskytteren skal jævnligt inspiceres for tegn på slitage eller beskadigelse. Kontroller desuden, om der er fremmedlegemer i hængslerne, og fjern eventuelle fremmedlegemer med vand eller trykluft.

I tilfælde af en ulykke skal man inspicere Bionic knæbeskytteren omhyggeligt. Hvis den ikke er intakt og/eller der er revner eller splinter i strukturen og/eller revner i remmene, skal Bionic knæbeskytteren udskiftes. Kontroller hængslerne ved at bøje Bionic knæbeskytteren og sikr, at hængslerne fungerer korrekt under den fulde bevægelse. Hvis ikke, skal Bionic knæbeskytteren udskiftes eller repareres. I tvivlstilfælde skal du kontakte en autoriseret Alpinestars-forhandler. Forsøg under ingen omstændigheder at ændre Bionic knæbeskytteren eller adskille hængslerne eller andre dele af Bionic knæbeskytteren - dette omfatter påføring af maling eller andre klistermærker, som vil kompromittere Bionic knæbeskytterens materialeintegritet.

Når Bionic knæbeskytteren er udtjent, skal den bortskaffes i overensstemmelse med lokale affaldsbestemmelser. Der er ikke brugt farlige materialer til fremstilling af Bionic knæbeskytteren.
