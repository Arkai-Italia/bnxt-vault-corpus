## **Introducing the F2R Y1000**

---

The Gamma1000 has been developed over the course of three and a half years, drawing on F2R’s extensive expertise in roadbook navigation, honed through years of experience in the extreme conditions and environments of rallies. Additionally, it incorporates the team’s deepest knowledge of various navigation apps. Designed as a **navigation aid made for bikes and SSVs**, the device has undergone rigorous testing, covering more than 55.000 kilometers (*34.175 miles*) to ensure reliability and performance.

It’s purposely built to excel under extreme conditions, including wide-range temperatures, intense vibrations, direct exposure to sunlight and high-impact scenarios.

The Y1000 features, among other apps, a Roadbook app designed by F2R to transform the traditional roadbook navigation into a highly interactive and digital experience. Our goal has been to **digitalise Roadbook navigation** without changing its essence.

## **Standout Features**

---

* **Fully CNC-Machined Aluminum Housing** Designed to resist Tough Impacts;
* **4mm Display Glass** built to Endure Most Crashes and Helmet Impacts;
* ****4:3 Ratio Display**** Designed to **Match the Shape of an A5 Paper Roadbook Roll** and Tripmasters into One single Device improving Functionality****.******Ratio Display** Designed to Match the Shape of an A5 Paper Roadbook Roll;
* **High-Brightness Display with F2R’s Special Coating**, Optimized for Visibility in Direct Sunlight; even when sunlight hits the screen, it creates a limited reflection spot in that specific area while keeping the rest of the screen clearly visible.
  The **Maximum Brightness is Sustainable 100% of Time**, independent of outside conditions.
* **Touchscreen Operable with Gloves**;
* **Fully Waterproof**;
* Its Precision-Engineered Aluminum Housing and Integrated Heat Sink Allow the Device to Efficiently ******Stand up to****** **High Temperatures**;
* **Detachable GammaBox** (Android Box) enables users to transfer their information between bikes effortlessly. Even in a racing scenario, the chassis can be swapped without losing the roadbook or validated waypoints.
* Operating System: **Android 13** Fully Customized by F2R to Provide the Simplest User Experience While Retaining All Essential Features for the Device’s Purpose;
* **Roadbook Software** includes up to two odometers, a CAP header, a speedometer with customizable warnings, and a waypoint arrow, delivering the **most advanced and comprehensive navigation experience;**
* **Third-Party App:** Install and use various navigation apps, import GPX tracks, and download offline maps effortlessly;
* **High-Performance Integrated GPS** with a Refresh Rate of Up to 25Hz and a Circular Error Probable (CEP) of Less Than 1 Meter;
* ****F2R Power Management System**** – Detects inactivity, automatically powers off in specific situations, and enters power-saving mode to optimize battery’s vehicle consumption;
* A **Remote** (DR001, sold separately) that **automatically adapts its functionality to each app**, enabling seamless control of roadbook applications as well as other navigation, GPS, and music apps;
* Covered by a **3-Year Warranty** Against Manufacturing and Material Defects.
