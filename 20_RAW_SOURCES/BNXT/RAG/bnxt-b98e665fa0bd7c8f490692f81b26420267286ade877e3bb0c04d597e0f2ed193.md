# Electric motors and active parts

#### High performance and efficient electric drive solutions for passenger cars and light commercial vehicles

The electric motor serves as a power unit for pure electric and plug-in-hybrid vehicles. As a traction motor, the electric motor converts electrical energy into mechanical power and provides hybrid or purely electric propulsion. The electric motor transmits its torque directly to the transmission gear based on the application. Four different standard diameters are used to satisfy the various torque requirements. By converting mechanical to electrical energy, the electric motor charges the vehicle's battery.

## Direct cooling for electric motors: the future of cooling technology

Martin Fickert, product manager for electric motors, presents how Bosch is handling this challenge.

Electric motors from Bosch achieve efficiencies of up to 98%. Some of the remaining losses result from thermal dissipation. It is a significant challenge to cool this heat directly at the source – especially when space is limited.
