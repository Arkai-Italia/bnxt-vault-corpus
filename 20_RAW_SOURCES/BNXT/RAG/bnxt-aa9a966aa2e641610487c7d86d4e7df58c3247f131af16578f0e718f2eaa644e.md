Have a damaged CheckMate™ no longer covered under warranty? No problem. Send it in and we'll recondition the computer for you.

**PLEASE READ THIS INFORMATION CAREFULLY...**

Place your order online and you'll receive an order confirmation email. The order confirmation email contains the mailing instructions, our mailing address, and your receipt.

Reconditioning includes:

* Fresh battery
* Replacement wire pigtails
* A new lens and red bezel
* LCD replaced as required
* Buttons replaced with new "click" style
* Any other repairs as required
* 90 day warranty

Reconditioning only available for red CheckMate™ and CheckMate+™ computers. For the grey CheckMate XL™, we offer our [battery replacement service](https://www.icoracing.com/products/checkmate-battery-replacement) only.

Parts for refurbishing were backordered for a long time, so service time will be longer than usual -perhaps 3 weeks- while work catches up with demand.
