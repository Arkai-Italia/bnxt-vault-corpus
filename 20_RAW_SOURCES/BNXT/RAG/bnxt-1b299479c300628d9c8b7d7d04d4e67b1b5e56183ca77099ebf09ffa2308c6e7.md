### Mitas Rocket Ride

## On Thursday from 12:00 PM, only one thing matters: full throttle up the longest steep slope of the Erzberg! Each driver has two attempts, but only the 48 fastest will make it to the grand super final at 8:00 PM. Then it's: Six riders, one slope, pure adrenaline action! Only the fastest advance – until the first Erzbergrodeo rock trophy is awarded. Whether professional or amateur rider: this is where the wheat is separated from the chaff. Fans will find excellent views in the visitor zones, just a few minutes from the arena – with catering and commentary during the evening finale.

### Xtreme Trial Challenge

Many extreme enduro stars learned their skills in trials – and this shows why: perfect riding technique determines victory or defeat!

On Friday and Saturday, the riders will battle it out in qualifying for the fastest lap on the spectacular course featuring a 5-meter-high banked turn. Only the 48 fastest will make it to the grand super final on Saturday evening (8:00 PM).

There, top-level action awaits – and a highly coveted prize: a starting place in the front row at the Red Bull Erzbergrodeo!
