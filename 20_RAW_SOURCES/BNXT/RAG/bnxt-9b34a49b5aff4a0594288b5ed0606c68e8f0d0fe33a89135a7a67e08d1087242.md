# Tread Owner's Manual - Driver Assistance Settings

# Driver Assistance Settings

Select
 > Driver Assistance.

Rider Alerts
:   Allows you to enable or disable alerts for upcoming zones or road conditions ([Rider Awareness Features and Alerts](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-16B1D74D-857B-4FFB-8DE2-A0960FE0D090/EN-US/GUID-EEB783D7-1CEE-47C0-AE49-2380869F02F8.html)).

Speeding Alert
:   Alerts you when you are exceeding the speed limit.

Reduced Speed Tone
:   Alerts you when the speed limit decreases.

Route Preview
:   Shows a preview of the major roads in your route when you start navigation.

Proximity Alerts
:   Alerts you when you approach speed cameras or red light cameras.
