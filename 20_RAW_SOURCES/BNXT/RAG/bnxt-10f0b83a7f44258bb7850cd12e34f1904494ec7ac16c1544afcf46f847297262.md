**Motoklik Intended Use**

Motoklik is a data acquisition device designed for use with motorcycles that have off-road capability. The device is designed to provide users with information representative of the device’s latitude and longitude, and representative of the front and rear suspension position of the motorcycle. Motoklik cannot guarantee complete accuracy of the data recorded by, or represented on the device or it’s associated software. Motoklik is designed to be as safe and effective as possible, but issues may still arise through use of the product that are out of Motoklik’s control.

There are two types of Motoklik user:

1. “Rider users” that can view their own data.
2. “Suspension technician users” that can upon an accepted request to a rider user, view the rider user’s data through the online dashboard.

**Software:**

Motoklik is designed to work with Android and iOS devices.

Motoklik analytics dashboard(s) are designed to work with web enabled devices.

The key features of the Motoklik app are:

1. Discovering Motoklik devices within the user’s vicinity.
2. Connecting to a Motoklik device within the user’s vicinity.
3. Wirelessly communicating with the Motoklik device to:
   1. View the devices current latitude and longitude.
   2. Start recording data.
   3. Stop recording data.
   4. View the number of saved sessions on the device.
   5. Select and download a single session of data from the device.
   6. Delete sessions of data from the device.
   7. View the real time representative values of the front and rear suspensions millimetre position.
4. Present the representative satellite position data so that it can be analysed to show:
   1. Lap time data.
   2. Lap section time data.
5. Present the representative suspension millimetre position so that it can be analysed to show:
   1. The front and rear suspension position and speed for the entire session.
   2. The front and rear suspension position and speed for a full lap.
   3. The front and rear suspension position and speed for a selected section of a lap.
   4. The latitude and longitude coordinates where the front or rear suspension position has gone above a user specified millimetre value.
6. Upload session data to the Motoklik online database.
7. Accept and Reject requests from suspension technician users to view rider user’s data through the online dashboard.

**Hardware:**

The Motoklik device comprises of a number of sub-devices to produce the system. The Motoklik device has been designed so that there is no modification or alteration needed on the motorcycle.

The sub-devices are:

1. The Central Control Unit, which is the main sub-device, is used to distribute power to, and communicate with the other sub-devices, record information, and wirelessly communicate with the Motoklik app. The Central Control Unit also contains a rechargeable battery.
2. The Front Suspension wand is used to determine the position of the front magnet to provide representative data of the front suspension position.
3. The Rear Suspension sensor is used to determine the position of the rear magnet to provide representative data of the rear suspension position.
4. The Satellite Position antenna is used to determine the latitude and longitude of the antenna, and also encloses a push button which can be used to start and stop recording session data. The push button is illuminated by a led with two colours, red and green. The illumination of the button has the following meanings:
   1. Pulsing Red, the satellite antenna does not have a fix, and the Motoklik is powered by the internal battery.
   2. Flashing Red, the satellite and antenna does not have a fix, and the Motoklik is powered by the motorcycles power rail.
   3. Pulsing Green, the satellite antenna does have a fix, and the Motoklik is powered by the internal battery.
   4. Flashing Green, the satellite antenna does have a fix, and the Motoklik is powered by the motorcycles power rail.
   5. Solid Green, the button has been pressed, and the Motoklik is recording data. The button must be pressed again and the Motoklik will return to the Flashing green status.

5. The Motoklik system can be powered via the power supply cable. The power supply cable plugs into the motorcycles On-Board Diagnostic (OBD) port, and takes advantage of the motorcycles power rail which is powered when the bike is running. When the motorcycle is running, and the power supply cable is attached, the internal battery in the Motoklik is recharged and the Motoklik system is powered by the external power supply through the power rail. When the motorcycle is not running, the power rail is disabled after a period of time and the internal Motoklik battery is no longer charging. The Motoklik will then remain powered by the internal battery for a period of ten minutes after the power rail supply is no longer available, but data cannot be recorded when there is no external power being supplied from the motorcycles power rail. The power supply cable is equipped with a fuse.

6. The Front Magnet Holder contains a strong magnet which is detected by the front sensor wand. The front magnet holder is designed to replace, or partially replace the front brake line clamp.

7. The Rear Magnet Holder contains a strong magnet which is detected by the rear sensor. The rear magnet holder is designed to replace or work alongside the rear brake line clamp or brake line guide.
