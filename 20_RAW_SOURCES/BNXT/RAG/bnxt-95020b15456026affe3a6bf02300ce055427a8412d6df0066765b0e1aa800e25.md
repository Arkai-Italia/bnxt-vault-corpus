# EnduroGP France: Josep Garcia crowned EnduroGP and E1 World Champion

Published on July 18th, 2026 by Nicki Martinez

**Josep Garcia delivers another historic day at the French GP. The Red Bull KTM Factory Racing rider wins Day 2 in Saint-Agrève to wrap up both the EnduroGP and E1 world titles with one round still to go. The Spaniard now has nine FIM Enduro World Championship titles to his name.**

A huge day for Josep Garcia in Saint-Agrève. The Spanish rider was crowned 2026 EnduroGP and E1 World Champion after winning the second and final day of the Polisport GP of France, round six and the penultimate stop of the Paulo Duarte FIM EnduroGP World Championship.

And he did it the way he knows best: by winning.

After a dominant opening day from Zach Pichon in front of his home crowd, Sunday once again turned into a straight fight between the Frenchman and Garcia. This time, though, the Red Bull KTM Factory Racing rider came out on top in a tense finale, taking the day win and both world titles in the process.

Garcia’s latest success takes his career tally to nine FIM Enduro World Championship titles.

**Garcia vs Pichon all the way to the final test**

Pichon started Sunday where he had left off on Saturday, setting the fastest time in the opening Expotrade Cross Test and GMoto Enduro Test. Garcia, back on his previous set-up, responded in the Polisport Extreme Test and ended the first of three laps with a 1.64-second lead over the Frenchman.

Behind them, Steve Holcombe held third, but was already 13 seconds back as Garcia and Pichon set a pace of their own at the front.

Pichon hit back on lap two, winning both the Cross Test and Enduro Test to retake the lead. But a costly mistake in the following Extreme Test turned the race around again. Garcia took full advantage, set the fastest time and moved back into first place.

Pichon wasn’t done yet. The Frenchman chipped away at the gap on the final lap and arrived at the last Extreme Test just 1.31 seconds behind Garcia.

At that point, second place would have been enough for Garcia to mathematically secure the EnduroGP title. But the rider from Súria wasn’t interested in settling for that.

He went for the win, set the scratch time in the final test and sealed the race and the world title in one hit.

**Day win, EnduroGP title and E1 crown**

Pichon had to settle for second, missing out on an EnduroGP double at his home round, while Steve Holcombe completed the podium in third, 45 seconds behind Garcia. Brad Freeman finished fourth, with Hamish Macdonald rounding out the top five.

The overall victory also gave Garcia his 12th Enduro1 win of the season, enough to successfully defend the E1 title with one round remaining. Jeremy Sydow finished second in the class, with Morgan Lesiardo third.

**Pichon wins E2, Holcombe takes control of E3**

Despite missing out on the overall win, Pichon was again the man to beat in Enduro2, completing a class double over the weekend. The result also cuts his deficit to championship leader Andrea Verona to just six points.

The Frenchman won by more than a minute over Hamish Macdonald, while Verona had to settle for third.

In Enduro3, Steve Holcombe rounded off a strong French GP with his second class victory of the weekend. The Brit also leaves Saint-Agrève as the new championship leader heading into the final round. Brad Freeman finished second, with Julien Roussaly completing the Sherco presence on the podium in third.

**Elgari back on top in Junior**

In Tsubaki Junior Enduro, Alberto Elgari bounced back from third place on Saturday with a commanding Day 2 victory.

The championship leader took control from the opening lap and stayed out front to claim his sixth win of the season, finishing 11 seconds clear.

Behind him, the fight for the remaining podium places went right down to the wire. Leo Joyon took second after edging Thibault Giraudon by just 0.01 seconds. Manuel Verzeroli finished fourth, with Romain Dagna completing the top five.

**Scardina takes Youth win**

The Putoline Oil Youth Enduro class once again delivered one of the closest battles of the day.

Pietro Scardina built up a lead of almost 20 seconds before the final lap, although Simone Cagnoni began clawing time back in the closing tests.

Scardina held on to take the win by six seconds over Cagnoni, while Gabin Allemand claimed his first podium of the season in third, just 0.66 seconds ahead of Valentin Mersin. Riccardo Pasquato rounded out the top five.

The 2026 Paulo Duarte FIM EnduroGP World Championship concludes in Wales on August 7-9, but Garcia will head to the season finale with the biggest prizes already secured: 2026 EnduroGP and Enduro1 World Champion.

The remaining titles, with the exception of Expotrade Women’s Enduro, already secured by American rider Rachel Gutish at the second Portuguese GP, will all be decided at the final round, the Forsitservices GP of Wales.

**Quer makes it two in Open 2 Strokes**

Jordi Quer (KTM) made it back-to-back wins in Open 2 Strokes on Day 2, this time finishing ahead of fellow Spaniard Jordi Galera (Rieju). Poland’s Kacper Baklarz (GASGAS) completed the podium in third.

In Open 4 Strokes, French rider Alex Pichaud (Beta) took the win ahead of Saturday’s winner Antoine Faveyrial (KTM), while Israeli Inbar Selinger, riding for the WP Èric Augé team, claimed third.

Juha Puotsaari (Husqvarna) was again the rider to beat in Open Senior, leading an all-Finnish podium ahead of Arto Kallioinen (KTM) and Janne Mukkala (KTM).
