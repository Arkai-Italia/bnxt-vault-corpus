# Honda Montesa 4Ride returns for 2027 - full specs and price revealed

Published on September 22nd, 2026 by Nye Davis

#### **Honda Montesa has lifted the lid on its 2027 4Ride model, and essentially, the recipe remains the same as it did last year – if it ain’t broke and all that...**

The big H has given the 4Ride’s two thoroughbred trials siblings (the Cota 301RR and Cota 4RT 260R) fresh graphics for the new year, but the firm’s resident do-everything play bike stays exactly as it did last year, after tweaks were made to bring it up to scratch with the latest Euro5+ emissions regs.

The 4Ride retains its somewhat unique angle as a dick-about-in-the-woods machine – a tad more trialsey than something like, say, a Beta XTrainer, but with greater range and flexibility than an outright trials scalpel. It's a market that is increasingly becoming the domain of leccy bikes, so kudos to Honda for carrying the ICE torch here.

As with the 2026 bike, you get a 258.9cc four-stroke motor to exploit, fed by a 4.3-litre fuel tank (that’s more than double the size of the unit fitted to its 260R stablemate), plus a bunch of hardware shared with the trials-specific bikes in the range, including the aluminium swingarm and silencer.

Suspension-wise, the 4Ride deploys the same rear damper set-up as the aforementioned Cota 260R, delivering 170mm of travel. Upfront, the forks supplied offer a little extra bounce, with the 39mm adjustable Tech units good for 190mm of travel.

Again, obviously not ideal for your local time card event, but decent enough if your riding is particularly slow and snotty (check out Toni Bou in such conditions in the pics) – or you simply don’t fancy the extra heft of a full-sized enduro bike. And speaking of which – weight, or lack thereof, is arguably the 4Ride’s main trump card – it tips the scales at just 82kg.

Pricing for the 2027 machine comes in at £7099 in the UK. At the time of writing, Honda continues to offer last year’s identical model for £100 cheaper.

All three models in the family continue to be built at Montesa Honda’s factory in Santa Perpètua de Mogoda, Barcelona.
