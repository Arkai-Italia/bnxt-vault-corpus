# Honda celebrates 50 years of Finke Desert Race with tricked-up CRF450RX for customers Down Under

Published on August 17th, 2026 by Nye Davis

#### **Australia's Finke Desert Race turned 50 this year and to celebrate, the big H is offering a special-edition version of its CRF450RX complete with a handful of parts proven in the two-day event – and its exclusively available to Aussie customers.**

Held annually in mid-June over the King’s Birthday long weekend, the Finke runs from Alice Springs to the Finke River and back across two days, tackling the varied terrain of Australia’s Northern Territory and producing some wild side-on tracking shots in the process!

For national riders, Finke has long been an important proving ground for aspiring rallyists. Its roll of honour includes several who have gone on to compete on the world stage, with Toby Price perhaps the most famous example. The former KTM factory rider won Finke on two wheels before becoming the first competitor to do the double and also take victory on four.

Honda has enjoyed plenty of success at the event, too – hence this trick special-edition build. Most recently, David Walsh claimed the 2026 motorcycle-class win aboard a Honda-badged 450, giving the brand its 21st win in the bike category across Finke’s 50-year history.

The new CRF450RX Finke Edition is based on Honda’s standard cross-country model but adds a smattering of trick bits. The most obvious change is a 12-litre IMS fuel tank, providing roughly twice the capacity of the standard machine. There’s also an HGS stainless-steel exhaust system, along with a special graphics kit and seat cover that commemorate the race’s half-century, rounding out the package.

Honda says the Finke Edition comes with more than AUD$3500 worth of upgrades, while the complete bike is priced at AUD$16,399 ride-away. That compares with AUD$15,099 for the standard CRF450RX, meaning the special-edition costs AUD$1300 more.

For more info, click [here](https://motorcycles.honda.com.au/models/offroad/competition/crf450rx).
