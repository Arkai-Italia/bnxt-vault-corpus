# Tread - Overland Edition Tread XL - Overland Edition Owner's Manual - Adjusting Warning Angles

# Adjusting Warning Angles

You can adjust the angles at which the device activates a pitch or roll alert. You are responsible for knowing and setting the correct pitch and roll angle for your vehicle.

1. From the main menu, swipe to the left and select
   Pitch and Roll >  > Warning Angles.
2. Swipe up or down to adjust the pitch or roll warning angles.
3. Select
   Accept.
