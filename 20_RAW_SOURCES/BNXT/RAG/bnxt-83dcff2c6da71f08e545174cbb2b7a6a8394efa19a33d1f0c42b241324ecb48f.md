# GBB air conditioning blower motor and module

#### With efficient BLDC motor for air conditioning of vehicle interiors

The GBB is a brushless blower motor/module. Smart motor topology enables a compact design which significantly reduces weight and axial length and increases power density. EC technology provides increased lifetime of up to 15,000 hours and outstanding efficiency of up to 80%. With a customized product portfolio, we can serve motor only, module, or blower applications. Thus, the system provides a flexible solution to meet various customer requirements. With enhanced features including improved EMC (electro-magnetic compatibility) and NVH (noise, vibration, and harshness) performance, the GBB provides an engineered solution for increased climate comfort.

## Compact EC blower with scalable motor performance

**Motor**

The blower motor has a 12/10 topology and is equipped with ferrite magnets. The product is designed to cover a wide performance range of up to 300 W with high efficiency. By using a robust bearing system, our product ensures long lifetime to the end user. The GBB can communicate both via LIN and PWM controls.

**Module**

It comprises the motor and a customer-specific adapter with which it can be mounted into most known HVAC units.Due to its compact design and changeable connector position and type, it can be easily fitted into the customers’ HVAC units.

**Blower**

The GBB can be equipped both with Bosch standard and customer-specific fan wheels. The modular design allows the wheel to be mounted at the customer’s location.Due to its superior thermal behavior, high air flow efficiency can be achieved in the HVAC system.
