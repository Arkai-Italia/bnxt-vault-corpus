# Tread Owner's Manual - Hands-Free Calling

# Hands-Free Calling

NOTE:
While most phones and headsets are supported and can be used, a particular phone or headset cannot be guaranteed to be compatible. All features may not be available for your phone.

Using
Bluetooth® wireless technology, your device can connect to your compatible mobile phone and wireless headset or helmet to become a hands-free device. To check compatibility, go to
[www.garmin.com/bluetooth](http://www.garmin.com/bluetooth).
