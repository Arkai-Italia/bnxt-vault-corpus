# Laconia Rally Set to Stun June 11-19

* by [Joy Burgess](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/joybelle98/)
* May 17, 2022
* 0 Comments
* 2144 Views

May 17, 2022

The motorcycle rally in Laconia, N.H., has been a fixture for a century, as it celebrates its 100th anniversary next year. (The 1959 gathering, shown here, was extra-special from what we hear…) But there’s plenty happening at the 99th edition this June 11-19, and you should definitely plan to be there.

Besides being one of the AMA Gypsy Tour stops for 2022, the nine-day event will feature group rides, live music, vintage and flat track racing, hill climbing competition, demo rides, plenty of cool vendors, Lake Winnipesaukee rides, skill contests, bike shows and much more. There’s a reason this gathering has lasted so long.

There’s a lot going on during June, with the nearby Americade Rally happening the week before Laconia. But if you can get you and your motorcycle to Central New Hampshire’s lake district in June, you’ll not be disappointed.

Check [*laconiamcweek.com*](http://laconiamcweek.com) for more information.
