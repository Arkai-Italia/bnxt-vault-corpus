## Tips for testing and storing batteries

### Check batteries

* Measure voltage with voltmeter (e.g. Bosch BAT 131 / BAT 115)
* Charge at a voltage below 12.4 V
* Avoid deep discharge (prevents sulphation and damage)
* Below 10.5 V = defect, replace battery
* Only carry out a standard battery test after at least 4 hours of rest after charging

### Storing batteries

Store batteries in a dry, cool place (approx. 15 °C) and out of direct sunlight. High temperatures accelerate self-discharge and ageing.

**To do:**

* Check voltage every 2 months
* Charge with OCV below 12.4 V
* Observe "First in – First out" during storage
