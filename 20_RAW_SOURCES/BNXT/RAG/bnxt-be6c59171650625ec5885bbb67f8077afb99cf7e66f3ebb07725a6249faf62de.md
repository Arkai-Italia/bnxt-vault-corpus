# KTM and Husqvarna expand electric ranges with fresh junior models

Published on October 8th, 2026 by Enduro21 Team

#### **KTM and sister company Husqvarna have expanded their kids bikes offering, slotting a new electric model into their respective electric junior line-ups, stretching them to four models each.**

Sitting between the existing SX-E 2 and SX-E 3, the new KTM SX-E 2.12 is joined by the near enough identical, but considerably less orange Husqvarna EE 2.12, offering young riders a stepping stone before moving up to the larger machines.

Both bikes use a 1.8kW maintenance-free hub motor built into the rear wheel, doing away with the need for a chain and sprockets. Fifty Nm of maximum torque is said to be on tap, and both machines feature three ride modes with different top speeds to allow youngsters to progress incrementally without going full gas right from the off.

The 2.12 models are based around the SX-E 2 but get a larger 12-inch front wheel, extended suspension and enlarged chassis. Seat height sits 66mm higher than the SX-E 2 at 566mm, though this can be dropped down to a less lofty 536mm for shorter legged folk.

Suspension comes from WP, with the KTM using a 90mm-travel hydraulic fork and 70mm rear shock. Both bikes also feature a rollover sensor and wrist lanyard to cut the motor in the event of a tumble.

Power comes from a removable 9.4Ah lithium-ion battery, with an external 500W charger capable of a full charge in around 60 minutes.

The 2027 KTM SX-E 2.12 costs £2275.20, while the Husqvarna EE 2.12 is priced at £2549 in the UK. Both are now available through their respective authorised dealer networks.
