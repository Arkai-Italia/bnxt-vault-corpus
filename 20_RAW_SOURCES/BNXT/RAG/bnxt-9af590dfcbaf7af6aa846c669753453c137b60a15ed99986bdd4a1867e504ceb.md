# Red Bull Romaniacs camp

The **Red Bull Romaniacs Camp**, hosted by **[Mosko Moto](https://moskomoto.eu/)**, returns in **2027.**

****

Designed for both Adventure and Enduro competitors, The Camp is much more than just a place to sleep. It is a rider-focused basecamp where participants can recover after a long day on the race tracks, connect with fellow competitors, share stories around the campfire, and prepare for the next stage of the race.

**The Location**

The Camp includes a **dedicated campervan and tent camping area.**

Friends and family accompanying competitors are welcome. **Every person staying at the Camp will needs to purchase a Camp entry Ticket.**

**What You Can Expect**

* Bivouac-style camping for competitors and their support teams
* Dedicated campervan and tent camping area
* Friends and family accompanying competitors are welcome — every person needs to purchase s Camp entry Ticket
* Large stretch tents providing shade and shelter from rain
* Workshop tent equipped with essential tools and Rabaconda tire-changing tools
* Toilets and showers on site
* Evening campfires to relax and connect with other riders
* A welcoming atmosphere and helping hands whenever needed

**Important Information**

* Sleeping equipment (tent, mattress, sleeping bag, etc.) **is not provided** and must be brought by participants.
* No food services will be available on site, so please plan accordingly.
* The Camp is intended for competitors, their teams, friends, and family.
* Every person staying at The Camp must have a valid Camp Ticket.
* This is not a spectator campground or a party zone.

**More Than a Campsite**

The Camp is a place where Adventure and Enduro riders come together to experience the unique Red Bull Romaniacs spirit long after the engines stop. Whether you're maintaining your bike, exchanging stories from the day's ride, or simply enjoying the atmosphere around the campfire, The Camp is where the community comes together.
