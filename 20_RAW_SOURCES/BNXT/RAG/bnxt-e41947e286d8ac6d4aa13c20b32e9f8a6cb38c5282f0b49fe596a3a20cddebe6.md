# Supermoto Range – Betamotor S.p.A.

# supermoto

## From the city to the track, always with Beta

Beta Supermotos are designed for those who want to move fast (and with style) between traffic lights, roundabouts and curves. From the brilliant 50 2-stroke to the efficient 125 4-stroke, these bikes are light, intuitive and fun, with all the character of Beta. Aggressive geometry, 17″ wheels and a factory look: from leaving school to your first lap on the track, you’re always ready to open the throttle.

[Find a dealer](https://www.betamotor.com/en/dealers/)

design

#### MOTARD LOOK, 100% BETA

Modern lines, slim plastics and eye-catching graphics. Attention to detail like on racing bikes, with monochrome X Special Edition versions: impossible to go unnoticed.

ERGONOMICS AND VEHICLE

#### AGILITY AND CONTROL

Compact frames and slim geometry for quick steering and fun on roundabouts and bends. Road tyres for true motard grip, extended swingarm for more stability at speed. Suspension and brakes (Ø260/Ø220) give you the control you need, in the city and on the track.

ENGINE

#### THE STRENGTH YOU WANT, ALWAYS

50 2-stroke: lightweight, responsive, with precise 6-speed gearbox and oil mixer. 125 4-stroke: choose between the smoothness of the new T (low fuel consumption, super easy) and the personality of the R (Minarelli engine, premium, stronger character).

FUNCTIONALITY AND TECHNOLOGY

#### SMART EVERY DAY

New digital instrument panel, single lock for everything, LED headlight for greater visibility (only on 125 4T). The 125 4T models are also equipped with CBS combined braking developed by Beta: carefree safety, especially in the city.
