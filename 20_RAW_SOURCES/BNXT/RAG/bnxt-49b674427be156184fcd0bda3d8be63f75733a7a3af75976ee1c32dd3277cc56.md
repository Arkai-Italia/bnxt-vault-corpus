# Estenson Racing to Serve as Ring Sponsor for the 2026 AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony

* by [AMA Tech](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/amatechama-cycle-org/)
* September 9, 2026
* 0 Comments
* 4163 Views

PICKERINGTON, Ohio (Sept. 9, 2026) **—** Estenson Racing — one of the most respected racing teams in American Flat Track — will serve as the Ring Sponsor for the 2026 AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony presented by Yamaha Motor Corporation. The event will take place Thursday, Sept. 10, in Pickerington, Ohio.

Each year’s inductees receive an engraved ring commemorating their induction — a lasting symbol of their place among motorcycling’s most accomplished figures.

“Every new class of Hall of Fame inductees is presented with engraved rings as a special reminder of their status as Hall of Famers,” AMA Business Development Manager Michael Kula said. “We are so grateful to Estenson Racing for coming aboard to support this effort and helping make this year’s induction ceremony one to remember.”

Founded in 2016 by Tim Estenson, a lifelong supporter of flat track racing, Estenson Racing has grown into one of the sport’s top teams, fielding competitive riders on the American Flat Track circuit and building a reputation for professionalism both on and off the track. For more information on Estenson Racing, visit [*estensonracing.com/*](https://estensonracing.com/)*.*

Six outstanding motorcyclists will be celebrated during the 2026 AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony, with Russell Bobbitt, Damon Bradshaw, Eraldo Ferracci, Jake Johnson, Gary Sellers and Dale Walker set to be inducted during the event. Broc Glover — a 2000 inductee into the AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame — will also be recognized as an AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Legend.

Tickets to the AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony are on sale now at [*MotorcycleMuseum.org/events-experiences/induction-ceremony/*](https://motorcyclemuseum.org/events-experiences/induction-ceremony/).
