## ***Italian brand releases Seiemmezzo STR Naked Street Bike in U.S.***

July 14, 2023

By Keaton Maisano

Legendary Italian motorcycle maker Moto Morini is bringing a slice of Italy to the United States after announcing the release of its Seiemmezzo STR Naked Street Bike.

Moto Morini, which launched in 1937, designed the STR to be a blend of style, comfort and premium features. Available in Metallic Red, Vivid White and Anthracite Smoke at authorized Moto Morini USA dealers, the bike boasts a 649cc parallel twin engine and starts at an MSRP of $7,499.

“We are excited to bring the Moto Morini brand to the United States,” Moto Morini COO Chris McGee said in a release. “The STR is just one example that reflects the Moto Morini mantra of delivering performance, quality and exceptional craftsmanship. It’s a great looking machine that’s super fun to ride.”

Along with the STR, Moto Morini is offering the Seiemmezzo SCR Scrambler and the X-Cape Adventouring models.

For more information, visit [*MotoMoriniUSA.com*](https://www.motomoriniusa.com/).
