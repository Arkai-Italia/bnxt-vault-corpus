# Transferring GPX Files From Your Computer

Apple® computers may require additional third-party software, such as Android File Transfer, to read and write files on this device.

If you have already created routes that you want to use on your device, you can transfer GPX files directly from your computer.

1. Connect the device to your computer ([Connecting the Device to Your Computer](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-157A2412-594F-45D8-A5EE-5A5327DA0914/EN-US/GUID-E5BDCF7D-A93B-4007-BDB6-74EE969EBED5.html)).

   The device appears as a portable device.
2. On your computer, open the file browser.
3. Select a GPX file.
4. Select
   Edit > Copy.
5. Browse to the GPX folder on the device.
6. Select
   Edit > Paste.
