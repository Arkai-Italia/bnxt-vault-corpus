## WHAT IS ILLYRIA RAID?

- - -

​"ILLYRIA RAID" is the the biggest Off-Road Raid in Europe

and it crosses multiple in seven days in an "NON COMPETITIVE" event.

- - -

2027 will be the 11-th Year of Illyria Raid and  the Orga Team decided again to bring in the table new excitement to go and try new countries and new routes....

- - -

2027 Edition will start from Bucharest in Romania 

and will finish in Thessaloniki in Greece.

- - -

An special opportunity to ride on the most beautiful and undiscovered 

landscapes of Romania, Bulgaria, Greece, North Macedonia and Albania.

- - -

It will be a unique experience in the remote mountains, forests, beaches, 

valleys and more; in an seven days "escape" from everyday reality.

- - -

The daily route is between 220-400 km, 75% of them off-road.

Every day in a different city, where the group will be 

accommodated in the best hotels in town.

- - -

The route can will be driven

with GPS Garmin and/or Smartphones and Tablets.

- - -

Illyria Raid is the best opportunity for Adventure Tourism 

and of course a very intense and pleasant vacation time.

- - -

The route is best suitable for Enduro Moto and also for Heavy Enduro

and consists of everything; from stones, mud, dust, river crossing, forest, grass, valleys and going from the sea level to highest mountains.

- - -​​

## SUPPORTAND ASSISTANCE

- During the riding days will be two Medical Vehicles,

two Assistance Vehicles on track and one Sweeper Vehicle.

 

- Double Satellite Tracking System 24 hours a day

for control and safety of participants.

 

- Pick up vehicles in the track for mechanical assistance

and transporting damaged vehicles to the next stop point.

 

- Four Doctors in the Orga Team for fast medical assistance.

 

- Road ambulance in stand by mode if needed to go in hospital.

 

- One Truck for transport of luggages and spare parts from hotel to hotel,

(Maximum per rider is two pieces, max 30 kg per piece, box or luggage).

 

- Safety closed and guarded space on bivouac.

 

- Preparation of parking place before the group arrives.

 

- Three mechanics and specialized service every night in the hotel,

(personal extra cost on request).

 

- Changing tires, mousse, tube (extra cost)

 

- Tire availability in mechanical service tent.​​
