Sonora Tower Honda Bracket Install
             Notes

    Congratulations on choosing the lightest, strongest
             tower available for your build!

 Installing your Sonora is very simple, and can be done by anyone with basic
 mechanical ability. The Sonora is designed to be a universal fit tower. Every
 bike is a little bit different, and we can’t foresee all of the details of your
 particular install, so the notes below are intended as suggestions to help
            you make good decisions in fitting it to your bike.

    Anymodificationsthatyoumaketoyourbikeareatyourownrisk.

               Give us a call (01-604-374-5943) or send us an email
     (store@rallymotoshop.com) if you have any questions, or if there is any
                          way we can help.

              Tools and Supplies Recommended:

   • 6mm Allen
   •  Ft/lbs or nm torque wrench with 6mm Allen bit
   • Red and Blue Locktite (or equivalent)
   •  General tools for servicing your motorcycle

Installation Tips:

  •  Remove the headlight mask and associated parts and secure the wiring
    out of the way.

  •  Position the Sonora Billet Mount on the frame bosses on the front of
    the motorcycle headstock, and drill to a depth of 20mm using the
    supplied drill bit.

## Pagina 3

•  Tap both holes using the supplied tap

  •  Insert HeliCoil using provided tool following included Helicoil
    Instructions. Note: Red Loctite can be used to bond the insert in place.

  •  Install the Sonora Billet mount using the included M10 bolts using
    blue Loctite. Tighten to 20nm.

  •  The Sonora mounts onto its bracket using the steel mounting bolts, the
    nylon breakaway bolts, or a combination of the two. We generally
   recommend a steel bolt on the lower hole and a nylon one on the top.

  • Most customers remove the stock odometer and ignition from their
     bikes, but if you want to keep the odo, we offer a Honda Odo mount for
   our Modular Roadbook Bracket.

  •  Ensure that all wiring is secured and protected from chafe through
    the full swing of the handlebars.
