## MEDIA ACCREDITATIONS

Welcome, and thank you for your interest in covering **FIM SuperEnduro World Championship 2025/2026**.

There are two access levels for media – **international** and **local**.

#### INTERNATIONAL

---

If the media you cover have an international reach and are published in English, please complete the form (click the button **MEDIA APPLICATION** below) so we can learn more about your coverage request.

If we accept your media application you will be informed how to collect your media accreditation a week before the event you have applied to.

For last minute application please contact media.pl@sportupagencja.pl.
