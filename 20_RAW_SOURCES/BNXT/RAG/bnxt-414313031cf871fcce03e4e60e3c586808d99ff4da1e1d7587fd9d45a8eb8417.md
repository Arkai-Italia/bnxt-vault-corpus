# Tread Owner's Manual - LED Controls

# LED Controls

The LED controls allow you to control the LED lights on your connected
Tread® 6.5" XS-LED Tower Speakers and optional, addressable LEDs. You can set the color of the LEDs, sync the LEDs to your music or the speed of your vehicle, and display a call sign color during a group ride.
