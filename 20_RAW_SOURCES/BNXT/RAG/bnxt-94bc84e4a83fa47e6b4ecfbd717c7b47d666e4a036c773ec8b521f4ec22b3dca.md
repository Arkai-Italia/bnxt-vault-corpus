### HD DIGITAL ELEVATION MAP

A highly detailed terrain map gives you the most realistic view of what lies ahead through elevation profiles.

### STANDARD DIGITAL ELEVATION MAP

Use the standard detail terrain map to navigate or locate your preferred terrain.

### CONTOUR LINES

Contour lines with elevation labels show the topography of the land to help guide your expedition and find the best ascents and descents.

### NORTH AMERICAN ADVENTURE ROADS AND TRAILS

Get detailed trail networks containing OpenStreetMap™ data and U.S. Forest Service data organized by moto, vehicles less than 50 inches wide, full-size off-road vehicles or all vehicles.

### CITY NAVIGATOR® ROADS, HIGHWAYS AND INTERSTATES

Experience the most detailed street maps available so you can navigate with clear, turn-by-turn directions to a specified address or intersection.

### PUBLIC LAND BOUNDARIES

Get public land boundary maps for areas such as Bureau of Land Management, wilderness, army corps of engineers land, national forests and more.

### PRIVATE LAND BOUNDARIES AND LANDOWNER INFORMATION

Know before you go by checking for private land boundaries with landowner information for parcels greater than 4 acres.

### POPULAR MOTORCYCLE ROADS

Find and ride popular motorcycle routes with a heatmap layer showing you where other motorcyclists like to ride.

### OFF-ROAD RIDING AREAS WITH DIFFICULTY RATINGS

Detailed maps for popular off-road areas and private off-road parks with difficulty ratings ([where available](https://support.garmin.com/?faq=XjVMEXhueq9MCqc19RVMx9&_gl=1%2ay4dfkt%2a_ga%2aNTA1NzkzNjc3LjE2MTA2NTM1OTk.%2a_ga_K5X2MQK80V%2aMTY2NTY3OTk5NS4zMjAuMC4xNjY1Njc5OTk1LjAuMC4w)) help you find the best places to ride.

### SNOWMOBILE TRAILS

View snowmobile trail content for select areas within the U.S. and in Canadian provinces. This includes snowmobile-accessible locations such as fuel stops, warming shelters, restaurants and more.

### SATELLITE IMAGERY

Enjoy high-resolution satellite imagery showing vivid aerial views to make it easy to find paths through forest, prairie or desert.

### ULTIMATE CAMPGROUNDS (45K+ U.S. AND CANADA)

Experience details for more than 45,000 public state and national campgrounds across the U.S. and Canada to make finding a stopover spot easier.

### SLOPE ASPECT

Colors depict the direction of the slope face so a rider can stay aware of avalanche-prone slope faces. Slopes can become dangerous from directional wind and sun exposure. Available in [select regions](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/p/893561/#additional) with purchase of an [Outdoor Maps+](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/p/893561) plan.

### AVALANCHE RISK REPORT

Access avalanche reports directly on your compatible device or in the Tread® app. Available in select regions with purchase of an [Outdoor Maps+](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/p/893561) plan.

### SLOPE ANGLE

Colors depict the severity of a slope so a rider can easily see slopes that could be prone to an avalanche. Available in [select regions](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/p/893561/#additional) with purchase of an [Outdoor Maps+](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/p/893561) plan.
