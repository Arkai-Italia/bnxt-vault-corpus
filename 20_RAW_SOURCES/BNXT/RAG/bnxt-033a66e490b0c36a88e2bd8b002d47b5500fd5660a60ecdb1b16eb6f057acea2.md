# Use your AMA $3 discount for International Motorcycle Show tickets

* by [Jim Witters](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/jwittersama-cycle-org/)
* November 4, 2019
* 0 Comments
* 4617 Views

Be sure to catch one of the Progressive International Motorcycle shows around the country and use your AMA discount to shake off the winter blahs.

As an AMA member you get $3 off an advanced adult one-day general admission.

Just use your AMA promo code AMA19 when you buy your tickets online at [www.motorcycleshows.com](https://www.motorcycleshows.com).

Progressive, Cycle Gear, and J&P Cycles are major sponsors of the tour, which gives motorcycle enthusiasts and consumers an opportunity to check out the latest models coming to market as well as brand-new accessories, merchandise, and more.

Committed to getting new riders on two wheels, IMS debuted Discover the Ride, a forward-thinking initiative designed to introduce riding motorcycles to consumers of all ages in a safe and controlled environment, on the 2018/2019 tour.

“IMS’ engaging approach to increasing new ridership is exciting and we are thrilled to continue our title sponsorship of this industry leading event for another five years,” says Eric Doubler, Progressive recreational vehicle business leader. “The show provides a fantastic platform for our brand to connect with both new and seasoned riders, and each year we look forward to presenting motorcycle enthusiasts with a can’t be missed experience, deeply-rooted in motorcycle culture.”

Returning to the 2019/2020 tour is the motorcycle builder competition called the Ultimate Builder Custom Bike Show, which is sponsored by J&P Cycles.

Also returning this year is the Marketplace presented by Cycle Gear, an interactive retail space allowing consumers to touch, test, and buy the latest gear, parts, and accessories from key aftermarket brands with specialists on hand to educate, introduce products, and answer questions to any gear needs.

The shows are scheduled for:

* Nov. 22-24: Long Beach, Calif., Long Beach Convention Center
* Dec. 6-8: New York, Javits Center
* Jan. 3-5: Dallas, Kay Bailey Hutchison Convention Center
* Jan. 10-12: Washington, D.C., Walter E. Washington Convention Center
* Jan.17-19: Denver, Colorado Convention Center
* Jan. 24-26: Cleveland, I-X Center
* Jan 31-Feb. 2: Minneapolis, Minneapolis Convention Center
* Feb. 7-9: Chicago, Donald E. Stephens Convention Center

To learn more about the Progressive International Motorcycle Shows, go to [www.motorcycleshows.com.](https://www.motorcycleshows.com)
