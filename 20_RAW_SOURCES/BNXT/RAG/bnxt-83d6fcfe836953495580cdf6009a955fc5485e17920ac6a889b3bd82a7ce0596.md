# Act now to bid on more than 500 motorcycles

* by [Jim Witters](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/jwittersama-cycle-org/)
* September 30, 2019
* 0 Comments
* 3471 Views

## The online bidding closes Oct. 2

Whether you want to restore a 1975 Yamaha RD350 or need parts for your 1981 Kawasaki KZ 550 LTD, you should take a look at the K-Bid.com online auction that features more than 500 motorcycles.

But if something in the auction grabs your interest you’d better act fast. The online bidding ends Wednesday, Oct. 2, 2019.

More than 500 motorcycles, dirt bikes and scooters are being sold out of Clarks Grove, Minn. There are Hondas, Yamahas, Kawasakis, Harley-Davidsons, Suzukis and more from the 1960s into the 1990s. The bikes belong to Tony Joslyn, who has been selling motorcycle parts through eBay and other aenues for about 25 years.

About 100 of the machines have titles.

In a news release, Joslyn says: “Now it’s time to clear the herd and slow down. Once it starts to feel like a job, you lose some of the joy. I’m looking forward to getting back to working (on) a few motorcycles now and then.”

“It’s hard to pick a favorite out of the collection as I am partial to all of them,” he said, “but I’ve always enjoyed the Kawasaki three-cylinders.”

The auction is live at [www.k-bid.com/auction/29939](http://www.k-bid.com/auction/29939).

This auction is cooperatively managed by A2C Auctions and JMS Auctions on [www.K-BID.com](http://www.K-BID.com).

K-BID.com is an online auction venue headquartered in Maple Plain, Minn. Each auction on K-BID.com is contracted and managed by independent auction companies.
