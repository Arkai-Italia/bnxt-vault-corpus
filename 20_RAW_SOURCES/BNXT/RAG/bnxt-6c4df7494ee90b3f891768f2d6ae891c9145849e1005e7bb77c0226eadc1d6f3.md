# Tread Owner's Manual - Vehicle Information

# Vehicle Information

You can use the vehicle information page to view the status of your vehicle. For example, you can view the vehicle's speed, temperature, and fuel gauge. You can also view the pitch and roll of your vehicle, the battery voltage, and the direction your vehicle is facing.

You can select
 to view the vehicle information page at any time.
