# Tread - SXS Edition Owner's Manual - Avoiding Traffic Delays on Your Route

# Avoiding Traffic Delays on Your Route

Before you can avoid traffic delays, you must be receiving traffic information.

By default, the device optimizes your route to avoid traffic delays automatically. If you have disabled this option in the traffic settings ([Traffic Settings](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-EFC7A885-2733-4E2F-9923-624CD4CD6429.html)), you can view and avoid traffic delays manually.

1. While navigating a route, select
    > Traffic.
2. Select
   Alternate Route, if available.
3. Select
   Go!.
