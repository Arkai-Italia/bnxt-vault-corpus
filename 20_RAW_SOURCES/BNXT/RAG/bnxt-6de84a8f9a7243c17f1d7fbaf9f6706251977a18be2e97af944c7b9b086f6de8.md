## Operation principle of the occupant protection system

The occupant protection electronics optimizes the protection of vehicle occupants against injuries in collisions, as well as in case the vehicle rolls over. The occupant protection system from Bosch consists of peripheral sensors and an intelligent centerpiece – the airbag control unit. Based on the sensor signals, it detects the strength and direction of a collision and can activate the restraint mechanisms in the vehicle, for example seat-belt pretensioners and airbags, as required for maximum occupant protection.

With increasing electrification, automation, connectivity and new interior concepts, innovative passive safety systems will continue to play a major role in the future. These megatrends in the automotive industry pose interesting questions and challenges for occupant protection.

The vision of accident-free mobility will always remain in focus. In order to support future needs, in addition to the ESP® sensors the high-precision 6D inertial sensors can also be integrated in the airbag control unit to enable relative vehicle positioning. This is used in automated vehicles if sufficient information about the surroundings is temporarily unavailable or if the satellite connection is interrupted.To minimize fire risks for hybrid and electric vehicles in particular, a battery protection function can be integrated in the airbag control unit. This reliably detects and prevents the risk of short circuits by disconnecting the high-voltage battery in the event of accidents that occur while driving or during charging.
