# 2024 Permco AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days Tickets Now on Sale to AMA Members

* by [Jack Emerson](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/jemersonama-cycle-org/)
* January 4, 2024
* 0 Comments
* 7324 Views

## ***Registration for North America’s largest motorcycle swap meet is also open to previous swap meet vendors***

PICKERINGTON, Ohio (Jan. 4, 2024) — The American Motorcyclist Association is pleased to announce that tickets to the 2024 Permco AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days — which is set to run July 26-28 at the Mid-Ohio Sports Car Course in Lexington, Ohio — are now available for purchase to AMA members.

“We anticipate another fantastic weekend of vintage motorcycling fun for all those in attendance at 2024 Permco AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days,” AMA Director of Marketing and Communications Joy Burgess said. “As is always the case, VMD will be chock full of vintage motorcycling activities for everyone, and we cannot wait to celebrate our 100th anniversary with the riders who have helped our organization thrive for a century.”

The 2024 edition of Permco AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days will carry special significance, with the AMA ringing in its centennial celebration during the event alongside countless activities for vintage motorcycle enthusiasts. With amateur vintage racing in multiple disciplines, North America’s largest motorcycle swap meet, demo rides, vendor displays and stunt shows, among numerous other vintage motorcycling happenings, the event will have something for riders of all ages.

AMA members can purchase discounted tickets and secure tent camping spots through the AMA at *[VintageMotorcycleDays.com](https://vintagemotorcycledays.com/tickets/)*. Tent camping is for non-reserved, non-self-contained units only and spaces are first-come, first-serve. RV passes are required for an RV or self-contained unit and will go on sale, alongside non-AMA member tickets, today (Jan. 4) at [*www.midohio.com*](http://www.midohio.com)*.*

[Swap meet booth registration](http://services.americanmotorcyclist.com/vmd_swap_meet/) is also now available to previous swap meet vendors.

Race registration and sign-up information for additional activities, including the AMA Hall of Fame bike show, Ashland Dinner Ride and AMA Life Member Breakfast, will be available soon.

Proceeds from Permco AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days are donated to the American Motorcycle Heritage Foundation, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization that financially supports the AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame and its mission to preserve the history of motorcycling.

There are still several exciting announcements regarding 2024 Permco AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days coming in the next few months, so stay engaged with [*vintagemotorcycledays.com*](https://vintagemotorcycledays.com/) and follow the VMD social media channels on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/amavintagemotorcycledays/) and [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/AMAVintageMotorcycleDays) for up-to-date information on the event.
