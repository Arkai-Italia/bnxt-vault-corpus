# TC 85 19/16 | 2027 - Step up and stand out | Husqvarna Indonesia

### 01. Performance

#### Engine

Continuing to utilise the latest 2-stroke technology, the engine remains well ahead of its 85 cc competition. Overall performance can be customised depending on rider preference or track conditions too, as the TC 85 benefits from two engine maps. A close ratio six-speed gearbox and hydraulic clutch completing the build of this highly competitive machine.

#### Cylinder

A light and durable aluminium cylinder is designed around an innovative powervalve system that ensures unrivalled performance. The cylinder head is designed for strong bottom end to mid-range power, which reduces the need for riders to feather the clutch in heavy track conditions.

#### Crankcase

Manufactured using a high-pressure die-cast process, the crankcases feature thinner walls for a low weight while being strong and durable. This design allows for a more compact engine arrangement that brings the internal components closer to the centre of gravity to enhance handling.

#### Clutch

The reliable, lightweight hydraulic system from Miba ensures consistent play, even in the toughest of conditions, and offers a near maintenance-free operation. The clutch itself is housed inside a strong steel clutch basket for consistent action and assured durability.
