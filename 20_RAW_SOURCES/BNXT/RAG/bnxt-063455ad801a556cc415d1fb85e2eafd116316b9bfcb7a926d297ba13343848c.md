# WEBSITE PRIVACY STATEMENT

Last updated on 22 April 2026

## Introduction

Amaury Sport Organisation (hereinafter referred to as either “A.S.O.”, or “We”) is aware that You, as a User, i.e. a visitor to the Dakar official website <u>www.dakar.com</u> (hereinafter referred to as the “Website”), are concerned by how Your personal data are processed and recognises the importance of protecting Your privacy. This is why we, as the Controller of Your personal data, make every effort to protect Your personal data and respect Your privacy.

When You browse the Dakar Website or subscribe to a newsletter to be informed about any topics of the Dakar, or when You follow the Dakar on social media, or You share a video, You may, if you wish, authorise A.S.O. and/or our partners to collect and process a certain amount of personal data.

We explain in this short “Privacy Statement” why and how We collect and process Your data, if you consent to such collection and processing, with whom We are likely to share it and, in particular, Your rights in relation to the protection of personal data and how You can exercise them.

The French version of the Privacy statement supersedes any translation.

## I. Which data are processed?

### I.1. When You visit the Website:

Through Our use of analytics and essential (technical) cookies (text files placed on the device that You use to browse the Website) We may collect personal data about You:

- Information about the browser on Your device and the operating system that You are using

- The IP address of the device that You are using

- The webpages that You view

- The links that You click while interacting with Our services

- Further information on this subject, including storage periods, is provided in our Cookies Policy, which can be read HERE: https://www.dakar.com/en/cookies-policy

### I.2. When You follow news about the Dakar

#### I.2.1 When You subscribe to Newsletters

If You wish to be informed about all topics about the Dakar, You must complete a form. To do this, You will be redirected to the website of our processor “Sendinblue”. By completing the newsletter subscription form, You consent to the following information being transmitted to the processor:

- Identification data: title, surname, first name, e-mail address, date of birth, country,

- Language preferences: so that we can send to You the newsletter in Your own language.

### I.2.2 When you view our embedded videos on our website

You can choose whether you want to watch our videos about the current Dakar or about previous editions of the Dakar by clicking on the video embedded on our website, or by going to the Event channel on Dailymotion.

When you view the video on our website, Dailymotion informs you about the deposit of cookies on Your device.  If you want, you can refuse their deposit but, in such cases, You will not be able to watch Dakar videos.

If you want to follow the Dakar channel, you need to log in to your account and Dailymotion will place a cookie on your device.

We have no control over the process employed by any of these websites to collect information relating to your browsing associated with any of your personal data held by such websites. We invite you to read the privacy and cookie policies of these websites to familiarize yourself with the purposes, including advertising, for which they use the navigational information they may collect. Their respective policies should clarify your rights, how you can exercise them, and how to give or withdraw your consent.

# II. Recipients and storage of Your data:

When you enter your data and personal information to subscribe to the Dakar newsletter, we share your information with Sendinblue SAS.

## II.2. Our Sponsors

Your consent will also be requested in order to be able to transfer Your data to Our sponsors. This will enable them to offer You products and/or services in line with Your interests.

Your data will only be stored until You withdraw Your consent. When You unsubscribe and request Us to delete Your account, We and our processors and sponsors will ensure that Your rights are respected.

This Privacy Statement does not apply to these Third-Party Sites. A.S.O. encourages you to carefully read each one of the Privacy Policies of each Third-Party websites or to contact them for more information.

III. What rights do You have and how can You exercise them? II.1 Your rights

Pursuant to the regulations applicable to the protection of Personal data, in particular the French Data Protection Law no. 78/17 as amended by Law no. 2018-493 of 20 June 2018, and Regulation (EU) 2016/679, You have a right of access, a right to rectification, a right to erasure, a right to restrict processing, a right to object to receive our newsletters, and a right to portability of your data, in accordance with articles 15 to 21 of the GDPR.

# II.2 How can You exercise these rights?

You may exercise Your rights by completing this form, which is available HERE: <u>https://www.dakar.com/en/the-competitors/gdpr.</u>

You also have the right to refuse to be subject to automated individual decision-making based on Your data, in accordance with article 22 of the GDPR.

Please note that, in accordance with article 12 of the GDPR, We may  require for some rights requests (portability, right of access or right to object for the processing of Your Data on grounds related to Your particular situation), a copy of a document proving your  identity to be sure that it has not been stolen and that You are the right person requesting for these rights.

Finally, You have the right to submit a complaint to the French Data Protection Authority (CNIL) if You believe that We have failed to respect Your rights.
