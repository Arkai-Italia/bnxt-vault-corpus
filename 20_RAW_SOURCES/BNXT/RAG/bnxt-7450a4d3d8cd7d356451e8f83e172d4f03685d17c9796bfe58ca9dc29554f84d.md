Universal Navigation Clamps

Tips:
   1. Cut round cross-bar to desired length.
   2. Use blue loctite thread locking compound on all screws.
   3. Place Teﬂon tape between handlebars and big part of
    clamp to allow it to move and minimize damage to the
     navigation equipment or rider in big impacts.
   4. To ensure no slippage: Once you have these mounted in
     the position you like, drill a 2-3mm deep hole where the
    clamp set-screws meet the cross-bar.
