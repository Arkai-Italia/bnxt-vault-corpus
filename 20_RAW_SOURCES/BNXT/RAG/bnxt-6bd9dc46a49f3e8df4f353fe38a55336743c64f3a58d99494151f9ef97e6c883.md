# Pro Bike: Josep Garcia Walkaround Tour of his 2026 Red Bull KTM 250 EXC-F

Published on April 9th, 2026 by Enduro21 Team

##### EnduroGP World Champion Josep Garcia gives us a walkaround talkaround tour of his rocketship 2026 KTM 250 EXC-F factory race bike.

The 2026 EnduroGP World Championship begins this weekend in Custonaci, Sicily with Josep aiming at a third consecutive world title onboard the baby 250F.

Highlights include: KTM motocross fuel tank and rear brake lever, new Acerbis handguards replacing the stock parts and a switch to Renthal Fat Bar 821 bend.

Enduro21 caught time with both Josep and his mechanic Ivan at the end of the 2025 season to go into even more detail about what makes this bike so special.
