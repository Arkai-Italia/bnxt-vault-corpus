# Tread - SXS Edition Owner's Manual - Removing the Device from the Mount

# Removing the Device from the Mount

1. Pull up the release tab on the mount.
2. Grip the device by the top and bottom.
3. Pull the bottom edge of the device toward you until it releases from the magnet.

   Pulling the device straight toward you may cause the mount to release from the suction cup.
