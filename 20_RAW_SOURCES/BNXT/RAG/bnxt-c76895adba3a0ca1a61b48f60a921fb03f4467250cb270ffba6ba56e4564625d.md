# Tread - Overland Edition Tread XL - Overland Edition Owner's Manual - Transmitting Your Voice

# Transmitting Your Voice

Before you can transmit your voice, you must pair your
Tread® - Overland Edition device with a compatible
Bluetooth® headset or connect the fist microphone.

You can transmit your voice over the radio using the connected fist microphone or a compatible
Bluetooth headset.

Select an option:

* Hold the push-to-talk button on the side of the connected fist microphone.
* From the main menu, select
   > Adventure > Radio > Push to Talk.
* Use the voice dial feature on your connected
  Bluetooth headset ([Enabling Push-to-Talk with a Headset](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-157A2412-594F-45D8-A5EE-5A5327DA0914/EN-US/GUID-CA7FA289-39DA-4A4F-A8A1-5A9107722AD3.html)).

  TIP:
  You can press the pause or stop button to manually close the radio.

  NOTE:
  You must enable the
  Push to Talk with Headset setting to use this feature ([Radio Settings](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-157A2412-594F-45D8-A5EE-5A5327DA0914/EN-US/GUID-F515806E-DABE-4199-AA92-0DBE035F67B0.html)).
