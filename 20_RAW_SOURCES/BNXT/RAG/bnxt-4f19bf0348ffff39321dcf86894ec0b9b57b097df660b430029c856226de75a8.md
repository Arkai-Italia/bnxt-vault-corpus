## Description

Piezoresistive Pressure Mini Transducer, linear analogue output, amplified, designed for all motorsport applications: brakes, oil, water or fuel; stainless steel body. Ergal junction to fit the sensor on banjo screw available as optional.

Body structure and diaphragm are resistant to standard motorsport fluids.

Viton version is available on request for hermetic applications.

Custom calibrations available on request.
