# eAxle systems

#### Global platform –standardized quality – worldwide availability

Bosch eAxle systems are a compact, cost-effective electric drive solution for battery-electric vehicles and hybrid applications. The electric motor, power electronics, and transmission are combined in a compact drive unit. This reduces the complexity of the electric drive and the powertrain becomes cheaper, more compact, and more efficient.

The global platform for eAxles leverages standardized modules that can be flexibly adapted to local requirements. Automotive manufacturers benefit from faster time to market, reduced costs, and worldwide production locations.

## Operating principle

Bosch eAxle systems can be used in a variety of electric and hybrid vehicles. The modular design and flexible manufacturing approach facilitate custom-tailored solutions in terms of power output, torque, and installation space.

With system voltages of up to 800 V, higher power densities through silicon carbide semiconductor technology, and efficiencies of up to 93% in all relevant driving cycles globally, electric drive solutions are taken to the next level. The benefits are clear: impressive acceleration thanks to high torque, fast charging, and increased range. The eAxle can be used as a secondary drive unit for an extra performance boost. The compact design and the high level of system integration in combination with specific technologies provide a cost-effective overall solution. This makes the eAxle systems the ideal solution for the electric drive in battery-electric vehicles and hybrid applications, combining innovation, economy, and efficiency in one system.

## The 3-in-1 and 6-in-1 solution based on one common platform

X-in-1 eAxle

The eAxle systems combine the power electronics, electric motor, and transmission in a compact system housing and can be expanded to include additional functions. The global eAxle platform relies on standardized modules that ensure worldwide availability with consistent quality, while also offering the necessary flexibility to adapt to local market requirements.

Thanks to this common platform, customers can choose, for example, between the 3-in-1 and 6-in-1 variants depending on their requirements, without compromising on performance or integration.

Manufacturing on the same production line enables optimized and lean production. Thanks to standardized production processes, both variants can be produced on the same line.

Customers benefit from a unified interface and system architecture, which significantly simplifies the integration of the eAxles into existing vehicle architectures as well as future E/E architectures.
