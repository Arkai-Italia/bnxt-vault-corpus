# Synchronizing Garmin Explore Account Data

Before you can synchronize account data, you must connect to the
Tread® app and enable
Garmin Explore™.

You can synchronize data, such as tracks, with your
Garmin Explore account. This can be helpful to keep your data available on each of your compatible devices. Your device periodically synchronizes data with your account automatically. You can also synchronize data manually at any time.
