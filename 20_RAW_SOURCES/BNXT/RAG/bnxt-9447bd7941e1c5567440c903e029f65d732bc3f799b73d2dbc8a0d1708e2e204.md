# FIM EnduroGP announces provisional calendar for 2027

[Igor Aguado](https://www.endurogp.com/author/iaguado/)2026-09-01T11:44:56+02:008 August 2026|

* [View Larger Image ![](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns=%22http://www.w3.org/2000/svg%22%20viewBox=%220%200%202000%201333%22%3E%3C/svg%3E)](https://www.endurogp.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/josep-garcia_EnduroGP-2026-Rnd5-A_17124.jpg)

**Prime Stadium Events, promoters of the Paulo Duarte FIM EnduroGP World Championship, together with the FIM, are pleased to confirm the provisional calendar for the 2027 season.**

Held across seven months, from April to October, the championship will feature eight rounds in Spain, Portugal, Italy, the Isle of Man and Greece.

The six-round Expotrade FIM Women’s Enduro World Championship will be held at rounds two, three, four, six, seven and eight of the 2027 FIM EnduroGP schedule.

Spain, a nation steeped in enduro heritage, will open next season with two rounds in April at the beginning and at the end of the month. Valverde del Camino will be a new location for EnduroGP to visit, while Oliana welcomes EnduroGP back after two successful editions in 2025 and 2026.

Fafe, Portugal returns for back-to-back weekends of racing in June for rounds three and four. Thanks to its expansive terrain, it will offer a unique course and set of special tests for competitors at each round.

Always an important feature on every calendar, the GP of Italy will host round five in Carpineti in July to begin the second half of the season. Renowned for its challenging terrain and passionate enduro fans, it will be one not to be missed

An island steeped in motorcycling history, the Isle of Man will welcome the Paulo Duarte FIM EnduroGP World Championship for the first time ever during the final weekend of July.

Bringing the 2027 season to a close in October, EnduroGP returns to Greece for the first time since 2019. With back-to-back weekends of racing for rounds seven and eight, it’s here where the 2027 world champions will be crowned.

**2027 Paulo Duarte FIM EnduroGP World Championship**

Round 1: GP of Spain — Valverde del Camino — 2-4 April
 Round 2: GP of Spain II — Oliana — 23-25 April
 Round 3: GP of Portugal — Fafe — 11-13 June
 Round 4: GP of Portugal II — Fafe — 18-20 June
 Round 5: GP of Italy — Carpineti — 2-4 July
 Round 6: GP of Isle of Man — Douglas — 30 July – 1 August
 Round 7: GP of Greece — TBA —15-17 October
 Round 8: GP of Greece II — TBA — 22-24 October

**2027 Expotrade FIM Women’s Enduro World Championship**
 Round 1: GP of Spain II — Oliana — 23-25 April
 Round 2: GP of Portugal — Fafe — 11-13 June
 Round 3: GP of Portugal II — Fafe — 18-20 June
 Round 4: GP of Isle of Man — Douglas — 30 July – 1 August
 Round 5: GP of Greece — TBA —15-17 October
 Round 6: GP of Greece II — TBA — 22-24 October
