# What is SuperEnduro – SuperEnduro

## What is SuperEnduro?

---

### The best of enduro racing up close

SuperEnduro **brings the challenges of off-road motorcycle racing** into the heart of an arena and places you close to the action. **Fourteen riders start together** in two rows to battle shoulder to shoulder on a tough track combining speed, jumps and technical obstacles. With the entire track in view, you can **follow every battle from the starting gate to the finish line**.

Bringing enduro racing to the people – 14 riders, an amazing track and an arena packed with spectators. *Photo by: Future7Media*.

See all the amazing action right in front of you. *Photo by: Sport UP Agency/Lukas Nazdraczew*.

Enduro racing does not get any closer than this. *Photo by: Future7Media/Nicki Martínez*

### An off-road track inside an arena

**Rocks, massive logs, giant tires and dirt jumps** are all packed into a demanding race track. Riders jump over obstacles and try to **find flow and rhythm where none seems possible**. While every lap tests their speed, balance and endurance, just one **mistake can turn the race around**.

A construction site meets rock, wood, tires and shaped obstacles. *Photo by: Sport UP Agency/Lukas Nazdraczew.*

A mix of obstacles challenges the skills, endurance and racecraft.

Bridges can also be a part of the track. *Photo by: Future7Media/Andrea Belluschi*

### Big racing during an evening of entertainment

SuperEnduro combines **world-class enduro racing** with the **atmosphere of a live show**. Music, lights and rider presentations build the anticipation. When the gate drops, the racing takes over. **Intense races keep the action coming** throughout a compact program that is easy to enjoy, even if you have never watched motorcycle racing before.
