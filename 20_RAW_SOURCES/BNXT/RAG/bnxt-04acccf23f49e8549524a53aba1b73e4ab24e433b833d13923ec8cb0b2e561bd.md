# 2027 KTM 6DAYS range revealed ahead of centenary ISDE

Published on July 7th, 2026 by Enduro21 Team

##### KTM has lifted the lid on its 2027 EXC 6DAYS line-up, celebrating the 100th edition of the FIM International Six Days Enduro taking place in Grândola, Portugal this October.

In typical 6DAYS KTM fashion, the Ready to Race marque has taken its latest EXC and EXC-F range and festooned it with anodised bling from the PowerParts catalogue.

In a nod to the host nation, the MY27 6DAYS graphics incorporate the Portuguese national colours, while also featuring the Galo de Barcelos (Rooster of Barcelos), Portugal's best-known national symbol, as the centrepiece of the design.

Beyond the new stickers, the usual upgrades include CNC-machined triple clamps, a radiator fan, map-select switch, frame protection, 6DAYS seat, skid plate, Supersprox stealth rear sprocket, semi-floating front brake disc, solid rear disc, orange front axle puller and Metzeler 6 Days Extreme tyres, plus a selection of 6DAYS-branded hardware.

The five-bike line-up mirrors the EXC range, with the 300 EXC remaining the sole homologated two-stroke, joined by the 250 EXC-F, 350 EXC-F, 450 EXC-F and 500 EXC-F four-strokes.

Like the rest of KTM's MY27 enduro range, the 6DAYS models are also covered by the Indian-owned manufacturer's new 12-month warranty programme, included at no extra cost, provided servicing is carried out according to KTM's schedule through authorised dealers.

#### UK pricing:

* KTM 300 EXC 6DAYS – £10,949

* KTM 250 EXC-F 6DAYS – £11,049

* KTM 350 EXC-F 6DAYS – £11,249

* KTM 450 EXC-F 6DAYS – £11,599

* KTM 500 EXC-F 6DAYS – £11,849
