## Location and Facility Information

The venue for [Vintage Motorcycle Days, Mid-Ohio Sports Car Course](https://www.google.com/maps?ll=40.686821,-82.634395&z=10&t=m&hl=en&gl=US&mapclient=embed&q=7721+Steam+Corners+Rd+Lexington,+OH+44904), comprises a park-like, 330-acre complex just outside of Lexington, Ohio. Centrally located and easily accessible from any direction, Mid-Ohio is located 60 miles north of Columbus and 75 miles south of Cleveland.

Mid-Ohio hosts an impressive variety of locally, regionally and nationally sanctioned racing events for amateur, club and professional drivers and riders. The facility is the perfect location for AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days, with accommodations for road racing, motocross, hare scrambles, trials, and more.

The vintage dirt-track competition is held at the nearby [Ashland County Fairgrounds](https://ashlandcountyfair.com/) in [Ashland, Ohio](https://maps.app.goo.gl/p8rfHYY4MCjaMkUx9).

## Opportunities at VMD

**The opportunities for entertainment are almost endless at VMD. Do you love racing? VMD has it, with national championship racing in multiple disciplines including road racing, motocross, flat track, hare scrambles, observed trials, and more.**

##### Vintage Racing

AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days offers national championship racing in numerous disciplines including: road racing, motocross, hare scrambles, trials, flat track, and more! Whether your passion is two-stroke dirtbikes soaring over jumps or first-gen Superbikes roaring through Thunder Valley, VMD delivers racing thrills for everyone.

[VMD Racing](https://vintagemotorcycledays.com/racing/)

##### Vintage Events

Are you building a motorcycle collection, or searching for that last missing piece for your vintage motorcycle restoration? VMD hosts the largest vintage motorcycle swap meets in the world, with over 800 vendors.

There is no shortage of special events at AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days. Participate in the Lap For History, attend an informational seminar, check out a bike show, and explore the massive, 80-acre swap meet — we promise you won’t be bored for a moment at VMD!

[VMD Events](https://vintagemotorcycledays.com/events/)

**All this plus bike shows, informational seminars, demo-riding opportunities, period-correct entertainment like the American Motor Drome’s “Wall of Death,” Grand Marshal chalk talks and autographs, and so much more. So yes, it’s fair to call AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days vintage motorcycling’s best and biggest bash!**
