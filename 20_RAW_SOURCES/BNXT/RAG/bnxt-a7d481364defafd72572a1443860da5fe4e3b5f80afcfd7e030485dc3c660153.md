## severity of injury

The appropriate activation of restraint mechanisms in the vehicle offer the best possible protection for vehicle occupants.

## Operation principle of the occupant protection system

The occupant protection electronics optimizes the protection of vehicle occupants against injuries in collisions, as well as in case the vehicle rolls over. The occupant protection system from Bosch consists of peripheral sensors and an intelligent centerpiece – the airbag control unit.

Based on the sensor signals, it detects the strength and direction of a collision and can activate the restraint mechanisms in the vehicle, for example, seat-belt pretensioners and airbags, as required for maximum occupant protection.
