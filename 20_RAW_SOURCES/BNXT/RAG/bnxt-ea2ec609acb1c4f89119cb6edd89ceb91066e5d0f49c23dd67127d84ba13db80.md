# XEF 310

XEF 310

The reigning Junior World Champion bike, fresh from Axel Semb’s outstanding 2025 season, represents a new point of balance. The Fantic XEF 310 features a new engine, a new frame, enhanced ergonomics, and Euro 5+ homologation.

 A true blend of the agility of a 250 and the power delivery of a 450, designed for riders seeking maximum race performance, but also for enthusiasts looking for a smooth, intuitive, and enjoyable riding experience.

**New 304cc Engine: The Technical Heart of the Revolution**

**The technical revolution of the XEF310  begins with its new Euro 5+**–approved engine, completely redesigned to deliver superior performance and a more effective response in every situation. The exhaust system has been updated with a new high-efficiency architecture that improves power delivery and smoothness, while the racing unlock has been made even simpler and more immediate, allowing riders to fully exploit the engine’s potential.

**The new 304 cc engine has been developed specifically for modern enduro**: a powerplant designed to perform at its best across different terrains, from technical transfers to the fastest special stages. **The compression ratio, set at 12.8:1, is not extreme but optimised to provide strong, manageable torque and ideal elasticity for true 360-degree off-road riding.**

The result is an engine that is responsive, full and intuitive — capable of sustaining the pace of the most competitive riders without penalising those seeking a fun, progressive and less fatiguing ride.

**Advanced Electronics**

The new Athena ECU, developed with the know-how of Fantic Racing, manages every engine parameter with extreme precision, ensuring consistently optimised response and a high level of customization in any riding condition.

**The electronic package is among the most complete in the category: two handlebar-selectable maps (Hard/Soft), 1**0-level adjustable Traction Control, and standard WiFi COM, allowing every detail to be configured directly from a smartphone.

All functions are controlled via the new multifunction maps + traction button and the updated start/kill switch, designed to offer an even more immediate, robust and reliable user experience.

**Next-Generation Chassis**

From a chassis perspective, the XEF 310 introduces a newly designed aluminum perimeter frame, engineered to deliver rigidity, precision and a superior riding feel. **The Kayaba SSS suspension**, equipped with dedicated tuning, ensures impeccable behaviour even on the most demanding terrain, offering top-level support and sensitivity.

The braking system completes the package with a 270 mm front disc and a 240 mm rear disc, both designed to provide powerful, modular and reliable braking in every riding situation.

**Design: Renewed Identity, Unmistakable DNA**

Aesthetically, the XEF 310 introduces a new headlight mask paired with a lighter, more powerful and more modern LED headlight, complemented by **the graphics that emphasise an even more aggressive and distinctive look.**

Ergonomics, directly derived from competition, represent another key strength: compact layout, optimal mass centralisation, slim shrouds, integrated tanks (oil and fuel), direct access to the air filter, and shapes designed to ensure maximum freedom of movement in every riding condition.
