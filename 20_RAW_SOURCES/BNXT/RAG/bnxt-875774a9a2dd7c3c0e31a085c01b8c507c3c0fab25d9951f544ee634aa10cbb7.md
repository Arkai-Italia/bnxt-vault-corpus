KTM announces its 2026 offroad lineup, presenting a sharpened focus on competitive performance across all categories. Developed with direct input from top-level racing, the new range underlines KTM’s commitment to pushing the limits of offroad capability and maintaining its presence at the forefront of the sport.
This year's launch will roll out in phases, with dedicated release dates for each segment.
