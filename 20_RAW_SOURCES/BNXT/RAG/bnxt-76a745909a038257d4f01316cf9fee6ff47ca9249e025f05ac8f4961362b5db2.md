### For riders

---

#### Registration for 2026/2027

Information about registration is coming soon to the official noticeboard Sportity

#### Registration fee

The registration fee is **€130** for each class.

#### Late registration

A **€30 late fee** will be applied if you register fewer than 7 calendar days before the event.

[Number plate guidelines](https://superenduro.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Number-plate-guidelines-2026-27.pdf "SuperEnduro number plate guidelines")
