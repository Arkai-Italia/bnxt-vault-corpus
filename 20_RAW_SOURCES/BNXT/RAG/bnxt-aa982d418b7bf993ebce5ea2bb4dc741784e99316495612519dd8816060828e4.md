# Motorcycle Shippers now offers seven-day service

## Cuts shipping time by half

*[The following is an industry press release …](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/what-is-an-industry-press-release)*

FOUNTAIN VALLEY, Calif. — Motorcycle Shippers Founder/CEO Clint Lawrence announced today that his company has achieved their goal to increase your riding time, by cutting the time to ship your steed by half!

Motorcycle Shippers has now officially combined their quick-and-reliable transportation network with an innovative, patented new Pop-Up Crate technology that allows you to ship your bike from Point A to Point B in just 7-days! (The current average time in transit is 15-days).

And not only is it faster, this new service also makes shipping your bike extremely easy by eliminating the head-scratching dilemma of how to properly crate your bike to meet standards and to protect it during transit.

Motorcycle Shippers ships you the Pop-Up Crate, you roll your bike in and secure it, then they deliver it to your destination within 7-days or less! Plus, there’s no “Residential” fees, fuel surcharges, lift-gate fees or other sneaky stuff added on. The 7-day quote is all-inclusive; 100% of what you’ll invest to start riding at the new location!

Heck, they even include $7,000-worth of valuation coverage with ZERO DEDUCTIBLE at no additional charge to you! (And if your bike is decked out with all the bells and whistles, you can purchase additional coverage for just $7 per additional $1,000 in value!)

For more information about the Pop-Up Crate and 7-Day Motorcycle Shipping Service, visit <https://motorcycleshippers.com/7-day-motorcycle-shipping-service>, or call (800) 730-3151 for details and rates.

Founded in 1994 as a subsidiary of JC Motors, Motorcycle Shippers provides quick, safe and affordable motorcycle transport to riders and businesses across the US. The company ([motorcycleshippers.com](http://motorcycleshippers.com)) is focused specifically on motorcycle transportation solutions that give fellow riders more freedom to enjoy the bikes they love.
