# Tread - SXS Edition Owner's Manual - Map Tools

# Map Tools

Map tools provide quick access to information and device functions while you view the map. When you activate a map tool, it appears in a panel at the edge of the map.

Stop
:   Stops navigation of the active route.

Edit Route
:   Allows you to take a detour or skip locations in your route ([Changing Your Active Route](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-0E9562DB-43DB-4A5F-ABE6-E1899613A3A5.html)).

Mute
:   Mutes the device audio.

Cities Ahead
:   Shows upcoming cities and services along your active route or along a highway ([Cities Ahead](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-346636C4-D7A6-41F3-92F1-C9218CA3A685.html)).

Up Ahead
:   Shows upcoming locations along the route or the road on which you are traveling ([Up Ahead](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-FE51C4B3-18C1-4988-AE67-F4F61D20CE02.html)).

Elevation
:   Displays elevation changes ahead.

Traffic
:   Displays traffic conditions along your route or in your area ([Viewing Upcoming Traffic](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-1137F5DD-FA36-4984-AD53-C14CA5C9617A.html)). This feature is not available in all areas or for all device models.

Trip Data
:   Displays customizable trip data, such as speed or mileage ([Viewing Trip Data from the Map](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-F95590C9-A44B-40F2-AC95-607AA3856403.html)).

Track Recorder
:   Displays the track data for your active route.

Turns
:   Displays a list of upcoming turns in your route ([Viewing Turns and Directions](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-71F7D1F6-45F1-426E-AA0D-A5488933B36C.html)).

Weather
:   Displays weather conditions for your area.

Music Player
:   Displays media controls and media information.

Smart Notifications
:   Displays recent smart notifications from your connected phone ([Smart Notifications](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-04612806-8268-4E32-BAF7-5324D24E2294.html)).

Pitch and Roll
:   Displays the current angle of your vehicle
     ([Pitch and Roll](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-5EE76399-39B7-45BB-A463-32A2AEDF6D2E.html)).

Group Ride
:   Displays the distance and direction of the members in your group ride
     ([Group Ride](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-467103BA-F1E0-44D4-98D1-9937FD1A4517.html#GUID-467103BA-F1E0-44D4-98D1-9937FD1A4517)).

Radio
:   Allows you to use the push-to-talk feature with a compatible
    Bluetooth® headset ([Enabling Push-to-Talk with a Headset](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-0FA163AC-B5DB-4EA8-A92F-3E1204D6117B.html#GUID-CA7FA289-39DA-4A4F-A8A1-5A9107722AD3)).

Environmental Zones
:   Shows upcoming environmental zones that apply to your vehicle.

inReach
:   Allows you to view and send
    inReach® messages, and start
    inReach tracking ([inReach Features](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-F0AC3576-CD83-465E-983D-7AD585ADB59E/EN-US/GUID-5F259BE9-0FDF-4A17-A523-50207BBE5B8B.html)).
