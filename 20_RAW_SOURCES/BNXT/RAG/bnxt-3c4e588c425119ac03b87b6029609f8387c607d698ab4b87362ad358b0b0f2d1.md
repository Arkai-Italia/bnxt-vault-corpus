# AMA Newsletters

###### Email newsletters and other ways to connect with the AMA

Get the latest motorcycle news, racing results, event information, member benefits and discount information and so much more delivered straight to your inbox by subscribing to one — or all — of the AMA’s newsletters.

The AMA publishes several electronic newsletters to share information with AMA members, other motorcyclists and the general public. These newsletters make it easy to stay abreast of the latest and most important news in the world of motorcycling.

## AMA Email Newsletters

##### AMA Extra (monthly)

A collection of the latest AMA and industry news, member benefit discounts and much more.

##### AMA Hall of Fame Insider (monthly)

Learn about events related to the [AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Museum](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/hall-of-fame/), read about Hall of Famers, featured museum bikes and more.

##### AMA Life Member Newsletter (monthly)

Tailored toward AMA Life Members, this newsletter focuses on recent news pertaining to the AMA, government relations wins, and the stories of AMA Life Members.

##### AMA Member Exclusives (monthly)

Sent to all AMA members, this newsletter keeps you informed on the benefits of an AMA membership, how the AMA fights for your right to ride and the latest AMA and industry news

##### AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days (weekly from mid-May to July)

Get the latest scoop on all things related to the AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame’s largest fundraising event at the Mid-Ohio Sports Car Course every July. [Learn more about Vintage Motorcycle Days](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/events/scheduled-events/vintage-motorcycle-days/).

##### AMA Action Alerts (as they occur)

Get [notices from the AMA government relations department](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/rights/rights-resources/subscribe-for-rights-updates/) notifying you of ongoing threats to motorcycling rights and take action by signing petitions and writing to your representatives. [Subscribe to Rights Action Alerts now](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/rights/rights-resources/subscribe-for-rights-updates/).
