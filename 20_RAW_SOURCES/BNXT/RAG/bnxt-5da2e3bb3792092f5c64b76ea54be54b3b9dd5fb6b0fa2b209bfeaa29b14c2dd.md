# The platform around the app

ENDURO.FAMILY started as a rider’s app — and became a platform guides, rentals, workshops and creators make money on. **Six ecosystem products are already live**, and everything is tied to one foundation: the garage, the tracks, and every rider’s profile.

ECOSYSTEMplatform products

Live

For guides, rentals, and moto schools

### Partner storefronts

A business card right where riders pick the area's routes: guiding, rental, training, lodging.

* A pin on the community track map among the tracks
* A service catalog and direct contacts, no commission
* Tours and training sessions in the season calendar
* A verified partner badge after application review

**How it ties into the app**A rider opens a track in the area — and sees who will guide them, provide a bike, and put them up. One PRO subscription covers the guide, coach, and workshop roles at once.

[Page for partners](https://www.enduro.family/en/guides/)

Live

For workshops and garages

### Moto workshop CRM

Work orders with itemized jobs, client approval in the app, and completed work recorded into the maintenance log of their bike.

* Work order: scope and price locked by client confirmation
* Workshop roles: owner, service advisors, mechanics
* On-request access to the client's maintenance log and engine hours
* Service requests straight from a rider's garage

**How it ties into the app**The app counts engine hours and reminds about maintenance — the next service deadline brings the client back to you.

[Page for workshops](https://www.enduro.family/en/services/)

Live

For riders, guides, and organizers

### The 3D flyover movie pipeline

A GPU fleet edits a movie of every ride: a flyover of the track above satellite terrain, video inserts, and titles.

* A movie from any recorded ride — no editing required
* Insert editor: video clips and captions
* Priority rendering without the queue — as a one-time purchase
* Ready-made marketing for tours and races

**How it ties into the app**The movie is assembled from what the platform already knows: the track, the terrain, your footage. Every movie advertises the ride, the tour, and the app.

[More about the movie](https://www.enduro.family/en/movie/)

Live

For creators and brands

### Ambassador program

Riders earn on content and referrals: personal promo codes, a dashboard with metrics, and the Creators Fund.

* Ambassador dashboard: code activations and statistics
* A promo code = an extended trial for a newcomer
* Payouts to self-employed riders and sole proprietors, content and referral scoring
* For brands — measurable placements

**How it ties into the app**Content is born in the app — a ride, a movie, a post — and brings new riders in through a promo code. The effect shows in numbers.

[Ambassador dashboard](https://creators.enduro.family)

Live

For riders and stores

### Parts marketplace

Parts by exploded diagrams, the way dealers do it: pick the bike, the assembly and the part — and see which stores have it and for how much.

* Original KTM, Husqvarna and GASGAS catalog — 1,340 models
* Assembly diagram: tap a part, get its number
* Prices and stock across stores in one table
* Accessories and aftermarket parts as separate cards

**How it ties into the app**The marketplace runs on the website today (in Russian), and you buy from the seller's store. Next: matching by the bikes in your garage right in the app.

[Open the marketplace](https://www.enduro.family/parts/)

Live

For riders and teams

### Chats and direct messages

Plan a ride inside the app: direct messages and a shared chat for every event.

* Event chat — participants and the organizer are already there
* Direct messages: photos, videos, files, voice
* Reactions, replies, pins, forwarding, search
* Requests from strangers, reports, blocking, mute

**How it ties into the app**A chat appears where shared context already exists: a race, a tour, an outing. No need to gather people all over again, and the organizer can switch it to announcements only.

[How it works](https://www.enduro.family/en/help/clubs/messages/)
