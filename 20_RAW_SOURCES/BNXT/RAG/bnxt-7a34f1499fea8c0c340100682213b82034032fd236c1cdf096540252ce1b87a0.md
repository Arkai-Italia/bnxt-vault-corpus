# Tread Owner's Manual - Routines

# Routines

Routines automate your
Garmin PowerSwitch™ device. When you define a routine on your compatible
Garmin® navigation device, it turns switches on or off automatically when the conditions of the routine are met. For example, you can set a routine to turn on a switch at specific time each day or when your vehicle reaches a specific speed. Routines are available only when using a compatible
Garmin navigation device.
