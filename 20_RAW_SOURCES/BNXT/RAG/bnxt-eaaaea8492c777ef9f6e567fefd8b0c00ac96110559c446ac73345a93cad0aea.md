# **1. ENTRIES**

Entry fee for all the riders (FIM Europe) is 120 EUR (transponder pack included) paid to the promoter. All entries must be received before 7 days prior the start of the Event. The entry fees for late entries will be 150 EUR.

Entries must be made by an electronic entry form available on the site https://superenduro.org/for-riders/. All other information relating to the riders, team, sponsor and the brand of the machine shall be completed.

# **6. RIDERS ADMITTED**

The riders admitted will be established according to Art. 4 of FIM Europe SuperEnduro Cup Regulations.

The maximum number of riders accepted in FIM Europe class is: 28

# **7. STARTING PROCEDURE**

The starting procedure will be established according to Art. 6.8 of FIM Europe SuperEnduro Cup Regulations.

# **8. PADDOCK**

Paddock is located inside the Arena, close to starting gates. Dedicated refueling area outside the Arena will be provided and indicated by organizer.

## Pagina 5

The use of an environmental mat is always mandatory, also in the case of electric generators or other generators. It is compulsory for electric bikes to use an electrical insulation mat. Failure to comply with this obligation is subject to a disciplinary penalty of 100 EUR. In the event of non-payment of the penalty or failure to use the mat after the above-mentioned penalty has been imposed, the rider will be excluded from the event.

# **9. BIKE WARM-UP AREA**

This area will be designated near the paddock, outside. It is strictly forbidden to warm-up the bikes in any other location, especially at paddock inside building. When riding during warming-up the rider is obliged to wear a helmet. Riding on the green areas is forbidden.

# **10. INSURANCE**

By endorsing the application form for entry, the FNM of the rider certifies that the rider is insured in accordance with the FIM Europe requirements.

The organizer has contacted third party insurance in accordance with Art. 110.1 of the FIM Europe Sporting Code. This insurance includes a guarantee of the minimum amount as stipulated by the FIM Europe Management Council. The Insurance comes in effect 2 days before the first practice and ends after Prize giving ceremony of the meeting. The organizer disclaims all responsibility for damage to a motorcycle, its accessories and components arising out of an accident, fire or other incident.

# **11. SIGNALING ZONE**

The Signaling Zone is only available to team members actively taking part in the race.

# **12. RESULTS**

The classification will be established according to Art. 6.9. of FIM Europe SuperEnduro Cup Regulations.

# **13. FUEL**

According to FIM Specifications.

# **14. PRIZE-GIVING CEREMONY**

Ceremony will be held straight after second Final according to time schedule close to starting gates. Riders who qualify for prizes are invited to attend the prize-giving ceremony. If invited, attendance to the prize giving ceremony or press conference is compulsory.

# **15. PROTESTS AND APPEALS**

Any person or group of persons (rider, entrant, manufacturer, official, etc.), recognized by the FIM and concerned by a decision taken under the authority of the FIM Europe, may ask for redress for the consequences of that decision.

Protest must be lodged according to the FIM Europe Disciplinary and Arbitration Code and the Supplementary Regulations and be accompanied by a fee of € 350,- or equivalent amount in local currency, returnable if the protest is justified.

Protests against the admission of a registered rider, team or machine must be presented before the riders leave the waiting zone in order to participate in the practice.

Protests against results must be presented to the International Jury within 30 minutes following the publishing of the results.

An appeal can be made to the FIM Europe Disciplinary Commission against a decision of the International Jury.

Protest entailing a fuel control: See FIM Europe rulebooks.
