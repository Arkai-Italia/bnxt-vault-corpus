# Track and Trace

#### Asset tracking – transparency across the complete supply chain

Returnable container cycles and the associated demands placed on all companies involved are becoming ever more complex. Many different parties with an even greater number of different IT systems are involved in the process. Logistics departments thus require a solution that creates complete transparency regarding the use of returnable containers, while also taking the individual perspectives of each user group into account. To address these requirements, Bosch has developed a smart tracking solution called Track and Trace.

Track and Trace focuses on the needs of returnable container managers and planners, providing them with up-to-date information on the position and status of any returnable container. Custom dashboards ensure that each user group can access exactly the information that it requires.

To enable asset tracking in real time, sensors are permanently attached to the returnable containers. Logistics specialists receive regular updates regarding the location of the returnable container and, if necessary, the status of the goods. Routes can be tracked in real time, thus enabling immediate identification of disruptions or delays.

IoT gateways collect the sensor data and enrich it with position data before transferring it to the application in the cloud. Gateways are easily attached and can withstand even the harshest conditions. The localization is usually based on GPS, while the transfer takes place via GSM.

## Real-time data analysis

The data received from the IoT is pre-processed in the system before being made available to users in the web frontend in the form of reports. This provides returnable container managers with real-time information on inventories, overall productivity, potential losses as well as throughput and transport times of returnable containers, for example. Necessary measures to boost efficiency in the container cycle can thus be implemented as soon as possible.

## Installation process

## Step 1: attach hardware

* Secure the sensors (permanently) to the returnable containers
* Attach gateways at critical positions within the process chain or in trailers

## Step 2: link known information with sensor data

* Sensors linked to shipping notes or packing lists are presented as digital images in the system

## Step 3: start data transfer

* Automatic data transfer in real time from the sensor via the gateway to the cloud – 24 hours a day, 365 days a year

## Step 4: make information available

* Real-time data for daily operations and optimizations
* Basis for additional services

## Frequently asked questions

#### How does Track and Trace meet the needs of the automotive industry?

Bosch is well acquainted with the challenges of the automotive industry. The Track and Trace solution combines this know-how with logistics expertise: the smart tracking solution was developed by experts with years of experience in complex supply chains, automotive logistics and beyond. Track and Trace can be easily integrated into your processes. A solutions architecture comprising asset tags and centralized gateways can be quickly installed on-site and put into operation (plug and play). An implementation partner is therefore not necessary. This setup offers a cost-effective solution that allows you to connect a large number of assets (>500,000).

In addition, this solution is independent of the existing IT infrastructure, obviating the need for a complex integration process. The robust hardware has a service life of over eight years and covers a vehicle generation without hardware replacement. It is also suitable for outdoor use without a power supply. Bosch works closely with partners from the automotive industry and continuously updates the solution to meet new requirements.

#### What does the tracking solution cost?

There is no standard answer to this question. The costs for an end-to-end tracking solution are dependent on several factors. Before you get started with a tracking project, you should clearly define your use case. Among other things, the following questions play a key role:

* Which parameters would you like to track (e.g. position, temperature, shock, humidity)?
* Would you like to track in real time or will a status update at specific intervals suffice?
* Which hardware is best suited for your use case?
* How many assets would you like to monitor?
* How long should the contract run?

Bosch can help you find the solution setup that best serves the purpose of your use case and works together with you to calculate the costs of your business case, including the return on investment (ROI).

#### How long can the hardware be consistently used in the field?

The lifecycle of the sensors and gateways is over eight years. Automakers can therefore track their assets over the course of a vehicle generation (typically seven years) without changing the hardware.

The sensors and gateways can be easily installed and permanently fixed, which explains why Bosch customers have never lost the hardware to date.

#### Is the Bosch solution also suitable for large-scale asset tracking projects?

Track and Trace is also being used by large automakers that manage over a million returnable containers and hundreds of users and locations. Moreover, the tracking solution has multi-client capability. This allows you to set specific access rights and other permissions for individual plants or partners.

#### What reporting functions does Track and Trace offer?

The web application of Track and Trace provides several different preconfigured dashboards. You can customize these dashboards to meet your requirements, allowing you to have an overview of the following aspects at all times:

* Overall productivity
* Inventory
* Turnaround tim
* Losses
* Transport time
* Asset network

#### Can the data from Track and Trace also be made available in other IT systems and how does Bosch deal with white spots?

All data captured by Track and Trace can also be linked to customer systems such as ERP, WMS, and TMS.
