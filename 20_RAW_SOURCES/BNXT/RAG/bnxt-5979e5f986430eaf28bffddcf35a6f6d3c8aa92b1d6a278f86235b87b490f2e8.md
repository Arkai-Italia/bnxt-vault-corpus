# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Africa Eco Race is one of the last authentic rally raids connecting Europe to Dakar, crossing Morocco, Mauritania and Senegal over more than 6,000 kilometers of desert and off-road terrain.

Created by rally legends Jean-Louis Schlesser, Hubert Auriol and René Metge, the event preserves the spirit of the original Dakar rally and finishes at the legendary Lac Rose in Dakar.

With global television coverage, strong digital engagement and an international participant base, Africa Eco Race offers brands a powerful platform for global exposure and strong positioning in African markets.
