# Iron Pony Motorsports Announced as the Official Motorcycle Dealer of 2022 AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days

* by [Joy Burgess](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/joybelle98/)
* May 26, 2022
* 0 Comments
* 2918 Views

PICKERINGTON, Ohio (May 26, 2022)— Iron Pony Motorsports has been announced as the **Official Motorcycle Dealer of the 2022 AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days presented by Royal Enfield**, which takes place July 22-24 at the Mid-Ohio Sports Car Course in Lexington, Ohio.

“Iron Pony Motorsports is proud to be the Official Motorcycle Dealer for 2022 AMA Vintage Days. We couldn’t be more excited to be a part of this year’s event,” said Sean Reifenberg, Director of Marketing for Irony Pony Motorsports

In addition to being the Official Motorcycle Dealership, Iron Pony Motorsports will again be participating in the Kenda Kids’ area, which will feature bikes from STACYC and YCF/Piranha.

“We believe it is important for the youth of today to experience the joy of two wheels, which is why Iron Pony is committed to helping get kids involved in the sport from a very early age,” Reifenberg said.

“It’s great to have a local and very active motorcycle dealer group partner with us as we carry on the fun and tradition of AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days,” said AMA Editorial Director Mitch Boehm. “It just goes to show why Ohio’s vibrant motorcycle culture and VMD go hand-in-hand, and that there is really one place to be in late July.”

2022 AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days presented by Royal Enfield will feature AMA Motorcycle Hall of Famer and 500cc World Champ Kevin Schwantz as Grand Marshal, who’ll be on hand to sign autographs, speak to enthusiasts from the AMA Soundstage, and participate in a Lap For History each day. A special highlight will be an exclusive showing of Schwantz’s 1985 Yoshimura-Suzuki AMA Superbike, which launched his legendary career.

The three-day event will also include all the vintage-oriented happenings classic-bike fans love, including Hall of Fame activities, the biggest motorcycle swap meet in North America, complete with parts and bikes and memorabilia from the past 120 years of motorcycling, and vintage road and off-road racing.

“This sponsorship marks an exciting time for us in the motorcycling industry as we now have three stores in Ohio in Mansfield, Columbus, and the new Lancaster/Carroll store,” Reifenberg said. “We have increased our selection to over 35 brands of motorcycles, electric bikes, and power sport vehicles. AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days [take place] in our backyard and we are looking forward to supporting this great event and the community of riders.”

To learn more about Iron Pony Motorsports, visit [*ironpony.com*](http://ironpony.com).
