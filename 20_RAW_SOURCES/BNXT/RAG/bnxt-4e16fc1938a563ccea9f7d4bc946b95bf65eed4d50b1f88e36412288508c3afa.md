3. Red Bull Romaniacs Offroad tracks

All the race classes compete at the same event alongside the world's top hard enduro professionals
 Each race class battles against their own personal physical endurance and the tracks to try to become Red Bull Romaniacs **championship winners** in their selected race class.
 **- Adventure class** have **rallye race tracks** to suit their motorcycles and competitors.
 **- Hard enduro motorcycles** have tracks that suit their motorcycles off-road capabilities
 **- eMoto's** have tracks that suit their bikes and battery capabilities

To complete in the Red Bull Romaniacs hard enduro rallye event is tough, it requires preparation, training, dedication and perseverance to get to the finish line and to become a **Red Bull Romaniacs hard enduro rallye FINISHER**

Difficult, technically demanding Offroad days on hilly landscapes are characteristics of the route and are manageable with standard hard enduro and Adventure motorcycles with an autonomy of 80 kilometres. Electric eMoto with an autonomy of 30 Kilometres.
  It is stated explicitly, that the selective characteristics of the route and the attainable maximum riding time for each section and each day are conditioned by the weather, perseverance and driving ability and can lead to a considerable failure ratio. The track has route banners, race class

6. Race card

Upon arrival in Sibiu, at the competitor [INSCRIPTION](https://www.redbullromaniacs.com/for-competitors/sibiu-inscription-arrival-times), you will receive your race card (event permit) issued by the Federația Română de Motociclism (FRM).
