# Our team - Honda RedMoto Racing

[Home](https://hondaredmotoracing.com) Our team

Honda Racing RedMoto World Enduro Team represents the Honda brand in the main Enduro competitions worldwide and is officially supported by Honda Motor Europe. Our team can count on a professional organisation that is constantly evolving and always ready to take on new challenges.
Since 2018, the racing department has been based in San Pellegrino Terme, where a staff of experienced technicians works to develop the best bikes for Enduro racing. In addition to carrying out all the necessary communication and logistics activities, we also organise the team’s presence at the races in the best possible way with hospitality trucks and service vans.

“We are a solid company with important partners, qualified mechanics and fast riders. A key-mix that has taken us to the top! We’re a great team and we have all the right skills to achieve great results. Our goal is always high and we’ll try to be first in every class. Our story is constant growth and we’ll do our best for our partners and sponsors.”

**Matteo Boffelli**
Team Manager
