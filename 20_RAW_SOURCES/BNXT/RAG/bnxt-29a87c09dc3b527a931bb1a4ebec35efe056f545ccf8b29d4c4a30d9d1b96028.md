# Why race July 27-31. 2027?

Event Opening Ceremony

One of the main reasons to compete is the unique enduro rallye terrain in the Red Bull Romaniacs regions. The terrain has gained a compelling reputation in the world of 'hard enduro'. Many competitors want to experience this for themselves and see if this enduro rallye can really be as gruelling and gnarly as its reputation and at the same time as varied and stunningly beautiful as the Romaniacs event [Videos](https://www.redbullromaniacs.com/videos) display

 Red Bull Romaniacs celebrates the worldwide growth of this sport and is recognised worldwide as **''**The World's toughest, hardest & longest **hard enduro rallye"**An unique event heldthat is designed to cater for the varied skill levels of Amatuer competitors and the professional Factory competitors.

 There are 9differentrace classes to choose from:-
 **3 - [**Adventure ((multi cylinder) rallye race classes**](https://www.redbullromaniacs.com/adventure-class/about-the-adventure-class) - Adventure Ultimate, Adventure Core,****Adventure Lite**
 **5 - Gold**, **Atom**, **Iron**, **Bronze**, **Silver,**
 **2 - [**eMoto race classes**](https://www.redbullromaniacs.com/for-competitors/emoto-race-class) -****eMoto hobby**
 Here are video and text recommendations for [Picking the right race class](https://www.redbullromaniacs.com/for-competitors/picking-the-right-class)

 A race class to suit every competitors skill level, but be warned, you got to train hard to be able to celebrate being a Red Bull Romaniacs Finisher, many have tried and many have failed !
 This is a**world class - must do event**, created by racing motorcyclists for enthisiastic racing motorcyclists.

 Competing in this unique, vast, wilderness terrain is a big motivational force that draws enduro enthusiasts from 60+ nations from all around the world. [Register to race in 2026](https://www.redbullromaniacs.com/for-competitors/2027-register-to-race)

 The Red Bull Romaniacs rallye has been run every single year since 2004, no matter what the worldwide 'current situation' might be, the Romaniacs event management team and staff make agreements, find solutions and apply them to allow competitors from all over the world to race in Romania every year.

 The 2026 **UNSTOPPABLE Edition 23**will bring competitors from all race classes together, competing alongside the world's top hard enduro professionals, while at times having to help & encourage each other in some of the most difficult, extreme, hostile and remote situations they have ever encountered offroad on a motorcycle.
 Tracks are made to suit the motorcycles of each competitors race class,
 - Multi-cylinder Adventure motorcycles
 - Single cylinder Dakar style Adventure motorcycles
 - eMoto bikes
 -  hard enduro motorcycles
 The large family of motorcycle racers, all come to Romaniacs, funtimes are available for everyone.

 The event is fully motivated by the 35,000+ altitude meters that the Gold class competitors have to climb and decend, this is almost four times the height of Mount Everest and the most altitude metres tackled in any 'hard enduro rallye' event.worldwide.
 Red Bull Romaniacs is, '**A must do event'**, many competitors come to try to be a 'Romaniacs finisher at least once', many competitors come and race every year, to challenge themselves and their motorcycles against the vertical and incredibly long up and steep decents Romaniacs tracks in the Carpathian mountains provide.
