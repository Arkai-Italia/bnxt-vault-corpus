# RALLYE DU MAROC CLASSIC

## Based on the principles of regularity and endurance.

Enjoy the same navigation system, classification format, and exclusive route and categories, just like Dakar Classic.
Starting in 2026, the Rallye du Maroc will introduce a new event: the Rallye du Maroc Classic, a regularity competition designed for historic vehicles.

Held alongside the main rally, it captures the spirit of the competition and translates it into a more elegant format focused on driving, excitement, and Morocco’s unique landscapes. A concept that celebrates motorsport history while adding a new dimension to the event.

To support new and experienced participants in learning and improving their use of navigation equipment and the rules of the Rallye du Maroc Classic, we suggest you contact our partner below to arrange training sessions so that you are as well prepared as possible on the day of the rally and can enjoy your race to the full:
