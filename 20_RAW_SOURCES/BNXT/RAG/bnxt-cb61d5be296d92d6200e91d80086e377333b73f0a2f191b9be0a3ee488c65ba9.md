# Amazon Prime Video to Release Exclusive MotoGP Docuseries

* by [Keaton Maisano](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/kmaisano/)
* November 30, 2021
* 0 Comments
* 3197 Views

November 30, 2021

By Keaton Maisano

From the track to your living room, Dorna Sports announced an Amazon exclusive docuseries that promises to take fans of the FIM MotoGP World Championship behind the scenes of one of the world’s most heated competitions.

Produced by The Mediapro Studio, the series will include eight 50-minute episodes released on Amazon Prime Video in 2022. MotoGP’s most captivating stars will be on display as Valentino Rossi, Marc Marquez, Fabio Quartararo, Francesco Bagnaia, Joan Mir and Jack Miller will all be included.

“MotoGP is a demanding, impressive sport with a global fanbase and we’re sure that this new Amazon Exclusive production will not disappoint the fans,” Barry Furlong, vice president of Prime Video EU, said. “We are delighted to continue the collaboration between The Mediapro Studio and Prime Video to create quality content with global sports stars.”

The docuseries will offer a look into the day-to-day happenings of the riders and team members that make MotoGP special. From the competition to the personal side of things, the series aims to provide viewers with unprecedented access.

“We believe that it will appeal not only to fans of motorcycling, but to all viewers interested in a good story, in this case of triumph and sacrifice both in sporting and in personal terms, which we are able to show thanks to the unique access that, for the first time, the protagonists of the championship have given to our production team,” Laura Fernández Espeso, CEO of The Mediapro Studio, said.
