## MAIN FEATURES

ELECTRONICS

#### LAUNCH CONTROL AND QUICK SHIFT

Making their debut on the 4-stroke range are Launch Control (LC), for perfect starts with zero wheel spin, and Quick Shift (QS), for changing gears without lifting off the throttle. The controls are mounted on a new button panel integrated into the redesigned handlebar guard, alongside the Map Switch and Traction Control.

SUSPENSION

#### KYB SUSPENSION KIT WITH New SETTINGS

The 48 mm KYB AOS closed-cartridge front fork and the KYB C46 RCU monoshock feature new hydraulic settings developed in competition. The kit ensures maximum shock absorption, traction and handling responsiveness even in the most extreme conditions.

DESIGN

#### LED HEADLAMP AND NEW HEADLIGHT MASK

Design and functionality come together in the new headlight mask with integrated headlamp, which features a powerful, latest-generation LED headlamp. It provides a more powerful beam and lower power consumption, which is better for the battery.

#### NEW TRAILTECH SPEEDOMETER

The new Trailtech dashboard makes its debut: it is larger, more robust and easier to read even in the harshest conditions. It is paired with the new wheel reed sensor for high-precision speed readings.

#### REINFORCED TRANSMISSION PROTECTION

The MY27 introduces the new chain guard with a reinforced structure to withstand mud and impacts, and the new front sprocket guard, redesigned to offer wider, more robust coverage that complies with competition regulations.

#### SOFT GRIPS

The new soft-compound grips absorb vibrations transmitted from the handlebars, reducing pressure on the forearms to ensure a secure grip and prolonged physical endurance.

#### ERGAL FOOTPEGS AND ZF SPROCKETS

Ergal racing footpegs with steel pins for maximum grip and to facilitate foot movement. ZF Sprocket rear sprocket with an aluminium core and steel teeth for the perfect blend of lightness and durability.

#### RACING SEAT AND BI-COMPONENT HANDGUARDS

Dedicated racing seat with non-slip covering and a document pocket. Reinforced handguards made from bi-component material to give a racing look and maximum durability in intense off-road conditions.

#### RACING LOOK

Red anodised details (quick-release wheel spindle, chain tensioner bolt, oil caps) and official racing graphics complete an aggressive and unmistakable aesthetic on the race track.

## Ideal balance between lightness and traction

The RR Race 350 4T combines responsiveness, lightness and the brilliance of an extremely versatile engine. With the MY27 technological innovations, including Launch Control, Quick Shift and race-tuned KYB suspension, rideability is taken to a whole new level. The race-ready set-up and high-spec components complete the perfect package to dominate in competitive enduro.
