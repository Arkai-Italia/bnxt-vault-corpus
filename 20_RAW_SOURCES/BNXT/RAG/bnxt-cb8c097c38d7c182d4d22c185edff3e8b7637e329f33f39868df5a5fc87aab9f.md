# Final Two AMA Supermoto National Championship Events Canceled

* by [Jim Witters](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/jwittersama-cycle-org/)
* July 30, 2020
* 0 Comments
* 3242 Views

## Races would have determined FIM North America Championship

The  final two rounds of the 2020 AMA Supermoto National Championship Series have been canceled, due to health concerns and restrictions associated with the coronavirus pandemic.

The six-event series had been scheduled to kick off April 4-5 in Primm, Nev., and wrap up Sept. 11-12 in Sturgis, S.D. None of the races was run.

The final two events—the Trois-Rivieres, Quebec, Canada, round July 31-Aug. 2, sanctioned by the Canadian Motorcycle Association, and the Sturgis event—would have determined the FIM North America Supermoto Championship.
