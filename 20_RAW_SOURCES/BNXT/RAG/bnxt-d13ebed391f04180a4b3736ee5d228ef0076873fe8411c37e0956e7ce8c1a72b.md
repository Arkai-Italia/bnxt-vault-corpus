# 2027 KTM 50 SX FACTORY EDITION - FULL FACTORY FLAVOR | KTM United States

## 01. Before The Gate

FIND THE RIGHT FIT

### Adjustablity

The KTM 50 SX FACTORY EDITION is designed to grow with the little racer, by being height adjustable. Different options were built into the KTM 50 SX construction using scientific statistical data and mannequins. So, finding the right fit for your junior ripper is ultra easy thanks to height adjustable suspension, handlebar position, subframe, and bodywork, taking the seat height from a low setting of 634mm to a taller setting of 682mm.

GET STARTED

### Ignition

Fitted with digital ignition, the KTM 50 SX FACTORY EDITION has a primed power and ignition curve, making for a cleaner burn and smoother power delivery. Individual ignition timing across the entire rpm range, just like that found on the full-sized KTM SX roster, adds a vital advantage to the digital ignition package. The throttle cable assembly benefits from a stabilization cap that extends the lifetime of the cable by reducing the risk of kinking and damage.

THE HEART

### Carburetor

A proven Dell'Orto type PHBG 19 BS carburetor produces the goods in the KTM 50 SX FACTORY EDITION, with settings that work perfectly in harmony with the engine, exhaust, and air intake design. Specific to the KTM 50 SX FACTORY EDITION, the reed valve case has a 'double stage' concept, with an additional set of membrane flaps for even better gas atomization.
