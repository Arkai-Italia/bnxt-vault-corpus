# 2025 KTM 250 SX-F ADAMO EDITION - CHAMPIONSHIP WINNING | KTM

## 01. Before The Gate

LESS FOCUS ON THE TOOL, MORE FOCUS ON THE TRACK

### Customization

Shape your 2025 KTM 250 SX-F ADAMO EDITION quickly and easily with the anticipated arrival of the Connectivity Unit Offroad. This two-part system is mounted between the right fork leg (protected by the shape of the number plate) and the GPS component on the redesigned front fender. The 'CUO' enables a wealth of easily adjustable options for engine behavior and suspension settings via the KTMconnect App. But that's not all. An official affiliation with LitPro will empower all riders to examine their lines, speed, air-time, throttle position and other information to make their lap-times worthy of the 2025 KTM 250 SX-F ADAMO EDITION.

BREATHE EASY

### Air filter box

The 2025 KTM 250 SX-F ADAMO EDITION uses a new one-piece air inlet sleeve and snorkel construction for more durability and less possibility for air deformation. Designed to provide maximum airflow, the air filter box also comes with a special black vented cover. The Twin Air filter and air filter support have a simple fail-proof mounting system for safe and accurate filter installation, ensuring the filter is easily accessed and, therefore easy for maintenance.

### Easy Suspension Adjustment

Like the rest of the 2025 KTM- SX-F range, the 2025 KTM 250 SX-F ADAMO EDITION comes with a totally tool-free suspension setup at both ends. The shock absorber design features hand-adjustable dual compression control, it allows riders to tweak high- and low-speed settings in a matter of seconds. On the front end, the forks are also easily changeable thanks to a single air pressure preload valve, as well as easy access click adjusters for compression and rebound. The air pump comes included.
