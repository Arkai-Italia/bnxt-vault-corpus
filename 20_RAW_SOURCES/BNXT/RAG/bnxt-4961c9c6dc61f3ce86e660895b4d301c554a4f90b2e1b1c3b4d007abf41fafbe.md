# Rieju MR 300i Hard Enduro 2027: fully equipped and ready to race for €11,199

Published on September 8th, 2026 by Nicki Martinez

.jpg)

#### **Rieju expands its 2027 MR 300i range, previously made up of the Racing and Pro versions, with the new Hard Enduro, factory-prepared for competition with engine, cooling and transmission upgrades, as well as a long list of protections and components specifically designed for hard enduro.**

Following the extensive overhaul introduced last year with the arrival of the new 300cc 2T engine with TFI electronic fuel injection, Rieju heads into 2027 without needing to reinvent a formula that, it has to be said, they got right first time.

* Related: [2026 Rieju MR300i fuel injected two-stroke first test](https://enduro21.com/en/news/video-2026-rieju-mr300i-fuel-injected-two-stroke-first-test)

The Figueres factory retains the Spanish-developed and manufactured fuel-injected engine across the entire MR family, with three power maps, an electronic exhaust valve, diaphragm clutch and counter-rotating balance shaft.

The big news this time comes at the top of the range: the new MR 300i Hard Enduro 2027, which joins the family as a dedicated model, loaded with factory extras.

### **Rieju MR 300i Hard Enduro 2027: ready for the hard stuff**

Until now, MR 300i Pro owners could move their bikes towards this specification with an optional Hard Enduro kit costing €699.99, although further protection and components still had to be added. For 2027, Rieju takes the concept a step further and turns it into a standalone version, with preparation that goes much further than simply adding a few protective parts.

The MR 300i Hard Enduro 2027 costs €11,199 and is based on the same TFI engine as the Racing and Pro, although it incorporates modifications designed to improve low-rpm performance, strengthen cooling and increase its effectiveness in the slowest and most technical sections. The engine receives a specific inertia bushing, designed to improve low-end drive and traction, as well as a specific spark plug.

The cooling system is also upgraded with a specific Hard water-pump cover featuring a higher-flow impeller, an expansion tank and a dual electric-fan module – particularly useful when riding at low speeds and airflow through the radiators is reduced.

.jpg)The exhaust is also specific to this version, while the transmission adopts shorter gearing, accompanied by an AXP chain guide.

Overall, the new Hard Enduro leaves the dealership with engine, cooling and transmission modifications, as well as the protection and components that would normally have to be added after buying the bike.

### **Specific equipment on the 2027 MR 300i Hard Enduro:**

* Specific inertia bushing to improve low-rpm response

* Hard water-pump cover with higher-flow impeller

* Specific spark plug

* Cooling-system expansion tank

* Dual electric-fan module

* OXA HARD OFF-ROAD exhaust specific to Rieju

* Shorter gearing

* AXP chain guide

* EXCEL wheels with machined black hubs

* Michelin Enduro tyres

* Nissin brakes

* Galfer Wave discs

* Specific Hard Offroad rear disc

* AXP Enduro skid plate with linkage guard

* Swingarm protector

* Front and rear disc guards

* Machined aluminium handlebar guards

* Offset stainless-steel footpegs

* Pull straps

* Black XTRIG triple clamps

* Simplified wiring

* Dual starter buttons

* Red Hard seat

* New Hard 2027 graphics

* New LED headlight and new MY27 front mask

### **MR 300i Pro 2027: few changes to an already well-equipped version**

Just below it in the range is the MR 300i Pro 2027, priced at €10,599.

Here, the updates are much more limited, which makes sense given that the real revolution arrived with the 2026 range and Rieju's switch to electronic fuel injection. For 2027, the brand has limited itself to refining some equipment, lighting and finish details.

The Pro retains its position as the version most focused on conventional enduro competition, with a higher level of equipment than the Racing.

It continues to use KYB forks with black-anodised DLC anti-friction treatment, accompanied by red XTRIG triple clamps, Renthal Fatbar handlebars, a Power Racing by OXA exhaust and EXCEL wheels with machined red hubs.

The tyres remain Michelin Enduro, while braking continues to be handled by Nissin, although the previous NG floating front disc makes way for a new Galfer floating disc.

Like the rest of the 2027 range, it also gets a new LED headlight and new front mask, along with specific MY27 Pro graphics.

### **MR 300i Racing 2027: the gateway to the TFI range**

The MR 300i Racing 2027, priced at €9,499, remains below the psychological €10,000 barrier and continues to be the entry point into Rieju's 300cc 2T fuel-injected family.

.jpg)Here too, changes compared with the previous model are minimal. It retains KYB suspension, EXCEL wheels with cast hubs, Mitas Terra Force tyres, a Power Racing by OXA exhaust, Neken handlebars and grips, as well as Nissin brakes with Galfer discs.

The main MY27 updates are once again concentrated on the visual and lighting side, with a new LED headlight, new front mask and specific Racing 2027 graphics.

Underneath it all remains the same TFI 300 2T engine with electronic fuel injection introduced last season, featuring Race, Forest and Wet maps, a diaphragm clutch, electronic exhaust valve and counter-rotating balance shaft.
