# Demanding Day Three sees competitors reach the midway point in 6DAYS® Portugal

October 14, 2026

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/69df6c3026382890d017c919/6acff2996aaffd8ab29e50c2__AZD7440%20(1).jpg)

**There was no change to the World Trophy leaderboard, with Italy continuing to lead the way in this year’s 6DAYS® FIM Enduro of Nations at the end of Day Three.**

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/69df6c3026382890d017c919/6acff2a838bacf22d06c8fb3_c3e9fca1f451b4b672fe76b02446ccee.png)

Always an important milestone in any 6DAYS®, **Day Three marked the midway point** in this year’s **hundredth edition**. On another tough and challenging day of racing, competitors would complete up to **two-hundred-and-sixty kilometres** of riding.

However, there was light relief for some, with organisers forced to **cancel the Santo Andrea Enduro Test** due to a technical timing issue, which **reduced the total number of timed special tests from six to four**. For those who reached the end-of-day service point, it would prove a welcome sight, with the first half of the race now behind them.

**Italy strengthen World Trophy lead on Day Three**

Despite some tense moments throughout the day, **Italy** never lost focus and **continue to lead the World Trophy** classification at the end of Day Three. Managing their advantage over **France in second** to perfection, they increased their lead by two seconds and enter the second half of the 6DAYS® Portugal with a healthy **three-minute-and-twenty-six-second buffer**.

With three riders inside the top fifteen, France looked fast on track today, but will be wondering tonight what it will take to start gaining time on Italy. While Italy and France control the first two positions, **Sweden in third** are coming under attack from the **United States in fourth**.

Putting in their **best ride of the week**, the United States have now **closed to within twelve seconds of Sweden**. With **Grant Davis** (KTM), **Jonathan Girroir** (KTM) and **Dante Oliveira** (KTM) all finishing **within six seconds of each other in fourth, fifth and sixth overall** respectively, it is clear the sandy terrain is favouring them.

*“We definitely gained some ground today that’s helped us catch back up to Sweden,”* said **Girroir**. *“It feels good to put in a ride like that as a team. Motivation will be high tomorrow to keep this going.”*

**Spain lie fifth** overall after Day Three and are still only a little over **seven minutes behind Italy**. **Finland slot into sixth**, albeit now eleven minutes behind the Spaniards, but are seven minutes ahead of **Chile in seventh**.

The remainder of the top ten sees **eighth-placed Belgium** lead **Canada in ninth** by one-minute-and-thirty-seconds, with **Mexico** just **five seconds behind Canada in tenth**.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/69df6c3026382890d017c919/6acff2a738bacf22d06c8fa7_be6f76e8-01bf-63b8-2ff4-b9e725648ec7.jpeg)

***Kevin Cristino at 2026 FIM 6DAYS® Portugal © Pole Position Communication***

**France and Italy go head-to-head in Junior World Trophy**

Holding their nerve during a challenging day, **France stand firm at the top of the Junior World Trophy** leaderboard. Despite continued pressure from **Italy in second**, they did **extend their lead by twelve seconds to forty-two seconds**. However, both teams will know that at this stage in the race, that advantage is minimal and any further mistakes from riders could see positions change hands.

As the **best-placed Junior rider**, Italy’s **Alberto Elgari** (TM MOTO) is determined to keep the pressure on France during these next three days of racing.

*“We’re not giving up,”* said **Elgari**. *“We’re here to fight the best we can. Both Kevin [Cristino] and Manuel [Verzeroli] are struggling a bit with injury, but they are still doing an incredible job.”*

The **United States complete the top three**, but are now three minutes behind Italy. With a **one-minute-and-forty-three-second advantage over Spain in fourth**, they have a little bit of breathing room on their rivals ahead of Day Four.

The **Czech Republic** close out the top five after Day Three and lead **sixth-placed Australia** by one-minute-and-thirty-seconds. **Chile** are riding strong for seventh. The fight for eighth is closely contested, with **Finland** heading **Sweden** in ninth by **only fifteen seconds**. **Host nation Portugal** round out the top ten.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/69df6c3026382890d017c919/6acff2a738bacf22d06c8fab_d51a74ff-ce41-40a6-61ba-c55f0f8add36.jpeg)

***Charlene Brisebard at 2026 FIM 6DAYS® Portugal © Pole Position Communication***

**Australia keep pressure on United States in MOTOREX Women’s World Trophy**

The battle for overall honours in the MOTOREX Women’s World Trophy category continues to be fought out between the **United States and Australia**. Today, there was little to separate each nation, with the **United States beating Australia for the win by just five seconds**.

With a **lead of four minutes** — the majority of which was gained on Day One — they still cannot shake off Australia. With **both nations chasing an elusive seventh 6DAYS® victory**, the fight for the top step of the podium will be exciting to watch throughout the remainder of the week.

Behind the United States and Australia, **France** are beginning to look comfortable in **third**. Although now thirty-three minutes behind the United States, they are in turn **almost eighteen minutes clear of New Zealand in fourth**. **Chile** complete the top five, and are only two minutes behind New Zealand and three minutes in front of **FIM LA in sixth**. **Canada and Brazil** are seventh and eighth, respectively.

After a strong start, **Portugal** saw their hopes of a medal result end, **dropping from fourth to ninth**. For team leader **Joana Goncalves** (Husqvarna) Day Three was bittersweet.

*“Today we lost a rider and hopes of a team result, but it’s how it goes sometimes,”* said **Goncalves**. *“So far it’s very difficult, with a lot of kilometres and hours riding on the bike. But I will keep fighting on and continue to give my best out there.”*

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/69df6c3026382890d017c919/6acff2a738bacf22d06c8fa3_a4e925b2-3071-9aa0-f2d9-069747a12df7.jpeg)

***Josep Garcia at 2026 FIM 6DAYS® Portugal © Future7Media***

**García and Verona continue to fight for outright individual honours**

Despite the reduced day of four timed special tests, Spain’s **Josep García** (KTM) still managed to **win the outright individual classification by eleven seconds**, further **cutting into the overall lead** of Italy’s **Andrea Verona** (KTM). Finishing **first and second**, **García** and **Verona** remain the **two most dominant riders** in this year’s 6DAYS®.

*“I’m still fighting to recover the time I lost on Day One,”* said **García**. *“It’s coming little by little; I will keep giving my best and push until the very end.”*

France’s **Zach Pichon** (TM MOTO) was once again a familiar face in **third**, but he had company, with **Davis** placing **just one second behind him in fourth**. **Girroir** rounded out the top five by **two-tenths of a second** from his teammate **Oliveira** in sixth.

Day Three marked **another Enduro1 class win for García**, with Sweden’s **Axel Semb** (Husqvarna) and **Max Ahlin** (Honda) separated by only five seconds in second and third, respectively.

**Verona** recorded his **third consecutive Enduro2 class victory** of the week, with **Pichon** and **Davis** rounding out the top three.

France’s **Julien Roussaly** (Sherco) **extended his Enduro3 class lead** with victory on Day Three. **Elgari** came home in second, with Great Britain’s **Brad Freeman** (Beta) taking third.

The United States’ **Brandy Richards** (KTM) secured her **third victory in the Women’s class**, while Australia’s **Danielle McDonald** (Yamaha) finished second to **move up to second overall**. A very impressive ride by New Zealand’s **Rachael Archer** (Yamaha) saw her finish **third**.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/69df6c3026382890d017c919/6acff2a738bacf22d06c8faf_c7b0b8f7-0cd9-23f6-9112-4db5d1b9bc11.jpeg)

***2026 FIM 6DAYS® Portugal © Pole Position Communication***

**Team Italy control the pace in Club Team Award**

Day Three saw **Team Italy strengthen their grip on the Club Team Award** category as they extended their race lead out to **three-minutes-and-twenty-six-seconds**. **Team Rabaconda** continue to hold onto second, with the **Missouri Mudders** completing the top three. A change in position sees **GTBN move up to fourth**, with **Motosport Bozkov 1** completing the top five.

**Gottbros Team lead the Veteran Club Team class**, with **Team Motokrosova Skola CZ** second and **Cone Valve Team-M.C. Sitge** third.

Tomorrow’s **Day Four** will see competitors complete **two laps of the Grândola to Santiago do Cacém loop** in the Alentejo region, with another **two-hundred-and-sixty kilometres** of riding expected.
