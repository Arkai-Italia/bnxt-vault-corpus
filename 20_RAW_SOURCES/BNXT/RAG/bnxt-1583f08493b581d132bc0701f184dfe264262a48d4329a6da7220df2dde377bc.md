# Vote for the 2021 AMA Racer of the Year Awards

* by [Joy Burgess](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/joybelle98/)
* December 28, 2021
* 0 Comments
* 3425 Views

By Joy Burgess

Dec. 28, 2021

It’s time to select the winners of the 2021 AMA Racer of the Year Awards, and if you’re an AMA member, you can have a voice.

Make your selections for AMA Athlete of the Year in both the National Championship Series and Grand Championships, AMA ATV Athlete of the Year, AMA Veteran/Senior Racer of the Year, AMA Youth Racer of the Year, and AMA Female Racer of the Year.

[maxbutton id=”23″]

Voting closes on Tuesday, Jan. 11, and winners will be announced shortly thereafter. Stay tuned to [www.americanmotorcyclist.com](http://www.americanmotorcyclist.com/) and the AMA’s social media channels for the final results.
