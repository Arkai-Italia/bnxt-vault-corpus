# Adventure Roads and Trails

WARNING

Entering your vehicle profile characteristics does not guarantee that your vehicle's characteristics will be accounted for in all route suggestions. Limitations may exist in map data for some route suggestions. Always defer to all posted road signs and conditions during vehicle operation. Failure to do so could lead to an accident involving personal injury or death.

This device can navigate off-highway roads and trails based on your vehicle profile. Trails  are color coded based on accessibility. You can select
 to set which adventure roads and trails appear on the map.
