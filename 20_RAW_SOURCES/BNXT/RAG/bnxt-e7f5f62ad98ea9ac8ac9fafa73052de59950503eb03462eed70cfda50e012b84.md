# Why a home 6DAYS® Portugal is proving special for Francisco Leite

October 15, 2026

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/69df6c3026382890d017c919/6ad0a6fa59fe99ea42d1e8b8_0fd0b90a-2031-4d21-a124-408f751e0902.jpg)

**At just twenty-one years old, Francisco Leite (Triumph) is representing Portugal on the Junior World Trophy team in this year’s 6DAYS® FIM Enduro of Nations.**

Putting on a national jersey for any edition of the 6DAYS® is always an exciting opportunity, but to do it at the hundredth edition of the longest-running off-road event on the FIM calendar on home soil makes it extra special.

For Leite, this is his fourth 6DAYS®, but already his clear favourite.

“*It’s really nice to be here,*” says Leite. *“This is actually my fourth 6DAYS®, but it’s my first time racing at home in Portugal. It feels crazy to see so many people here for this race.*

*“The support feels incredible. When you wear the national jersey in Portugal, it changes so much.*

*“People who you don’t even know are cheering for you to do well. This is something I love and I’m thankful to experience.”*

With the weight of his *nation behind him*, and urging him to do the best he can, Leite has been able to embrace that pressure despite his young age.

*“With three 6DAYS® behind me already, I feel more confident in what I am doing,” he says. “Having that extra experience is helping me to enjoy this race more.*

*“For sure, I know we are going to have some tough days, that is to be expected. Sometimes the key will not be full gas, but instead to be patient.*

*“But I feel like I can give my best for the team. Ultimately, my goal is to aim for a top fifteen result. If I can do that, I will have done everything I can.”*
