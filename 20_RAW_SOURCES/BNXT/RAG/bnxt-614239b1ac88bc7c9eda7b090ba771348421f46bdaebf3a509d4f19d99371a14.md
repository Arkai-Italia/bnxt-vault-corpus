# HEWC Confirms Calendar Updates for 2026 Season

29 April 2026·2 min read

### OFFICIAL STATEMENT

The FIM Hard Enduro World Championship (HEWC) confirms updates to the 2026 calendar, as part of its ongoing commitment to delivering a strong, balanced, and sustainable championship for all stakeholders.

## Abestone Event Rescheduled

Round 6 of the championship, originally scheduled as Abestone Rodeo Miravalle (Italy) on 10–12 July, will now take place on:
4–6 September 2026
The event will also be renamed Abestone Hard Enduro, while continuing under the same experienced organisation.
Located in the region of Tuscany, in the town of Abetone, the event will continue to build on its strong foundations, with significant local support and a passionate fanbase expected to attend.

## Wildwoods Extreme Withdrawn from 2026 Calendar

HEWC regrets to confirm that Wildwoods Extreme Genoa, originally scheduled for 12–14 September 2026, has been removed from the 2026 calendar.
Due to circumstances outside the control of both the local organiser and the championship promoter, it has not been possible to proceed with the event this season.
The championship would like to thank the Genoa organising team for their efforts and professionalism throughout the planning process and remains committed to working closely with them, with the aim of returning the event to the calendar in 2027.

## A Balanced and Sustainable Approach

HEWC acknowledges that calendar adjustments can impact riders, teams, organisers, and partners, particularly within a global championship environment where planning and logistics are critical.
These decisions are taken with careful consideration of the entire championship ecosystem, including riders competing across all categories.
The championship apologises for any inconvenience caused and appreciates the continued understanding and support of all stakeholders.

Looking Ahead

The 2026 season remains a strong and diverse championship, featuring world-class events across multiple continents.
Further updates regarding the calendar will be communicated through official HEWC channels when appropriate.

### ABOUT HEWC

The FIM Hard Enduro World Championship unites the world’s toughest off-road motorcycle races into one global series. Born from legendary events like the Roof of Africa and other iconic hard enduros, it brings together professional and amateur riders on the same demanding terrain, celebrating endurance, camaraderie, and adventure. Sanctioned by the Fédération Internationale de Motocyclisme (FIM) and promoted by Hard Enduro Promotions Marketing Management LLC, HEWC showcases the full potential of hard enduro as a modern, global motorsport platform.

### ABOUT THE FIM

The FIM (Fédération Internationale de Motocyclisme), founded in 1904, is the global governing body for motorcycle sport and the worldwide advocate for motorcycling. Recognised by the International Olympic Committee (IOC), the FIM oversees a wide range of world championships including MotoGP, Superbike, Motocross, Trial, Enduro, Cross-Country Rallies, Speedway, and Hard Enduro, while also working in areas such as public affairs, road safety, and touring.
