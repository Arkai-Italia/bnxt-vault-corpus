# EnduroGP France: Zach Pichon dominates at home to win Day 1 in Saint-Agrève

Published on July 17th, 2026 by Nicki Martinez

**Zach Pichon delivers a near-perfect opening day at the French GP. The TM MOTO Boano Factory Enduro Team rider wins eight of Saturday’s nine special tests to take the EnduroGP victory by 33 seconds over Josep Garcia, with Andrea Verona completing the podium.**

After winning Friday’s Akrapovic Super Test, Zach Pichon showed he is hitting peak form at exactly the right time in Saint-Agrève. The Frenchman dominated the opening day of the Polisport GP of France, round six and the penultimate stop of the Paulo Duarte FIM EnduroGP World Championship.

Pichon was fastest in eight of the nine special tests and ended the day with a comfortable 33-second advantage over Josep Garcia. Despite pushing hard, the Spaniard, who had been trying some new set-up settings during the opening day, couldn’t get close enough to challenge the Frenchman. Andrea Verona finished third, seven seconds behind his Red Bull KTM Factory Racing teammate.

Backed by huge numbers of French fans lining the special tests, Pichon hit the ground running by winning the Expotrade Cross Test before repeating the feat in the GMoto Enduro Test and Polisport Extreme Test to complete a perfect opening lap.

With an 11-second advantage over Steve Holcombe heading into lap two, Pichon swept all three tests again to keep extending his lead. Two more fastest test times on the final lap effectively sealed the victory before he finished the day with the third-fastest time in the final Extreme Test.

**Garcia fights back to second**

Behind Pichon, Holcombe initially held second place, but the pressure from the two factory KTMs continued to build. A mistake from the Brit in the Enduro Test allowed Josep Garcia through into second, a position the Spaniard held all the way to the finish.

Holcombe made another mistake in the final Cross Test and Andrea Verona took full advantage to move into third. The Italian also upped his pace on the final lap, leaving the Sherco rider unable to fight back for the podium.

Holcombe eventually finished fourth, while Julien Roussaly delivered his best result of the season with fifth in front of his home crowd. Hamish Macdonald was sixth, completing an impressive 4-5-6 for Sherco Racing Factory at their home GP.

**Pichon wins E2, Garcia remains unbeaten in E1**

Pichon’s overall victory also gave him the win in Enduro2, finishing 40 seconds ahead of championship leader Verona. Macdonald completed the class podium in third.

In Enduro1, Josep Garcia claimed his 11th consecutive victory of the season, moving another step closer to the title. Jeremy Sydow finished second, with Morgan Lesiardo third.

In Enduro3, Steve Holcombe made it four wins in a row, leading a Sherco one-two ahead of Julien Roussaly. Brad Freeman completed the podium for Beta Factory Racing.

**French one-two in Junior**

The home crowd had more to celebrate in the Tsubaki Junior Enduro class, where Thibault Giraudon claimed his first victory of the season.

The Fantic Factory Racing rider came out on top after a close battle between the French youngsters, beating Leo Joyon, who finished second for Triumph Factory Racing.

Alberto Elgari moved through late in the day to claim third, getting the better of Romain Dagna, who eventually finished fourth. Manuel Verzeroli completed the top five.

**Cagnoni takes Youth win**

In Putoline Oil Youth Enduro, Simone Cagnoni secured his first victory of the season after a hard-fought battle with Pietro Scardina.

The Team KTM Pro Racing Sport rider held his nerve on the final lap to take the win by 13 seconds over Scardina.

Yago Dominguez, part of the RFME Junior Team, completed the podium after finishing just six seconds behind the Italian, while Valentin Mersin and Gabin Allemand rounded out the top five.

**Jordi Quer tops the Open classes**

Spain’s Jordi Quer (KTM) took the Open 2 Strokes win, finishing 20 seconds clear of French rider Thomas Ravail (Sherco). Fellow Spaniard Jordi Galera (Rieju) rounded out the podium in third.

There was an all-French top three in Open 4 Strokes, where Antoine Faveyrial (KTM) claimed victory ahead of Alex Pichaud (Beta) and Enzo Vimond (Husqvarna).

The Open Senior podium had a distinctly Finnish flavour, with Juha Puotsaari (Husqvarna) taking the win from Arto Kallioinen (KTM) and Mika Paavola (Husqvarna).
