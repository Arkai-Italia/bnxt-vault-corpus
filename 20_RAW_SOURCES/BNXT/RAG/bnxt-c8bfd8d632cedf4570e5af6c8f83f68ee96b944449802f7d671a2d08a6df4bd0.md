# 2027 KTM 690 RALLY - GO OUT. MEET YOURSELF. | KTM United States

## 01. PRE-RIDE

GO FURTHER BETWEEN STOPS

### Dual Front Fuel Tanks

The KTM 690 RALLY features two additional front-mounted fuel tanks, adding 1.84 gallons (7 liters), working together with the rear tank to bring total fuel capacity to 5.28 gallons (20 liters). A dedicated fuel management system allows riders to actively switch between front and rear tanks via the handlebar-mounted switch cube, with the reserve light corresponding to the selected tank. This provides maximum flexibility and control when riding in remote terrain.

Reduced fatigue over long distances

### Rally Tower & Windshield

A purpose-built rally tower with integrated windshield improves wind protection at higher speeds and over long distances. This reduces fatigue during extended off-road stages and fast liaison sections.

Stay focused on the terrain

### 4.2” TFT Display & Connectivity

A 4.2" TFT display delivers clear access to ride modes, navigation, and bike information. The compact layout keeps key data visible without distracting from the terrain ahead.

Improved control in off-road conditions

### Rally Bodywork

Slim, rally-inspired bodywork is designed for rider movement and control, allowing easy transitions between seated and standing riding in technical terrain.
