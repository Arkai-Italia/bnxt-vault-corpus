# EnduroGP France: Zach Pichon sends Saint-Agrève wild with Akrapovic Super Test win

Published on July 16th, 2026 by Nicki Martinez

**Zach Pichon gets the French GP off to the perfect start. The TM MOTO Boano Factory Enduro Team rider wins the Akrapovic Super Test in Saint-Agrève ahead of Josep Garcia and Steve Holcombe, on an evening dominated by the home riders.**

The Polisport GP of France, round six and the penultimate stop of the Paulo Duarte FIM EnduroGP World Championship, is officially underway. And it kicked off in the best possible way for the home fans, with Zach Pichon taking victory in Friday evening’s Akrapovic Super Test.

Saint-Agrève delivered the atmosphere you would expect. Big crowds, a proper GP buzz and French fans fully behind their riders as the head-to-head runs unfolded. By the time the EnduroGP class lined up, the banks around the Super Test were packed and noisy.

One of the first riders to get the crowd going was Julien Roussaly, who put his Sherco Racing Factory machine at the top of the timesheets provisionally. But all eyes were on Pichon. And the Frenchman delivered.

The TM MOTO Boano Factory Enduro Team rider produced an almost faultless run to take over at the top, with only Andrea Verona and Josep Garcia still to come.

Garcia came closest to spoiling the French party. The Red Bull KTM Factory Racing rider pushed all the way to the line, but finished just 0.41 seconds behind Pichon. It wasn’t enough.

Once the time was confirmed, Saint-Agrève erupted. Pichon had landed the first blow on home soil, making it clear from Friday evening that he intends to be one of the main players this weekend.

Josep Garcia had to settle for second, while Steve Holcombe, riding for Sherco Racing Factory, completed the top three 3.32 seconds behind the winner.

The home success continued in the Tsubaki Junior Enduro class, where Leo Joyon, riding for Triumph Factory Racing, set the fastest time. Romain Dagna, from Team KTM Pro Racing Sport, finished second, with Clement Clauzier of Ambiance Moto third to complete an all-French top three in Junior.

If Pichon’s EnduroGP win was close, the Putoline Oil Youth Enduro class was even tighter. Pietro Scardina, from TM MOTO Costa Ligure, took victory by just 0.04 seconds over Simone Cagnoni, riding for Team KTM Pro Racing Sport.

There was Spanish interest in third place, with Hugo Cuesta (TM MOTO) edging out Yago Dominguez, from WP Eric Augé, for the final spot on the podium.
