### Vintage Motorcycle Days

Featuring America’s largest motorcycle swap meet, national championship vintage racing, bike shows, retro-oriented vendors, and so much more, AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days is a bucket-list event for vintage bike lovers everywhere.

**July 23-25, 2027**

**Mid-Ohio Sports Car Course**
**7721 Steam Corners Rd
Lexington, OH 44904**

### Vintage Racing

AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days offers national championship racing in numerous disciplines including: road racing, motocross, hare scrambles, trials, flat track, and more! Whether your passion is two-stroke dirtbikes soaring over jumps or first-gen Superbikes roaring through Thunder Valley, VMD delivers racing thrills for everyone.

[VMD Racing](https://vintagemotorcycledays.com/racing/)

### Vintage Events

There is no shortage of special events at AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days. Participate in the Lap For History, attend an informational seminar, check out a bike show, and explore the massive, 80-acre swap meet — we promise you won’t be bored for a moment at VMD!

[VMD Events](https://vintagemotorcycledays.com/events/)

### Get Involved

Do you want to become a part of the team that makes the VMD magic happen? Whether you want to support the event as a sponsor or volunteer your time on the ground, we’ve got opportunities for you. **All event proceeds benefit the AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame.**

[Get Involved](https://vintagemotorcycledays.com/about/)
