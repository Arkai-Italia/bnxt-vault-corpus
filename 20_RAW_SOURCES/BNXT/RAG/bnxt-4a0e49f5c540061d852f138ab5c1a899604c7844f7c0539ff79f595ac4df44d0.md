# Rallye du Maroc launches its YouTube channel

The **Rallye du Maroc** is expanding its digital presence with the launch of its official YouTube channel.

A dedicated platform to experience the rally from the inside: exclusive footage, stage highlights, driver interviews, behind-the-scenes content and much more. A true immersion into the heart of the race, where every detail matters and every story comes to life.

Whether you’re a long-time fan or new to rally raid, this channel offers a new way to feel the intensity, emotion and adventure of the event.
