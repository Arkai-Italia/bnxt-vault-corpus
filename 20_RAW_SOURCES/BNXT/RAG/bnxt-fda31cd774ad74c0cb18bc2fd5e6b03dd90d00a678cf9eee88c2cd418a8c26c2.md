# KTM’s 2027 300 EXC HARDENDURO is back – here’s what you get

Published on September 3rd, 2026 by Nye Davis

##### **KTM’s out-of-the-box extreme enduro machine is back for 2027, returning with all the usual bolt-on goodies and reworked suspension that keeps the model distinct from the common-or-garden 300 EXC.**

There’s a shiny new graphics kit for the new year, and that’s about it as far as new and exciting bits go. Production kicks off this month (September 2026, those reading this from the future), though pricing and regional availability remain under wraps for the time being.

The haaard enduro edition has been a fixture of KTM’s orange stable since 2024, when the company moved away from the Erzbergrodeo name previously used for its toughest 300 EXC.

The recipe hasn’t changed much since, that being to take the existing and very capable 300 EXC and give it a makeover with some strudy Power Parts bits, while also fettling the suspension and cooling to suit snotty, techy riding.

For 2027, KTM says the model receives an updated graphics package, helping stand it apart from both the standard EXC range and the 6DAYS bikes. The orange frame remains, as does the familiar black-and-orange colour scheme and a healthy scattering of bling bits throughout.

Beneath the new stickers is the same bike we got last year. Where suspension is concerned, that means the fitment of 48 mm WP XACT Closed Cartridge forks, complete with base valve preload adjusters, plus new (for 2026) damping settings and lighter, shorter springs, as well as a new pressure reservoir adapted to the new geometry of the spring.

.jpg)At the other end, KTM retains the WP XPLOR PDS rear shock, while the chassis gets black-anodised CNC-machined triple clamps and black-anodised engine hangers.

There’s also a Factory wheelset consisting of black Excel rims, black spokes, orange hubs and orange spoke nipples, wrapped in Metzeler 6 Days Extreme tyres with a soft-compound rear.

Protection is, predictably, a major part of the package. Customers get a skid plate, front brake disc guard, solid rear brake disc, rear brake safety wire, orange rear disc guard, clutch slave cylinder protection and an orange Factory frame protector set as standard, plus front and rear pull straps.

A radiator fan is fitted as standard, while the controls get a map-select switch and soft grey ODI lock-on grips. A black ribbed Diamante Factory seat and wrap-around handguards complete the add-ons.
