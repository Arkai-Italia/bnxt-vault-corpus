# The official figures

January 2
nd
2026
- 23:50
[GMT + 3]

**Dakar**

On completion of the administrative and technical scrutineering, **317** vehicles have been approved to participate in the 48th edition of the Dakar, amounting to a total of **564** riders, drivers and navigators, representing **49** nationalities. **115** motorbikes have been invited to take starter’s orders for the prologue on Saturday morning (**15** Rally GP, **100** Rally 2, including **26** Original by Motul), as well as **72** cars in the Ultimate class, **7** in the Stock class, **37** in the Challenger class, **41** in the SSV category and **45** trucks.

\*\*\* **Dakar Classic** For the 6th edition of the Dakar Classic, **97** vehicles will take starter’s orders for the race of consistency, which is also contested on dedicated routes: **75**cars and **22** trucks carrying a total of **214** competitors.

\*\*\* **Mission 1000** **7 vehicles** are taking part on dedicated routes in the Mission 1000 challenge, which is reserved for vehicles powered by alternative fuel or energy sources: **6** motorbikes and **1**truck.

\*\*\* **Saudi Next Gen** **5**SSV crews have been selected to take part in the academy.

\*\*\* In total, the Dakar bivouac is made up of **787** competitors riding or driving **421** vehicles enrolled in one or another of the rally’s categories (excluding Saudi Next Gen).

**The podium of nationalities** 1.    France (**188** competitors) 2.    Spain (**106** competitors) 3.    Italy (**68** competitors)

\*\*\* **W2RC** **182** vehicles ridden or driven by **253** competitors are enrolled in the first leg of the 2026 season’s FIA-FIM W2RC: **109** motorbikes (**9** Rally GP, **100** Rally 2) and **71** cars (**34** Ultimate, **5** Stocks, **18** Challenger, **14** SSV).
