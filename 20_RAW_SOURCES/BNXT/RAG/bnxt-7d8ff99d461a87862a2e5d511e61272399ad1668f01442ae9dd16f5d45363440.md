# 701 SUPERMOTO | 2027 - The essence of riding | Husqvarna Indonesia

### 01. A masterpiece

#### Engine

Redesigned crankcases reduce drag, which plays a vital role in enabling the single-cylinder engine to create more torque and peak power than the previous generation. Together with an extensive list of new or revised components and updated valve timing, the proven LC4 engine is compliant with the latest emissions standards and retains its linear and controllable power delivery. An added benefit of the latest engine are the service intervals, with oil changes now required at 15,000 km instead of the 10,000 km on the outgoing model.

#### Exhaust

The latest-generation 701 Supermoto is equipped with an improved exhaust system. Two lambda sensors, together with the catalytic converter - which has been relocated into the header pipe - control the emissions and ensure the machine is emissions compliant. This updated design allows riders to replace the silencer with one from the Technical Accessories range quickly and easily for enhanced performance.

#### Gearbox

The 6-speed gearbox is equipped with an optional Quickshifter+ that allows all gear changes to be made without using the clutch. The system uses two sensors, one of which is on the shift arm. When it detects movement on the gear lever, power is reduced for a fraction of a second. The second sensor is for gear selection, and once the next gear is engaged, full power returns. The Quickshifter+ delivers smoother acceleration, improved traction, and works even under heavy load.

#### Cylinder head

The compact, Single Overhead Camshaft (SOHC), 4-valve cylinder head features 42 mm intake valves and 34 mm exhaust valves. These are actuated by a reliable rocker arm system that delivers precise valve timing at high engine speeds. A dual-spark plug layout, with individually controlled ignition timing, ensures optimal fuel consumption.
