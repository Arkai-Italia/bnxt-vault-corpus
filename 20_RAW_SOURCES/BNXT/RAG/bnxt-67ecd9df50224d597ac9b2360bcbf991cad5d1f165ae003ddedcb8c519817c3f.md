## Join the AMA, Get involved!

##### [Members Discounts](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/members-community/members/deals-and-discounts/)

[Exclusive deals](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/members-community/members/deals-and-discounts/) with 40+ motorcycling retailers and services put money in your pocket. You’ll find ways to save and make your ride even sweeter.

##### [Volunteer](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/members-community/volunteer/)

Be part of the action. [AMA Volunteers](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/members-community/volunteer/) support events, rides, fundraising and advocacy—while having a ton of motorcycling fun.

##### [Find an Event](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/events/event-resources/find-an-event/)

Find world-class, [AMA racing opportunities near you](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/events/event-resources/find-an-event/). Browse over 2,000 AMA-sanctioned events across the country each year.

##### [Find a Club or Charter](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/organizers/charter/chartered-organizations-list/)

No matter how you ride, as an AMA member, [you’ll find other riders like you](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/organizers/charter/chartered-organizations-list/). An AMA community of riders has your back on the street or on the trail.

##### [Shop AMA Gear](https://www.amagear.com)

Visit [AMAGear.com](https://www.amagear.com) for AMA-branded apparel and swag. Watch for limited-edition AMA 100-Year Anniversary and ISDE merch!

##### [*American Motorcyclist*](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/magazine/)

As a member, you’ll receive 12 print issues of [*American Motorcyclist* magazine](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/magazine/) each year. Enjoy stories, history, and motorcycling photos.

## World-Class Events

### AMA-Sanctioned Events

The AMA sanctions thousands of competition and recreational events each year. Find a motorcycle event near you!

[AMA-SANCTIONED EVENTS](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/events/event-resources/find-an-event/)

### Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony

Honors motorcyclist’s most accomplished and significant people in a AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame induction celebration.

[HALL OF FAME INDUCTION CEREMONY](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/hall-of-fame/hall-of-fame-members/induction-ceremony/)

### Flat Track Grand Championship

The premier amateur flat track event in the U.S. and the only opportunity to earn an AMA National No. 1 plate in flat track.

[FLAT TRACK GRAND CHAMPIONSHIP](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/racing/track-racing/flat-track/)

### AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days

AMA Vintage Motorcycle Days is one of the biggest and best vintage motorcycle gatherings in the world. Don’t miss it!

[AMA VINTAGE MOTORCYCLE DAYS](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/events/scheduled-events/vintage-motorcycle-days/)

## AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame

###### Preserving the History and Spirit of Motorcycling

The AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame works to collect, record, preserve and honor the events, motorcycles and people who define the spirit of motorcycling in America. It specifically honors the lives of men and women who have influenced the growth and development of America’s motorcycle culture.

[More about the Hall of Fame](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/hall-of-fame)
