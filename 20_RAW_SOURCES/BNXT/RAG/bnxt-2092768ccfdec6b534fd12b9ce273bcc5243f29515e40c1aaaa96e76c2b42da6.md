# Introducing the Rallye du Maroc Classic

The **Rallye du Maroc** enters a new phase with the introduction of the **Classic** category, designed for those who see rally raid as more than just speed.

From 2026, historic vehicles will have their own dedicated competition, focused on regularity, navigation and race management. A format that rewards precision, terrain reading and the connection between driver and machine.

Run alongside the modern rally, the Classic shares the same environment while offering a different experience: more strategic, more authentic and deeply rooted in the origins of the discipline.

A new way to experience Morocco, where each stage becomes a journey through heritage, driving and adventure.
