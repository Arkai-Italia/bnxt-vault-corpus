# Tread - Overland Edition Tread XL - Overland Edition Owner's Manual - Checking for Messages

# Checking for Messages

When you send a message, your device listens for replies for 10 minutes. The device also checks for new messages every hour. When you are using the
inReach® tracking feature, your device automatically checks for messages at your tracking interval.

NOTE:
To receive messages, your device must have a clear view of the sky and be in view of a satellite when it checks for the messages.

You can force a check for messages by manually checking for messages, sending a message, or sending a track point. During a check, your device connects to satellites and receives messages waiting to be sent to your device.

Select
inReach > Check.
