# 2027 KTM 300 XC-W HARDENDURO - CHALLENGE THE EXTREME | KTM United States

## 01. ENGINE & PERFORMANCE

PURE 2-STROKE RESPONSE. MAXIMUM CONTROL.

### 2-Stroke Power & TBI

At its core, the KTM 300 XC‑W HARDENDURO delivers strong low-end torque and highly responsive power, making it ideal for technical hard enduro terrain where traction, precision, and control are critical. Equipped with advanced Throttle Body Injection (TBI) and a separate oil injection system, it ensures optimal fuel-air mixture and consistent performance across all conditions, reducing rider workload and ensuring reliable performance across varying altitudes and temperatures. Its lightweight design enhances agility, allowing riders to navigate tight, slow-speed technical sections with greater confidence and reduced fatigue.

RACE HARD. LAST LONGER.

### Race-Ready Drivetrain

The KTM XC‑W drivetrain is engineered for durability and efficient power transfer in extreme conditions, ensuring consistent drive and traction during clutch-heavy, low-speed riding where maintaining momentum is critical. On the KTM 300 XC-W HARDENDURO, this is complemented by a Supersprox stealth rear sprocket and additional HARDENDURO-specific components such as an orange oil plug and clutch slave cylinder protection, ensuring durability and reliability in the harshest environments.

PROTECTION WHEN IT GETS TOUGH

### HARDENDURO Protection Package

Designed for extreme terrain, the KTM 300 XC-W HARDENDURO adds a comprehensive protection setup including a radiator fan, skid plate, frame protection set, and front and rear brake disc guards. Additional HARDENDURO components such as black anodized engine hangers further improve durability and resistance, protecting critical areas of the bike during impacts, rock strikes, and repeated technical obstacles.
