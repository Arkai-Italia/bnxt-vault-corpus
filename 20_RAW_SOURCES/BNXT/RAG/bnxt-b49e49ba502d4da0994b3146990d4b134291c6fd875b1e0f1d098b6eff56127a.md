## *Balcony seating for the induction ceremony still available*

October 7, 2021

By Joy Burgess

The AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony will take place Oct. 15 in Pickerington, Ohio, at 7:00 p.m., and while premier seating for the event has reached full capacity, overflow seating for the ceremony is still available.

The AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Class of 2021 includes Dave Arnold, Nancy Davidson, Gary Denton, Tommy Hays, Loretta Lynn, Scott Plessinger, Kenny Tolbert and Ryan Villopoto.

For fans who want the chance to watch their motorcycle heroes accept this great honor, balcony seating is available. **Tickets are $40 and can be purchased by calling 1-800-342-5464.**

Your overflow seating ticket also gives you access to the invite-only cocktail hour, from 4:00 p.m. to 5:30 p.m., at the AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame museum prior to the induction, which will be held a short drive away. A coach will be provided.

In preparation for the event, the **AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame museum will be closed to the public after 1:00 p.m. on Friday, Oct. 15**.

Saturday, Oct. 16, there will be an open house at the AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame Museum, as well as the installation of the Class of 2021 exhibit. The museum opens at 9:00 a.m. and the Class of 2021 installation will take place at 10:00 a.m. This event is free and open to the public.
