# Red Bull KTM Extend Contract with Flat Track Racer Kody Kopp

* by [Joy Burgess](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/joybelle98/)
* September 6, 2022
* 0 Comments
* 2873 Views

September 6, 2022

By Kali Kotoski

KTM North America, Inc. has signed a two-year deal for AFT Singles Championship points leader Kody Kopp to remain with the Red Bull KTM Factory Racing team through the 2024 American Flat Track Championship.

“I am overjoyed to be extending my commitment with the Red Bull KTM Factory Racing team for another two years. This team has been nothing short of a dream to ride for – the bike suits my riding style and the team suits me personally,” said Kopp.

In his first season with the Red Bull KTM Factory Racing team, Kopp amassed six AFT Singles wins and a pair of runner-up finishes in 2022.

“I think Kody’s results really speak for themselves this season. He entered the team at 17 years old with only one full AFT season under his belt, so I would say he’s exceeded my expectations so far,” said Chris Fillmore, Manager of the Red Bull KTM Team. “The drive and talent are evident from watching him on the track and he’s also quite mature and thorough away from the racetrack.”
