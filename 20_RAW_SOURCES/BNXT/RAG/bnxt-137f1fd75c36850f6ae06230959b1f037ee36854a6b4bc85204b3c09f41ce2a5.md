# RallyMoto GPS Power Lead Fitting - RallyMoto

**RallyMoto GPS Power Lead Fitting Guide**

RallyMoto has developed its own GPS tracker system and invested in very high accuracy tracking devices to determine the accuracy of the course ridden by a rider and to improve safety for our events, which usually take place over long distances and in remote terrain.

GPS tracking will become a standard part of what we do, whether it is Adventure Cannonball ride outs or on our large events.

You will need to purchase a GPS Power Lead for events. <https://rallymoto.co.uk/product/rallymoto-gps-tracker-power-cable/>

**Fitting your GPS power lead**

* The GPS power lead must have a 12V power supply from your bike, whether the ignition is on or off.
* The lead should be fitted directly to the battery if possible  (the red wire is the + connection) – you may wish to add an in-line fuse to the GPS power lead.
* DO NOT CONNECT TO A USB POWER SUPPLY – this is only a 5V output and will not power the GPS tracker
* The cable should then be routed through to the handle bar area, where the water-proof SuperSeal connector should be easily accessible allowing the organisation team to simply connect your GPS tracker and cable tie it to the handle bars or loosely to the cables.

**FIT YOUR GPS POWER LEAD BEFORE YOU TURN UP AT THE START**
