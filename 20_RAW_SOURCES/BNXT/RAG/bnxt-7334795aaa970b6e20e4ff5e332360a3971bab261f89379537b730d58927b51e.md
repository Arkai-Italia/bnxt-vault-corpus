# 2027 FIM EnduroGP calendar announced: eight rounds and Isle of Man debut

Published on August 9th, 2026 by Nicki Martinez

##### **The provisional 2027 EnduroGP World Championship calendar has been announced, with eight rounds scheduled between April and October. Spain, Portugal and Greece will each host two events, Italy gets one round, while the big new addition is the Isle of Man, which joins the championship for the first time.**

Prime Stadium Events, promoters of the Paulo Duarte FIM EnduroGP World Championship, together with the FIM, have unveiled the provisional schedule for 2027, with the series once again heading to some of Europe’s traditional enduro strongholds.

Spain will kick off the season with two Grands Prix in April. Valverde del Camino will make its EnduroGP debut on April 2-4, before Oliana returns on April 23-25 after hosting successful rounds in 2025 and 2026.

June sees the championship head to Fafe, Portugal, for two consecutive weekends of racing. Rounds three and four will take place on June 11-13 and June 18-20, with separate courses and special tests planned for each GP.

Round five takes EnduroGP to Carpinetti, Italy, on July 2-4, before one of the most anticipated additions to the 2027 calendar.

#### **Isle of Man joins EnduroGP**

From July 30 to August 1, the EnduroGP World Championship will visit the Isle of Man for the first time, with Douglas hosting round six.

It is a significant addition to the series, taking EnduroGP to one of the most famous motorcycling destinations in the world.

A long summer break then follows before Greece hosts the final two rounds of the season in October.

EnduroGP will return to Greece for the first time since 2019, with back-to-back events on October 15-17 and October 22-24. The exact venues are still to be confirmed, with the 2027 world titles set to be decided there.

#### **Provisional 2027 EnduroGP calendar**

Round 1 – GP of Spain: Valverde del Camino | April 2-4
Round 2 – GP of Spain II: Oliana | April 23-25
Round 3 – GP of Portugal: Fafe | June 11-13
Round 4 – GP of Portugal II: Fafe | June 18-20
Round 5 – GP of Italy: Carpinetti | July 2-4
Round 6 – GP of Isle of Man: Douglas | July 30-August 1
Round 7 – GP of Greece: TBA | October 15-17
Round 8 – GP of Greece II: TBA | October 22-24

#### **Six rounds for the Women’s Enduro World Championship**

The Expotrade FIM Women’s Enduro World Championship will feature six rounds in 2027.

The women’s championship will begin in Oliana before taking in both Fafe rounds, the Isle of Man and the final two GPs in Greece.

#### **2027 Women’s Enduro World Championship calendar**

Round 1 – GP of Spain II: Oliana | April 23-25
Round 2 – GP of Portugal: Fafe | June 11-13
Round 3 – GP of Portugal II: Fafe | June 18-20
Round 4 – GP of Isle of Man: Douglas | July 30-August 1
Round 5 – GP of Greece: TBA | October 15-17
Round 6 – GP of Greece II: TBA | October 22-24
