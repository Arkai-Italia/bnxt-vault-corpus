# Pro Bike Gallery: First look at Billy Bolt’s 2026 Husqvarna Factory Racing TE 300

Published on April 14th, 2026 by Enduro21 Team

##### First look Pro Bike gallery of Billy Bolt’s 2026 Husqvarna Factory Racing TE 300 TBI two-stroke all prepped and ready for Hard Enduro World Championship round one at Alestrem in France this weekend.

Following hot on the heels of World Champion Mani Lettenbichler’s 2026 race bike reveal, Husqvarna Factory Racing have done the same with Billy Bolt’s saucy TE 300 two-stroke (photo shooting happened in the same place).

It’s our first proper look at the race bike Bolt will race in the HEWC series this season, plus select major events including Erzberg and Romaniacs. It’s a year full of promise for the SueprEnduro World Champion, not just because is a new dad, but because he arrives fit and with ample prep time to take on Mani.

We’re still scouring the bike for clues of any differences over last year and over standard TE 300 models. We’re liking the new colours, the vertical block of yellow down the side radiator panel looks good to us. Check out the updated Moto Master brake rotors and the slinky-shaped top engine mount.

Development on the TBI, fuel injected two-strokes continues they tell us, perfecting the beast further despite the unrivalled set of results in the HEWC, Erzberg and Romaniacs with Lettenbichler on the KTM version.

Bolt will no doubt be looking to continue the Austrian dominance but end his German rivals as the season begin in Ales this weekend, April 17-19.

This just a feast your eyes gallery until we can get to the races for a more in-depth “Pro Bike”.
