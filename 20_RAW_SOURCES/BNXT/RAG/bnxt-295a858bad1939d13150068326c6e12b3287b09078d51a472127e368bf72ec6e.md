# AMA Congratulates 2026 Champions of AMA NATC National MotoTrials

* by [AMA Tech](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/amatechama-cycle-org/)
* October 15, 2026
* 0 Comments
* 32619 Views

**Photo Credit: Steph Vetterly**

PICKERINGTON, Ohio (Oct. 15, 2026) — The American Motorcyclist Association congratulates the champions of the 2026 AMA NATC National MotoTrials, which wrapped up with the Ohio Buckeye National in Toronto, Ohio.

During the Ohio Buckeye National, TRRS’s Jorge Casales secured his sixth win of the season and clinched his second consecutive NATC Pro Championship, capping off a dominant season. Additionally, GasGas’ Maddie Hoover won the Women’s class, winning all eight rounds of the season, while Beta’s Cole Cullins claimed the Expert class title.

“Jorge, Maddie and Cole showcased the necessary skill, demeanor and heart to be champions this year, each showcasing their trials prowess en route to their respective championships,” AMA Deputy Director of Racing Mike Burkeen said. “Congratulations to all of the champions and thank you to all of the riders who helped make this another successful year of the AMA NATC National MotoTrials series.”

The 2026 AMA NATC National MotoTrials season spanned eight rounds across four states, with each venue providing a distinct challenge for the competitors.

For more information about the AMA NATC National MotoTrials, visit [*www.mototrials.com/*](https://www.mototrials.com/).
