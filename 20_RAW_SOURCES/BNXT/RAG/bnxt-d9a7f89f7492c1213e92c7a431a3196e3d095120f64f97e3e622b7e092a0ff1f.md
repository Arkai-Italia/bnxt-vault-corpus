# Tread Owner's Manual - My Garmin PowerSwitch will not stay connected

# My Garmin PowerSwitch will not stay connected

* Verify the
  Garmin PowerSwitch™ device is receiving power.
* Verify that
  Bluetooth® technology is enabled on your paired navigation device or smartphone.
* Move the navigation device or smartphone closer to your
  Garmin PowerSwitch device.
* Verify that your vehicle's battery is supplying at least 12 V of power.

  The
  Garmin PowerSwitch device turns off all outputs automatically to preserve your vehicle's battery if it is receiving less than 11 V of power ([My accessories are not receiving power](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-700B8BF2-F8BB-42A2-A617-3CC00CB4CCA0/EN-US/GUID-4F224017-6FA9-4E28-A05D-B5D3660ABFCE.html)).
