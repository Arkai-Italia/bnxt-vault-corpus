### [Motoklik - Video Installation Instructions - KTM, GasGas and Husqvarna](https://www.motoklik.com/installation-instructions/#)

The front magnet holder now includes a 3mm bolt that is used to secure the arm of the magnet holder in place. To fit the 3mm bolt, attach the front magnet holder as per the video, and then drill a 3mm hole through the fork guard using the magnet holder as a guide (green arrow). When the hole is drilled through the fork guard, pass the supplied 3mm hex head bolt through and tighten into the supplied nyloc nut, using the magnet holder recess as a retainer for the nut.

The Rear Magnet Holder for 2023 KTM and Husqvarna models is a new design. This design requires the rear magnet holder to be pushed in from the side, between the brake line, and the brake line holder. The brake line holder does not need to be removed to install the rear magnet holder. There is a cable tie slot in the new rear magnet holder that allows a cable tie to be pushed through the part, and wrapped around the brake line to secure the rear magnet holder in place.

The rear magnet holder for 2022 and previous KTM, GasGas and Husqvarna models has been updated to make it easier to fit. The rear brake line clamp no longer needs to be removed as per the video. The rear magnet holder simply fits in place on the green area highlighted in the image below, and then cable tied in position.
