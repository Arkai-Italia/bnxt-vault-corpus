# Tread Owner's Manual - Transmitting Your Voice

# Transmitting Your Voice

Before you can transmit your voice, you must pair your
Tread® device with a compatible
Bluetooth® headset.

You can transmit your voice over the radio using a compatible
Bluetooth headset.

Select an option:

* From the main menu, select
  Apps > Adventure > Radio > Push to Talk.
* Use the voice dial feature on your connected
  Bluetooth headset ([Enabling Push-to-Talk with a Headset](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-700B8BF2-F8BB-42A2-A617-3CC00CB4CCA0/EN-US/GUID-CA7FA289-39DA-4A4F-A8A1-5A9107722AD3.html)).

  TIP:
  You can press the pause or stop button to manually close the radio.

  NOTE:
  You must enable the
  Push to Talk with Headset setting to use this feature ([Radio Settings](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-700B8BF2-F8BB-42A2-A617-3CC00CB4CCA0/EN-US/GUID-F515806E-DABE-4199-AA92-0DBE035F67B0.html)).
