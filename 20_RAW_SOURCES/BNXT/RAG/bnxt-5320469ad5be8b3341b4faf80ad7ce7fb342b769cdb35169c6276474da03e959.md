# Former KTM marketing boss joins Gravity Sports Group

Published on August 4th, 2026 by Enduro21 Team

#### Gravity Sports Group has appointed former KTM Global Marketing boss Riaan Neveling as its new Director of Sales and Marketing, seeing the South African taking up the role from July 2026.

Neveling will lead the global sales and marketing strategy across the group's portfolio of off-road brands, including O'NEAL, ONE Industries and KINI Red Bull.

"I've always believed that strong brands are built through authentic passion, meaningful relationships, and unforgettable experiences," said Neveling. "Joining Gravity Sports Group presents an incredible opportunity to help shape the future of some of the most iconic brands in motocross and action sports."

He arrives after a lengthy spell at KTM in Mattighofen, where he most recently headed the Austrian manufacturer's global marketing operation. In that role, he oversaw worldwide brand and product initiatives for KTM, Husqvarna Motorcycles, GASGAS, WP Suspension and the company's motorsport communications, while also managing licensing and trademarks across the KTM Group.

Away from marketing, Neveling has a background in motocross, supermoto and superbike racing, and has also supported high-profile rally raid campaigns.

He helped Botswana's "Kalahari Ferrari" Ross Branch secure sponsorship and build the foundations of his career, while also supporting fundraising efforts for Joey Evans' bid to complete the Dakar Rally a decade after the racing accident that left him paralysed from the chest down – a journey documented in his best-selling book *Para to Dakar*.
