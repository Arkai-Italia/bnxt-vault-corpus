## before you start FIVE TOOLS

#### Brake cleaner spray

Degrease every plastic part with it, new or used. New plastics often carry silicone residue from production.

#### Squeegee (plastic)

A plastic squeegee presses the vinyl on firmly, moving from the middle outwards. Metal tools are out: they mark both the plastic and the laminate surface.

#### Heat gun, low setting

You need a heat gun: a hair dryer cannot get the adhesive active or the vinyl pliable on curves. Use it on a low setting on curves, corners and edges, and take care around the fuel tank and lines.

#### Craft knife or sharp scissors

For trimming and for cutting the backing paper where the guide asks for it.

#### Clean lint-free cloth

Wipe the plastics with it after degreasing and keep skin oils off the cleaned surface. A household paper roll works too; ordinary cloth leaves fibres under the vinyl that weaken the bond.

## the fitting DRY. HEAT GUN. CENTRE OUT.

Backyard Design kits go on dry: degrease, align, then press down from the centre outwards with the heat gun on low. Never use a hair dryer, and work one panel at a time.

Finish with another pass of heat over every edge, then leave the graphics to rest for 24 hours, ideally at room temperature, before you ride.

Watch the video guide once before you start and keep the checklist close. Take your time, since a careful fitting lasts a whole season.
