# About — suspension data acquisition from Brescia — BYB Tech

About BYB Tech

# Measuring what a rider feels but cannot name

BYB Tech is an Italian company that builds suspension data acquisition hardware and software. Its sensors record what a fork and shock actually do through a run, so a setup decision rests on a measurement rather than on a memory of the last lap.

Run data, read in the pits at a World Cup round.

BYB Tech SRL was founded in Brescia in 2019 and designs and builds every system in Italy. The hardware fits air and coil springs alike, and records the whole run rather than sampling moments of it.

## What BYB Tech makes

BYB Tech makes three product lines across four products. Telemetry V3 and Telemetry V3 Pro record fork and shock movement. Chrono records lap and run times. Focus is a riding light. Between them the range covers mountain bike, motocross, quad, car and road bike.

## How BYB Tech got here

BYB Tech started as a Kickstarter campaign in December 2018 that raised $38,000, and delivered its first 50 systems the following year. Three hardware generations later, the range covers mountain bike, motocross, quad and car.

1. Year: 2018

### The first campaign

A Kickstarter campaign for a suspension data acquisition system for mountain bikes opens in December and raises $38,000.
2. Year: 2019

### BYB Tech SRL, and the first 50 units

The company is founded in Brescia and ships the first 50 systems to the riders who backed them.
3. Year: 2020

### A second generation, and a second discipline

Telemetry 2.0 replaces the original hardware, a motocross system follows, and BYB Tech starts taking consultancy work for manufacturers.
4. Year: 2021

### World Cup

BYB Tech hardware is used in World Cup downhill competition.
5. Year: 2022

### Focus

6. Year: 2023

### Chrono

7. Year: 2024

### Telemetry V3 Pro

The third generation arrives, and it starts at the top: Telemetry V3 Pro ships first.

### Telemetry V3

## Who builds it

BYB Tech is an engineering company in Brescia, northern Italy, founded by a downhill national champion with an engineering background. It has designed and built every system since.

Since 2020 the same engineers have taken consultancy work, across several fields. Some of it extends the products for a manufacturer or a use case the catalogue does not cover. The rest has nothing to do with suspension at all, and is built on the same discipline: instrument it, then decide.

* ### Customisation on the products

Work that takes Telemetry or Chrono past the standard product — for a manufacturer, a team, or a measurement the catalogue does not cover.
* ### Industrial IoT and Industry 4.0

Sensing, acquisition and connectivity outside sport entirely, for companies with a measurement problem of their own.
