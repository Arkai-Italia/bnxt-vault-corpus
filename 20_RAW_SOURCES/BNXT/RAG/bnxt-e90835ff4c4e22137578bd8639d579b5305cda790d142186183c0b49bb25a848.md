# Dakar Competitors/Teams Guidelines

## **RULES FOR USING THE SHIELD**

- **The “Dakar” name and logo (cheich) are protected trademarks and the exclusive property of the Dakar organiser. Their use is therefore strictly limited to:**

- **the Dakar organiser**

- **the Dakar official sponsors and licensees**

- **the Competitors/Teams solely within the framework of the Shield.**

- **The** **<u>Shield</u> is specifically dedicated to the Teams and Competitors, within the terms and conditions of these Guidelines and Regulations for the event.**

- **The logo (cheich) may only be used within the context of the Shield, and in no way on its own.**

- **Commercial use of the “Dakar” name, the logo (cheich) and the Shield by the Competitors/ Teams is strictly forbidden.**

### DEFINITIONS

|COMMUNICATION|Information intended for promoting an initiative, a brand image etc.|
|---|---|
|COMPETITOR|An individual participating in the Dakar as a driver, navigator or race mechanic.|
|MANUFACTURER|Manufacturer of cars/motorcycles/trucks participating in the 2026 edition of the Dakar
via a Team’s registration.|
|PROMOTION|Highlighting the commitment/participation.|
|TEAM|A unit formed to participate in the 2026 edition of the Dakar, in accordance with the
race regulations. This unit brings together one or more Competitors as well as any
individuals dedicated to the latter (**Assistance**: manager, sports director, mechanic,
sports therapist, etc.**Media**: community manager and other media officers), all those
officially registered or applying for registration, on 1<sup>st</sup>November 2025, to compete
in the 2026 edition of the Dakar, with competition vehicles and, where required,
assistance vehicles.|
