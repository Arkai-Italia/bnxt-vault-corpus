## A practical record-keeping loop

A reminder is useful only when it is connected to the correct bike, last completed work, mileage, and source interval.

1. **1. Verify.** Check the model-specific manual and current odometer.
2. **2. Inspect.** Look for condition, wear, leaks, damage, or overdue work.
3. **3. Service safely.** Follow the correct procedure or use a qualified workshop.
4. **4. Record.** Save the date, mileage, work, parts, cost, and next due point.

## Motorcycle maintenance FAQ

How often should a motorcycle be serviced?+

There is no reliable universal interval. Follow the schedule for the exact model and year, including both mileage and time limits, and shorten it when the manual specifies severe-use conditions.

Which checks should happen before a ride?+

At minimum, inspect the items specified by the motorcycle manufacturer. A practical walk-around commonly includes tires and wheels, controls, lights, fluid leaks, brakes, and the final-drive condition.

Can an app replace the service manual?+

No. Vojo can organize records and reminders, but the official manual remains the authority for specifications, intervals, procedures, and safety warnings.

When should I stop and use a mechanic?+

Stop riding and seek qualified help when a safety-critical issue is suspected, the manual requires specialized work, or you cannot verify a repair.
