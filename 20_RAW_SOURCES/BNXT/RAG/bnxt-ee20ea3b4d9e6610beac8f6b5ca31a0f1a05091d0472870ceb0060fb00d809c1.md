### Who we are?

##### Sport UP Agency run by Tomasz Gagat, originator and organizer of sports events with 20+ years of experience.

**Sport UP Agency** is a dynamic company specializing in organizing prestigious sports events for years. The **FIM SuperEnduro World Championships** stand out as one of the most thrilling and spectacular events in its extensive portfolio.

Under the leadership of **Tomasz Gagat**, **Sport UP Agency** is responsible not only for ensuring the smooth running of the event but also for creating an unforgettable atmosphere for athletes, fans, and media alike.

**Sport UP Agency** goes beyond just handling the technical and logistical aspects of the event; it also focuses on its promotion and visibility. Through strategic partnerships and effective marketing campaigns, we believe that the **FIM SuperEnduro World Championships** will capture the attention of millions of fans worldwide.
