5 button switch for scrolling the roadbook and adjusting the odometer on a TowerOne.  Large buttons, ergonomically placed. The body and clamp are aluminum. The cable exits the rear of the switch to avoid pinching on the clutch master cylinder clamp.

This switch is only suitable for connecting to a TowerOne. If you would like a version that can be used with other digital rally devices or motorized paper roadbook please contact [service](mailto:customerservice@icoracing.com).
