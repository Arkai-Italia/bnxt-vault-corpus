# XE 125

XE 125

For years one of the most popular bikes among young riders and an absolute benchmark in its class, the XE 125 continues to be a major contender in the Enduro Youth World Championship thanks to the victories of Riccardo Pasquato and Valentin Mersin.

The latest evolution of Fantic’s “eighth-litre” machine, heir to three consecutive world titles (2021, 2022, 2023), arrives with targeted aesthetic and regulatory updates that further refine its character without compromising the performance and technical balance that have made it such a success.

**Factory Feeling in a 125 Version**

The compact yet incredibly powerful **125 cc 2-stroke engine of the XE** 125 takes you straight into the heart of the Fantic Factory Feeling. Technologies and performance derived from the experience of factory riders are now combined with even greater consistency, thanks to the new cooling system developed in-house by Fantic.

Fantic’s smallest Enduro model continues to evolve and, like the entire Enduro range, is now fully compliant with Euro 5+ regulations.

**Refined Technical Specifications**

**The 125 cc 2-stroke engine delivers outstanding performance,** optimized by the Fantic Racing ECU and complemented by a high-performance exhaust system developed in collaboration with Arrow, ensuring a responsive and aggressive power delivery at all times. The new radiators provide a 35% increase in cooling efficiency, making them ideal for tackling steep trails, slow-speed sections, and technical terrain in extreme conditions. **The result is an even higher level of performance** combined with uncompromising reliability, even in the most demanding situations. Get ready for an unparalleled riding experience.

The XE125 **comes standard with two engine mappings:**

**Hard**, designed to deliver maximum performance;

**Soft**, which retards ignition timing for smoother and more controllable power delivery, while still preserving the adrenaline of full power.

**Instant Power Delivery, Absolute Control**

Completing the engine **development are the Arrow-developed exhaust and the optimised Fantic ECU**, a combination that ensures power delivery that is immediate, responsive and consistently punchy.

The chassis remains one of the XE125’s strongest assets: **the ultralight aluminum frame,** **Kayaba suspension** and carefully engineered geometry provide total control on any terrain, ensuring precision and safety even in the most technical sections.

**Enhanced Ergonomics, Total Freedom**

The ergonomics follow the winning layout of the XE300, **introducing new slim plastics and a redesigned 9-litre fuel tank to provide even greater freedom of movement. Access to the air filter has been simplified,** making maintenance quicker and more efficient, even during the most demanding rides.

**Style: Iconic, Modern, Unmistakable**

On the aesthetic side, **the XE125 introduces a new headlight mask derived from the TL Enduro-Motard line,** now paired with a next-generation LED headlight: an upgrade that ensures greater visibility, a more modern look, and significant weight savings.

**The new Fantic** liveries recall the brand’s golden age, **reinterpreting the iconic red, white and blue colours in a contemporary way.** The chrome base adds a distinctive, visually striking touch, enhancing the bike’s lines and making it instantly recognisable on any terrain.
