# Find the group. Understand the route. Go ride.

Vojo brings nearby motorcycle rides, route details, group information, and ride creation into one focused iOS experience.

## Built around the records and decisions riders actually make

### Find nearby group rides

Browse upcoming rides and narrow the feed by distance and date before deciding to join.

### Understand the ride first

Review the route, timing, pace, motorcycle category, and available places in one view.

### Create your own route

Set a start, destination, date, group details, and ride expectations for the community.

### Open external navigation

Move from the ride details to your preferred supported navigation app when it is time to leave.

Vojo helps organize information. Maintenance decisions and safety-critical work should still be verified against the official manual or with a qualified motorcycle mechanic.

## Frequently asked questions

Can I create a motorcycle ride in Vojo?+

Yes. You can define the route, timing, group size, pace, bike category, and optional participant details.

Can I filter rides by distance?+

Yes. With location access, Vojo can show rides within a radius you choose.

Does Vojo provide turn-by-turn navigation?+

Vojo helps organize the ride and route, then can open supported external navigation apps for navigation.
