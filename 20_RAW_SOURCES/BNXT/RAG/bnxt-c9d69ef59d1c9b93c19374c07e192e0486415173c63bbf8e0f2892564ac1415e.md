During the VARG MX 1.2 development we carefully benchmarked and analyzed the current best-performing motocross bikes and used some of the best athletes in the world for real life testing. This methodology led us to craft a chassis that provides the ideal amount of vertical, lateral, and torsional flex to give a predictable, stable, and comfortable ride.
The VARG MX 1.2 frame features a significant improvement by increased vertical frame flex in the rear shock mount area and an improved lateral flex in the front area of the chassis. This leads to a plusher feel, better cornering grip and control.
Some of the world's most celebrated motocross athletes have put the chassis through the most extreme testing conditions, helping Stark achieve the best performance, comfort and durability. And thereby giving you a better ride.
The Stark VARG features a low, optimized center of gravity near the rider's lower leg position, making the bike feel significantly lighter and more agile. This design ensures optimal weight distribution, delivering an unmatched featherweight riding experience. Drawing on expertise from engineers, designers, and renowned motorcycle designer Tony Foale, the team developed an ultralight tubular chromoly steel frame. The VARG’s rear axle and swingarm feature two remarkable innovations.

Firstly, an advanced chain adjuster for precise and accurate chain adjustment. The adjuster block incorporates a spring and shaft, which at every 90 degrees rotation gives you an audible click feedback. Using a hex driver you can easily turn the same number of clicks on each side and perfectly align your wheel with optimal chain tension.

Stepping it up another notch we introduced a flush rear axle design that eliminates protruding nuts and bolts that can possibly catch on ruts, dirt or rocks -reducing risk while enhancing your riding experience and handling.

* Chain adjuster with clicks enables hassle free adjustment. You no longer need to use two wrenches, simply one hex.
