# Testing Every 2026 KTM Enduro Model at KTM Off-Road Experience

Published on April 3rd, 2026 by Enduro21 Team

##### Enduro21 goes for the full KTM experience testing every 2026 enduro model at the Alex Snow KTM Off-Road Experience, a training school and off-road riding centre based in the South West of England but with locations and tours across the UK and Europe.

The ‘school’ has a full fleet of KTM Enduro models (125 + 250 XC-W, 300 EXC, 250, 350, 450 and 500 EXC-F) plus will be doing the same orange Adventure bikes in the very near future.

For us it was about having a blast with mates and spending a day trail riding with no pressure, learning a little along the way. This Experience is for every rider basically and they work with anyone from first time off-road riders all the way up to Championship level racers, either in groups or one-to-one.

Enduro21 wrote more about our KTM Off-Road Experience day here: [Tested: KTM Off-Road Experience – Riding 2026 KTM EXC Range](https://enduro21.com/en/news/tested-alex-snow-ktm-off-road-experience-training-school)
