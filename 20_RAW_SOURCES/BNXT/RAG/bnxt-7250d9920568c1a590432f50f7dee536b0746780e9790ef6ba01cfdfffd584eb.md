# RFME Sponsorship information

To be a sponsor of the **Royal Spanish Motorcycle Federation (RFME)** is to be directly connected with the world of sports and its values. Among all national and international federations, the Spanish federation stands out as one of the most powerful in terms of media impact, endorsement, and reach. Spanish motorcycling is a global benchmark in all its modalities.

Our **100-year history** of creating world champions in all categories has made us **the federation with the most world titles of all time**, and we can provide a unique platform for mutually beneficial initiatives. We are powerful in streaming on YouTube and social networks in the Spanish market, also working together with our most representative pilots creating shared publications.

We can adapt to all your needs in our country. We have **digital or traditional advertising in streaming and on the track** in all our 19 categories **-SBK, Motocross, Enduro, Trial, Tourism-.** Our prices range starts with a web banner to the global sponsorship of the federation. We also create video, photography and text content, also functioning as digital media.

The RFME is a perfect asset to represent a brand. We offer a comprehensive range of advertising formats available in the market to ensure that your brand is represented in the best possible way.
