# VIDA unveils DIRT.E K3 6DAYS® Limited Edition at the 100th FIM International Six Days’ Enduro

October 15, 2026

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/69df6c3026382890d017c919/6ad13d3e38e0b913afd7a731_efbe0bb7-273d-217a-8c94-4098cc4e3616.png)

**VIDA, powered by Hero, unveiled the DIRT.E K3 6DAYS® Limited Edition** today in Grândola, Portugal, at the 100th Fédération Internationale de Motocyclisme (FIM) International Six Days Enduro (ISDE). Limited to an exclusive production run of just 100 units, each electric off-roader honours a specific edition of the world-renowned event, celebrating a legacy of endurance that began in 1913. **VIDA is the first Indian electric OEM brand to collaborate with ISDE, appearing in official partnership with the FIM.** The 6DAYS® Limited Edition carries official 6DAYS® branding , green and red accents taken from the flag of the host nation, Portugal, and race-inspired competition typography.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/69df6c3026382890d017c919/6ad13cc7da58513e91ef9f19_ee29924f-b5a8-43fb-ec97-0132900ed4ad.png)

**A YOUTH OFF-ROADER THAT ADJUSTS AS THE RIDER GROWS**
**The VIDA DIRT.E K3 is an electric off-roader for young riders,** introduced in December 2025 as the first model in VIDA’s off-road product line. Developed under the philosophy of *“Wild Beyond the Ride”,* what makes the VIDA DIRT.E K3 truly groundbreaking is its modular design: **it is built to grow physically and functionally alongside young riders.** It can be adjusted at three critical points: the handlebar height, the footrest position, and the seat height. Without the need for any special tools, these adjustments ensure the rider maintains perfect posture at every stage of their physical development.

**Through a connected app, users can monitor usage, set speed limits, and manage riding modes.** Three ride modes with thumb-throttle control offer speeds of 9 km/h in Mode 1, 17-18 km/h in Mode 2, and 24 km/h in Mode 3. Safety features include a magnetic kill switch that cuts power if the rider separates from the bike, and a 60 mm rear disc brake. A removable 360 Wh lithium-ion battery charges from 0 to 100 per cent in approximately three hours.
**The DIRT.E K3 is a Red Dot Design Award 2025 winner and a CES 2026 Innovation Award Honoree.**

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/69df6c3026382890d017c919/6ad13d13f8f25e0824fc52e8_827a6d53-3665-d668-2b99-b820ab921125.jpg)

***DIRT.E K3 © Vida***

Commenting on the announcement, **Kausalya Nandakumar, Chief Business Officer – Emerging Mobility Business Unit, Hero MotoCorp,** said: *“Being part of the historic 100th edition of FIM ISDE is a proud milestone for VIDA and an opportunity to showcase how electric innovation can shape the future of off-road riding. The VIDA DIRT.E K3 brings together the spirit of motorsport with forward-looking technology, building on the recognition it received at CES 2026 and the Red Dot Design Award. With our Portuguese- inspired 6DAYS® Limited Edition, we are celebrating the legacy of this iconic event while bringing a fresh perspective to electric off-road mobility. At VIDA, we believe innovation should go hand in hand with a safe, engaging, and empowering riding experience for young riders.”*

**Fabio Muner, Marketing & Digital Director, FIM,** said: *“On behalf of the FIM, I would like to sincerely thank Hero MotoCorp for its active participation in the 100th Edition of the FIM 6DAYS®. Thanks to the DIRT.E K3 6DAYS® Limited Edition bike, young riders have the chance to experience first-hand the beauty of motorcycling and enduro, an opportunity that is essential for building the future of our sport. The fact that these vehicles will be available on the market is a further great achievement for the FIM 6DAYS®. It is another example of how this historic FIM World Championship continues to widen its borders year after year, opening its doors to new generations and new ways of riding. Thank you, VIDA for sharing this vision with us.”*
