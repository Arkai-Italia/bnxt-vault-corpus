# Husqvarna Motorcycles App

Welcome to the **Ride Husqvarna Motorcycles app**.

This intuitive app is designed to enhance the riding experience for both onroad and offroad enthusiasts. Easily store and apply various engine mappings and suspension settings, track and analyse your riding activities, navigate with Turn-by-Turn+ directions, and access the Husqvarna Mobility Service. With features tailored to our offroad and supermoto competition range as well as the Husqvarna travel and naked models, the app aims to support peak performance and enjoyment on every ride. Whether you’re an experienced rider or just starting out, explore, connect to your machine, and ride with confidence.

## General features

#### Virtual garage (Riders paddock)

One place to add all your motorcycles and manage the settings.

#### Owners manual and service booklet

Direct and instant access to your owner's manuals, wherever you are. Keep track of your service history from dealer visits.

#### Navigation

Transform your phone into a powerful navigation system with the Ride Husqvarna Motorcycles app, powered by Sygic. Mirror your route navigation to your street motorcycle and enjoy seamless turn-by-turn directions right on your dashboard.

The **Ride Husqvarna Motorcycles app**is available for download in the Apple App Store and Google Play Store. All features without a specified cost are included at no charge, while optional functions can be activated via in-app purchase. To access all features within the app, riders must sign up.
