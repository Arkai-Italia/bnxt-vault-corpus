# Arenacross

###### When it comes to close racing action, it doesn’t get any closer than AMA Arenacross.

Featuring tight and technical indoor tracks built inside arenas in some of the nation’s liveliest metro areas, the fast-paced Arenacross events attract large crowds and put a premium on technique, finesse and rider fitness.

[The AMA Partzilla Arenacross Series](https://www.partzillaarenacross.com/) will serve as the AMA Arenacross National Championship with East and West Regional Championships, while [AMA GT Arenacross](https://arenamotocross.com/) will be the Midwest Regional Championship and the [AMA Throttlecross Series](https://www.throttlecross.com/) will serve as the Rocky Mountain Regional Championship. In amateur classes, the top five riders from the East, West and Midwest Regions will qualify for the AMA Arenacross Amateur Championship Finale, held at Lincoln Trail in Casey, Ill. Find the full schedule below:
