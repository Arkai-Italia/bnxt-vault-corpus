# TE 300 PRO | 2027 - LIKE NOTHING BEFORE IT | Husqvarna Indonesia

### 01. ENGINE & PERFORMANCE

LIGHTWEIGHT POWER. EXCEPTIONAL CONTROL.

#### Proven TE 300 Platform

Built around Husqvarna Mobility's advanced 2-stroke enduro platform, the TE 300 Pro delivers impressive torque, exceptional rideability, and a highly controllable power delivery. Advanced Throttle Body Injection (TBI) ensures consistent performance in all conditions while retaining the responsive character that makes the TE 300 the benchmark for modern 2-stroke enduro performance. Lightweight construction, optimised mass centralisation, and reduced vibration contribute to confidence-inspiring handling across all types of terrain.

CONSISTENT POWER. ANYWHERE.

#### TBI Technology

The TE 300 Pro features a 39 mm throttle body equipped with dual injectors positioned to optimise flow into the combustion chamber. Combined with advanced engine management, the system delivers instant throttle response, smooth power delivery, and consistent performance across changing terrain and conditions.

RACE-READY. BUILT TO LAST.

#### Pro Series Drivetrain

Designed for demanding enduro conditions, the TE 300 Pro enhances the standard platform with a Supersprox Stealth rear sprocket and clutch slave cylinder protector. These carefully selected components improve durability while providing additional protection when riding technical terrain.
