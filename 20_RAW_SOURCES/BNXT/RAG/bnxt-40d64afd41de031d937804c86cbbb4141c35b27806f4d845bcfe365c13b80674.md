# Tread - Overland Edition Tread XL - Overland Edition Owner's Manual - Tips After Pairing the Devices

# Tips After Pairing the Devices

* After the initial pairing, the two devices can connect automatically each time you turn them on.
* When you turn on the device, it tries to connect to the last phone to which it was connected.
* You might need to set your phone to connect to the device automatically when the device is turned on.
* You should verify the
  Bluetooth® features you want to use are enabled ([Enabling or Disabling Bluetooth Features](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-157A2412-594F-45D8-A5EE-5A5327DA0914/EN-US/GUID-3AD5407D-16AC-4FDF-A9A1-84295EEE3BFB.html)).
