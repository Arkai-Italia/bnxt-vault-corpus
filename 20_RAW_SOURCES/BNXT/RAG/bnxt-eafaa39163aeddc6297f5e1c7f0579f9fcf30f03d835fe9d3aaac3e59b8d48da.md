# MXGP ALBUM 2026 PRE-ORDER AVAILABLE NOW!

With the season barely over, we're already finalizing a new edition of the one and only one annual MX album, the MXGP Album, which celebrates its eighteenth anniversary this year!

From now until October 18th, to be sure to get it, you can pre-order this book on the dedicated website: [http://www.motocrossgpalbum.com](http://www.motocrossgpalbum.com/)

Choosing a cover photo wasn't very difficult!

* The standard edition features Guillem Farres and Camden McLellan. What a season the Triumph riders had in MX2!
* In MXGP, who better than Jeffrey Herlings to grace the cover of the limited edition? Jeffrey was masterful, securing his sixth world title.

In this 2026 edition of **Motocross GP Album** you will find:

* All about the **MX2** and **MXGP World Championships**, with never-before-seen photos of the top 10 in each category and a Miniview of each of the key players.
* A chapter on the **WMX**, **European 125, and 250** categories.
* A long section on the **79th edition of the Motocross of Nations**, featuring the Belgians' victory thirteen years after their last win. Alongside them are the Spanish, who had never before reached the podium, and the Dutch, who will be competing on home soil next year.
* The final curtain falls on **Dylan Ferrandis**, who hangs up his helmet after a brilliant career in the World Championship and in the USA.
* By both winning in Latvia, Sacha and Lucas Coenen have brought the total **number of victories for Belgian riders to over 600** since the creation of the World Championships. A brief retrospective was in order!
* How does one go from top-level rider to coach? **Clément Desalle, Kevin Strijbos, Marc De Reuver,** and **Jacky Vimond** tell us more about their second careers.
* The return of ‘**Travel with us**’ to coincide with the return of a GP to **South Africa**.
* **30 years** ago, World motocross entered a new era.

And finally, we take you into the paddock to discover the unsung heroes without whom nothing would be possible!

Hardcover, **192 pages**, over **190 photos**, most of them never before seen, taken at all 19 rounds of the World Championship, including the Motocross of Nations: this is the **Motocross GP Album 2026**, **available in early December for €46.90 (plus shipping)**, a price unchanged for the past three years.

Book available for pre-order in standard or limited edition.
