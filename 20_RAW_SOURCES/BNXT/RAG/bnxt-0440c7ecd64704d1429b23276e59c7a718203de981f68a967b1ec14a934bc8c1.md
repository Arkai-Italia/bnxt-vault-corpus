# eMoto race class

* **eMoto Expert** for experienced Hard Enduro competitors who now race e Bikes
* **eMoto Hobby** electricrace class is for competitors who are looking for their first taste of hard enduro rallye, a chance to enjoy the unique Romanian terrain. You are not looking to challenge yourself over the top - but still want to enjoy the Red Bull Romaniacs flair and the world’s most beautiful trails.

* We will send you onto all kinds of single trails in different conditions, including slippery roots and rocks, regular obstacles like trees, small steps, muddy tracks and hills.
* Being super fit is not required, but helps !  Basic off-road riding technique and the ability to keep your electric eMoto functional is sufficient to get you through each of the four offroad days.

   **Advisory:** The cost of competing in the eMoto race class will be less as the overall length of the race tracks and the overall race time will be lower than the other event race classes.

   *If there are further questions about the **eMoto race class**or **[******Registering to race******](https://www.redbullromaniacs.com/for-competitors/2027-register-to-race)**contact  Competitor manager **Cristina Badiu [cristinab@xventure.net](mailto:cristinab@xventure.net)***

**During the event**
 eMoto competitors on each Offroad day:
 - organise transport of their eMoto vehicles to the daily **eMoto Offroad start** and from the **eMoto Offroad finish**
 - Racing begins at the designated **eMoto Offroad start** and finishes at the **eMoto Offroad finish**
 - Organise battery charging / battery exchanging / eMoto servicing at the **eMoto charging points
 -** It is mandatory for eMoto competitors to stay **a MINIMUM of 20 minutes** at the daily eMoto **charging points**, competitors can stay longer to recharge their batteries. Any additional time that is required to charge or replace the batteries is **NOT** included in the competitors race time
 *(It is **NOT** included in the results).*

 eMoto **Service crew/mechanics** event **[Travel and distances](https://www.redbullromaniacs.com/for-competitors/about-the-race-tracks/travel-distances)** information is available **[here](https://www.redbullromaniacs.com/for-competitors/about-the-race-tracks/travel-distances)**

 All **fInal** and the **latest** event **eMoto information [**About the eMoto race tracks**](https://www.redbullromaniacs.com/for-competitors/about-the-race-tracks)** will be provided daily at the **[Romaniacs event breifings](https://www.redbullromaniacs.com/for-competitors/romaniacs-event-briefings)**

 It is the overall responsibility of the eMoto competitors to organise the charging of their vehicles and have available all and any specialist charging cables.
