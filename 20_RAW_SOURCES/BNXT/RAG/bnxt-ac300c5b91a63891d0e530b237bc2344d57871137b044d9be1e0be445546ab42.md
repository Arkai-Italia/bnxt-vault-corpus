# KTM hints at incoming 350 EXC two-stroke – and confirms 250 resurrection

Published on September 27th, 2026 by Nye Davis

.jpg)

#### **KTM lets slip plans to bring back its smaller-bore two-stroke enduro models – both currently confined to closed-course purgatory – and what’s more, there might just be a 350 EXC on the way, too!**

For the last two years, KTM, GasGas, and Husqvarna customers have had the choice of one model, separated only by colour and spec, where homologated two-strokes are concerned. The latest emissions regulations have put the 150 and 250 to rest, and the marque’s smaller-cc models are now only available in XC-W form. Now, though, Enduro21 can confirm that plans are afoot to bring them back.

At the first ride launch of the Mattighofen brand’s MY27 enduro and motocross models (review coming soon), we took a break from testing duties to chat with Fabian Gusta, Lead Product Management Off-Road for KTM, about what the future holds for the engine configuration most dear to our hearts, especially as we move towards ever-more stringent emissions targets.

To our (and presumably your?) joy, KTM’s stance can in effect be boiled down to one sentence; two-strokes aren’t dead; in fact, they might just be in for a revival. The Austrian marque is beavering away to resurrect the 250 and potentially the 150, and maybe more importantly, it is also considering adding more cubes to the range – in a move that *should* simultaneously up the performance ceiling and help meet Euro targets (a strategy that has been the go-to for years in the road bike world).

The idea of a bigger-bore smoker is nothing particularly new. The internet rumour mill has been churning away for a while now with whispers that KTM may be looking to do just that, and now we’ve finally had a hint of confirmation from an official within Mattighofen. Gusta stopped short of confirming that a 350 EXC is definitely on the horizon, but he did confirm that KTM is having the conversation – and seemingly working on prototypes behind closed doors.

.jpg)As for whether two-strokes are facing extinction? Gusta's answer was pretty emphatic; “Absolutely, definitely no.”

### **Here’s the full Q&A:**

**Are two-strokes a dying breed, especially with the eventual mandate of Euro 6 – whatever that will look like?**

*“As of now, there is no real conversation about a Euro 6 emission level. So, in that regard, we are on the safe side with what is in place. We are currently looking into how we can homologate the 250 and potentially a 150 again. So the plan is to bring the smaller bikes back whenever we would switch to a new platform.”*

**Is that next year or maybe two or three years down the line?**

*“It's still going to take a while. But once that's happening, we're hopefully going to have the 250 homologated again.”*

**Could we see a bigger two-stroke? The internet is full of rumours about a 350 EXC. Is that something that could work?**

*“Yeah, potentially, that conversation is being had. We have some creative guys in the company who work on the engineering side of things, and they are having fantasies like this. And then we have the motorsports department, and they are full of ideas.*

*“Whenever such a setup brings an advantage in performance, this is something we might see in production as well.”*

-2.jpg)**Are these ideas manifesting in a physical product, or is it just conversation?**

*“We're going to see sooner or later.”*

[At this point, Fabian stopped short of giving us a direct answer, but the smile plastered across his face told us all we need to know. So, while we can’t say emphatically that a big-bore EXC is right around the corner, Fabian’s poker face (or lack thereof) tells us that KTM is definitely working on it.]

**So the short answer is you're thinking about it?**

*“Yeah, we're thinking about it. We are having various prototypes. We are testing and trying to make sense out of it. But the bottom line is we are only bringing to production what really shows performance potential. And if we are not convinced, we are not going to bring it.”*

**And are two-strokes dead?**

*“Absolutely, definitely no.”*
