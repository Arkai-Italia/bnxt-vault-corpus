## **5. ENTRIES**

Rider’s briefing is compulsory to all riders, will be held according to according to attached Time Shedule at Belgrade Arena Conference Room. Briefing will be held in English Language.

# **11. PADDOCK**

Paddock is located outside of Arena, close to starting gate.

The use of environmental mat is mandatory at all times, also in the case of electric generators or other generators. It is compulsory for electric bikes to use an electrical insulation mat. Failure to comply with this obligation is subject to a disciplinary penalty of 100 EUR. In the event of non-payment of the penalty or failureto use the mat after the above mentioned penalty has been imposed, the rider will be excluded from the event.

# **12. BIKE WARM UP AREA**

This area is well delimited near the paddock, outside. It is strictly forbidden to warm-up the bikes in any other location, especially at paddock inside building. When riding during warming-up the rider is obligedto wear a helmet. No riding on green areas is allowed.

# **17. PROTEST AND APPEALS**

Any person or group of persons (rider, entrant, manufacturer, official, etc.) recognized by the FIM ad concerned by a decision taken under the authority of the MSS, may ask for redress for the consequences of that decision.

Protest must be lodged according to the MSS Disciplinary and Arbitration Code and the Supplementary Regulations and be accompanied by a fee of 350 EUR, or equivalent amount in local currency, returnable if the protest is justified.

In general, protests against eligibility of a rider entered, must be made before the start of the official practise. Protests against results must be presented to the Referee within 30 minutes following the publishing of the results. In case of FIM combined races an appeal can be made to the FIM Stewards Panel against a decision of the Race Direction.

# **<u>Helmet Safety and Modification Rules</u>**

Helmets are designed exclusively for protection and must not be used as a platform for attachments. Cameras and all other accessories or foreign objects are strictly forbidden from being attached to the rider’s helmet.

FIM Homologation (FRHPhe-02) Timeline

- Current (From 01/01/2024): FIM homologated helmets (with a valid FRHPhe-02 label) are strongly recommended.

- Mandatory (From 01/01/2026): FIM homologated helmets become mandatory. All other standards will be forbidden.

# **_<u>Exception for 2026 Championships Starting in 2025:</u>_**

FIM has exceptionally decided that for Championships starting in 2025 that span into the 2026 season, the mandatory requirement to wear an FIM Homologated Helmet (FRHPhe-02) will be waived, and it will remain only strongly recommended.
