# XE 50 PERFORMANCE

##### True enduro, from day one

XE 50 Performance

Unleash your free spirit

If you dream of dirt paths, silent forests and limitless adventures, the XE 50 Performance is the perfect partner to get started.

Designed for those with a passion for off-road in their hearts and the desire to master it, this bike is much more than a first step — it's your key to a world of freedom, technique and adrenaline.

Agile, lightweight and inspired by full-size enduro bikes, it gives you the confidence to face every new challenge.

**Engine**

**The heart of an explorer**

At the core of the XE 50 beats a 49.7cc 2-stroke engine, liquid-cooled and **compliant with Euro 5+ by Motori Minarelli**.

Electronic ignition and an electronically controlled exhaust valve ensure smooth but assertive power delivery — ideal for learning the rhythm of enduro. The exhaust completes the technical package with grit and personality. This is a bike that helps you grow, **improve and have fun, always with maximum control.**

**Chassis**

**Technology designed to take you further**

Whether you're tackling a technical section or just riding on a trail, the XE 50 Performance is always ready.

The **CrMo steel perimeter frame** provides strength and agility, while Fantic suspension — a Ø41 mm USD fork up front and a **rear monoshock with progressive linkage** — ensures stability and comfort even on rough terrain.

21” and 18” spoke wheels combined with Wave discs (Ø260 mm front / Ø220 mm rear) complete a **true off-road setup.**

**Equipment**

**Big-bike details, total accessibility**

Every aesthetic and functional detail of the XE 50 Performance tells a story of adventure.

Forged aluminium triple clamps, a Ø22 mm handlebar, full LED lighting and a redesigned front unit enhance both looks and function — **inspired by professional enduro bikes.**

With the XE 50 Performance, you’re not just learning to ride: you’re **learning to live the enduro spirit**. The Fantic way.
