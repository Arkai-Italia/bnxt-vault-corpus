This kit has the components needed to install a TowerOne on a bike with either a frame mounted navigation tower or an already-existing handlebar mount.

Included:

* TowerOne Display, GPS Antenna, Beeper.
* Mounting plate and isolators.
* Power lead.

You will need:

* A power switch.
* A handlebar remote. (Either the TowerOne remote or a standard rally remote with M8 connectors)
* Hardware to secure the mounting plate to your mounting system.
