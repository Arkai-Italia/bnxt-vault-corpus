# 6 DAYS ENDURO

**Want to Ride in the World's Most Iconic Enduro Event?**

---

Join us for the historic 100th edition of the FIM International Six Days Enduro (ISDE), taking place in Grândola (Alentejo Region), Portugal, from October 12–17. This year, Betamotor is proud to support the world’s boldest riders in partnership with the expert teams at Entrophy and Dirt Racing.
The ISDE is the ultimate test of endurance, skill, and reliability. To help you compete at the highest level, we are offering comprehensive, fully customizable motorcycle rental and technical support packages so you can focus entirely on your race.

**Choose Your Bike**

You can choose any model from the latest 2027 RR lineup. Each bike comes equipped with an exclusive "Six Days Limited Edition by Dirt Racing" kit, developed specifically for this event:
**2-Stroke: RR 125 | RR 200 | RR 250 | RR 300**
**4-Stroke: RR 350 | RR 390 | RR 430 | RR 480**

**Comprehensive Support Service**

The Dirt Racing team handles every technical and logistical aspect in compliance with FIM regulations, including:

* Access to the dedicated, fully equipped Dirt Racing paddock area
* On-site parts service, plus Liqui Moly oils and fluids
* Pre-race and in-event technical support, tuning, and preparation
* Personal locker, paddock space, and transport for your personal gear
* Race support stations with water, snacks, fruit, and air filter service
* Partner Benefit: Special pricing on MITAS tires and foam

  **Your Motorcycle, Your Setup**

To ensure an excellent experience, you can customize your ride with premium upgrades (available for an additional fee) such as raised seats, special handlebars, oversized rear brake discs, kick starters, and other technical modifications upon request.
