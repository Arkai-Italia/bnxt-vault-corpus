## VISIT THE WORLD OF CHAMPIONS

Step inside the magnificent **KTM Motohall** in the heart of Mattighofen and fully immerse yourself in the highspeed world of **KTM**. Across an area of 2600 m², this ultra-modern and interactive exhibition spread over three floors gives visitors first-hand experience of the bikes and heroes of motorsport as well as the history, innovations, engineering and technology of the motorcycle and the **KTM** name. And all of this is accessible for everyone, including the interactive displays, virtual tours, and original bikes. And that’s not all – there is also a 300 m² shop and the Garage restaurant in the adjacent townhouse. The **KTM Motohall** promises you and your family an experience that is truly unparalleled in the world of motorsport.

### First-Hand Innovation, Techniques & Technology

Discover the elaborate innovations, and sophisticated technical developments on display in the exhibition. See the entire design process from the initial sketch right through to the finished product and find out all about the designs, frames, engines, and chassis of the **READY TO RACE** motorcycles.

### THE KTM Story

The most famous **KTM** bikes are used to tell you the fascinating story of Europe's biggest motorcycle manufacturer from its beginnings to the present day, starting with the **KTM** R 100 from 1953 and going all the way through to the latest bikes in the Offroad, Street, and E-Mobility segments.

### Meet your KTM heroes

Street and Offroad bikes race along a replica banked turn, guiding you to the highlight of the exhibition: the Heroes area. Here visitors will meet the bravest and most successful KTM factory riders of all time, from Gennady Moiseev and Heinz Kinigadner to Shayne King, Brad Binder, and Matthias Walkner. At the same time, a spectacular, 120-meter-long, 360-degree video installation will immerse them in the world of KTM.

### LIVE HORSEPOWER TALK

Golden oldies: KTM Motohall´s living workshop gives classic bikes their sparkle back. Our in-house mechanic mainly works on early KTM motorcycles. You can watch the restorers up close as they bring bikes back to life, and if you want to know more, you are welcome to ask the head restorer questions directly.

### Workshops in the Innovation Lab

In the KTM Motohall's Innovation Lab workshops, young visitors can get to grips with the basics of electrical engineering and 3D and digital design. The workshops can be prebooked for kids and teens, giving them the chance to try out cutting-edge equipment, such as a design- and CAD software, a 3-D scanner, a 3-D printer, a laser cutter, a doodle, solder and a virtual reality station.
