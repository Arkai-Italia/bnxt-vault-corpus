# Tread - SXS Edition Owner's Manual - Receiving Data From Your Handheld Device

# Receiving Data From Your Handheld Device

Before you can receive data, you must enable broadcasting on your handheld dog device. See the owner's manual for your handheld dog device for more information.

Place your handheld dog device within 3 m (10 ft) of your
Tread® - SXS Edition device.

TIP:
Your
Tread - SXS Edition device receives data from one compatible handheld dog device at a time. Stay more than 3 m (10 ft) away from other handheld dog devices or disable broadcasting on handheld dog devices from which you do not want to receive data.

The devices connect and synchronize automatically.
