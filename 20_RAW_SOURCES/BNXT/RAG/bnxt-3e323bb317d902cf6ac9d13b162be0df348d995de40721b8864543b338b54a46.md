# Sharing the dream in the July issue of ‘American Motorcyclist’

* by [Jim Witters](https://americanmotorcyclist.com/author/jwittersama-cycle-org/)
* July 6, 2020
* 0 Comments
* 1907 Views

Do you share the motorcycling dream with others? AMA Motorcycle Hall of Famers Scot Harden and Ricky Carmichael do, as well as former AMA Board Chairwoman Maggie McNally-Bradshaw.

Learn their stories in the July issue of *American Motorcyclis*t magazine.

You can also read about how motorcycle manufacturers are selling bikes during the coronavirus restrictions, celebrate the life of AMA Motorcycle Hall of Famer Marty Smith, get the inside scoop on AMA Motorcycle Hall of Famer Miguel Duhamel’s AMA Formula Xtreme championship-winning Honda CBR600RR and get AMA Member Tony Mangia’s take on the first ride of the season.

In addition, AMA President and CEO Rob Dingman, who is also a Charter Life Member, discusses the return of AMA racing and riding activities during these challenging times as the nation faces COVID-19.

There are also stories about laws and actions at the state and local level to keep you informed.

You can read the issue online. Or get a hard copy at home if you are a Life Member Plus member. Not a Life Member Plus member?
