# VIP GOLD SKYBOX 2027 TICKETS NOW ON SALE: THE ULTIMATE MXGP EXPERIENCE IS BACK

**MONACO (Principality of Monaco) 13 OCTOBER 2026 – The countdown to the 2027 FIM Motocross World Championship starts today, as tickets for the VIP Gold Skybox officially go on sale. Fans can now secure the best seat in the house for the new season.**

Over the years the **[VIP Gold Skybox](https://sites.weezevent.com/mxgp-2027/)** has grown into one of the most popular hospitality experiences in the MXGP paddock, and 2026 was another standout year.

From the opening round to the final chequered flag, guests enjoyed premium trackside viewing, exclusive meet and greet with their heroes, generous gourmet buffets and the unique atmosphere that only a Grand Prix weekend can deliver.

For 2027 the experience goes even further. Infront Moto Racing continues to raise the standard of its VIP service year after year, with one simple goal: an even more exclusive, exceptional and unforgettable experience for every guest.

### **A VIP Gold Skybox weekend includes:**

* Access to the Skybox Terrace to get the best view of the Starts!
* Premium viewing areas with panoramic views over the track
* A full buffet with fresh, quality food and alcohol and non-alcoholic drinks throughout the day
* Comfortable, air-conditioned hospitality at the heart of the action
* Exclusive meet and greet and autograph sessions with the stars of MXGP and MX2
* VIP Parking Access (closest to the paddock/venue)

The meet and greet sessions remain one of the highlights of the package. Throughout 2026, VIP guests met many of the championship’s biggest names up close, collecting signatures, photos and memories to take home. In 2027, the best MXGP riders will once again be stopping by the Skybox across the season.

[](https://sites.weezevent.com/mxgp-2027/)

Places are limited and the most popular rounds sell out quickly, so don’t wait.

**Book your VIP Gold Skybox tickets now: [CLICK THIS LINK](https://sites.weezevent.com/mxgp-2027/)**

### **MXGP INNER CIRCLE** *Reserved for the few. Experienced like no other.*

You have experienced MXGP VIP. Now discover what lies beyond it.

Open only to VIP Gold Skybox guests, the **MXGP Inner Circle** reveals a side of the championship few ever see, with privileged access reserved for a handful of guests each weekend.

The exclusive **MXGP Inner Circle** takes you past the doors that stay closed to almost everyone else at a Grand Prix, out of the hospitality area and into the places where the race is decided. This is more than VIP. It is the chance to get closer, go further and be part of moments very few will ever experience.

**MXGP Inner Circle includes:**

* **Exclusive grid walk:** step onto the starting grid before the race, an area normally reserved for the riders and their mechanics
* **A privileged position for the start:** watch the gate drop from a preferential spot, as close to the action as it gets
* **Inside the press conference:** be in the room as the fastest riders of the weekend face the media
* **Exclusive tours:** behind-the-scenes access to the heart of MXGP, where very few are ever invited
* **The Inner Circle Plate:** an exclusive souvenir signed by the best riders, given only to members of the MXGP Inner Circle

*To book and be part of the **MXGP Inner Circle**, contact **[vipbackoffice@mxgp.com](mailto:vipbackoffice@mxgp.com?subject=MXGP%20VIP%202027%20-%20Privilege%20Upgrade)***
