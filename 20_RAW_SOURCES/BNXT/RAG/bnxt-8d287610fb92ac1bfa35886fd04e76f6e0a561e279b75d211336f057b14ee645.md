Sherco ST Acces – A Motorcycle for Trial Enthusiasts Looking to Keep Their Budget Under Control

We are pleased to announce the arrival of the Trial ST Acces, a limited offer available to a select few.

Designed for trial enthusiasts who want to manage their budget without compromising on riding enjoyment, style, or performance, the Trial Access is the ideal alternative.

Available in 125 cc, 250 cc, and 300 cc 2-stroke engine versions, it stands out with an excellent quality/price ratio\*.

**Key Features:**

* Access graphics kit
* S3 “Hard Rock” footpegs with pins
* Aluminum skid plate
* Keihin carburetor
* Liquid cooling
* Fan
* White hubs
* Front disc guard
* Brake discs: 185 mm front, 145 mm rear
* Fork with 170 mm of travel
* Rear shock absorber with 165 mm of travel
* Front and rear Morad black aluminum rims
