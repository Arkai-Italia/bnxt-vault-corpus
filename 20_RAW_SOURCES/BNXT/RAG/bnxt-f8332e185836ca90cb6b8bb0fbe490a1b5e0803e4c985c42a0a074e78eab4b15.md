# Electric motors

The electric motor is a central component of electrification and is shaping the future of mobility. Whether as a zero-emission main drive in electric vehicles or for increasing the efficiency of auxiliary units like fans and pumps – its role in the modern vehicle is diverse. High power density and optimized performance are crucial in this context.

Our development expertise ranges from highly integrated drive solutions like the eAxle to robust motors for commercial vehicles and climate applications. We offer scalable and technologically leading solutions characterized by high power density and durability. In this way, we support our customers in realizing high-performance vehicle concepts.
