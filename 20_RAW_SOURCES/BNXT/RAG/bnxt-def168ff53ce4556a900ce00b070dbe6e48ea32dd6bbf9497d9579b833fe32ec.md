# 2027 KTM SX-E 2.12 - GROW WITH CONFIDENCE | KTM

## 01. GROW WITH CONFIDENCE

FULL KTM SX LOOK

### GRAPHICS

Using the full-sized KTM SX range as a guideline, the 2027 KTM SX E-2.12 features modern, striking graphics in typical "big bike" fashion. The graphics are also applied using an in-mold process, ensuring the best durability and quality for many years of offroad action.

BIG BIKE DESIGN

### CHASSIS

Built tough to last, the KTM SX-E 2.12 chassis boasts an impressive list of construction materials. The upper triple-clamp, handlebar clamps, footrest carrier, and brake components are made of high-end forged aluminum alloys, while the lower triple clamps and the shaft are crafted from forged chromoly steel. The swingarm and skid plate complete the list, made from HPDC aluminum alloy.

BUILT TOUGH

### FRAME

Built around a center tube Aluminum Extrusion Frame, the 2027 KTM SX-E 2.12 features a design concept that reduces the number of components needed to ensure fewer connections for maximum durability and chassis rigidity. For the KTM SX-E 2.12, the frame geometry has been adapted to accommodate the larger front wheel and provide proportionally revised ergonomics for taller riders.

ALL-OUT ACTION

### SUSPENSION

Ready to tackle challenging track conditions, the KTM SX-E 2.12 features a WP hydraulic fork matched to a preload-adjustable WP monoshock. Together with the updated front-end package, the suspension setup complements the KTM SX-E 2.12's revised geometry and larger front wheel for confident handling and control.

MASTER THE TRACK

### WHEELS AND TIRES

A larger 12-inch front wheel paired with a 10-inch rear wheel provides a balanced and confidence-inspiring setup for taller young riders. Lightweight black powder-coated aluminum HPDC rims provide trouble-free durability, while KENDA Trakmaster tires deliver dependable grip across a wide variety of riding conditions.

BALANCE POINT

### ADJUSTABLE SEAT HEIGHT

Designed specifically for taller young riders, the KTM SX-E 2.12 features a higher riding position with a two-level adjustable seat height of 536 mm and 566 mm. More than simply raising the bike, the KTM SX-E 2.12 benefits from proportionally revised ergonomics, with 52 mm of the additional height located between the seat and footpegs.. This creates a natural riding position while allowing growing riders to stay comfortable and in control as their confidence and skills develop.

STOPPING POWER

### BRAKES

A set of radial, hydraulic 140 mm disk brakes on the front and rear ensure ample stopping power when the going gets too fast. Also, the hand levers are adjustable to fit smaller hand sizes, with the brake pads being compatible with high-end market bicycle brake pads for easy availability.

PERFECTLY MADE TO MATCH

### CONTROLS

The black anodized, aluminum alloy handlebars feature a reduced diameter grip area for the perfect ergonomic grip for little hands. The width and bend were also specifically developed for easy steering while ensuring a firm grip and control of the KTM SX-E 2.12. The ODI lock-on handgrips also feature a slim diameter to further improve control and feel through the bars.

EASY TO HANDLE

### LIGHT WEIGHT

Weighing in at 27 kg, the KTM SX-E 2.12 feels like a real, bigger motorcycle, while still providing easy handling. The combination of weight and agility provides a planted, more secure feeling as the speed builds, and is better suited to bigger, harder bumps. At less than 30 kg, the KTM SX-E 2.12 can also be easily transported to and from riding locations.
