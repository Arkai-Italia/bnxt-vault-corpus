### CONTOUR LINES

Contour lines with elevation labels show the topography of the land to help guide your expedition and find the best ascents and descents.

### CITY NAVIGATOR® ROADS, HIGHWAYS AND INTERSTATES

Experience the most detailed street maps available so you can navigate with clear, turn-by-turn directions to a specified address or intersection.

### HIGH-RESOLUTION SATELLITE IMAGERY

Enjoy high-resolution satellite imagery showing vivid aerial views to make it easy to find paths through forest, prairie or desert.

### ADVENTURE ROADS AND TRAILS1

Enjoy turn-by-turn trail navigation for traversing unpaved roads and trails using adventure roads and trails map content comprised of OpenStreetMap (OSM). Shows trail accessibility for vehicles less than 50” /1.27m wide, full-size off-road vehicles, snowmobiles or all vehicles. Preloaded for Europe and Middle East.

### TRACKS4AFRICA

Navigate from Cape Town to Cairo with included Tracks4Africa road and trails. Plus enjoy many Tracks4Africa Point of Interest covering where to stay, eat, fuel, what to see and do and much more.

### TOPOGRAPHIC MAPPING

Topographic maps with 3D terrain compiled using the community-generated OpenStreetMap (OSM) database.

### CAMPSITE DIRECTORIES

Simplify your trip with a preloaded directory of motorhome parks and services from ACSI™, Campercontact™ and Trailer’s PARK™, which can be filtered by amenity such as showers, electricity and more. So you don’t need a mobile phone signal to route to the best-established, wild and dispersed campsites.

### CUSTOM VEHICLE ROUTING2

Custom vehicle routing considers the size and weight of your vehicle then finds a recommended route. And you can save profiles for multiple vehicles or trailers.

### POPULAR MOTORCYCLE ROADS

Find and ride popular motorcycle routes with a heatmap layer showing you where other motorcyclists like to ride.

### SLOPE ASPECT

Colours depict the direction of the slope face so a rider can stay aware of avalanche-prone slope faces. Slopes can become dangerous from directional wind and sun exposure. Available in [selected regions](https://www.garmin.com/en-GB/p/893561/#additional) with a paid subscription to [Outdoor Maps](https://www.garmin.com/en-GB/p/893561/)+.

### AVALANCHE RISK REPORT

Access avalanche reports directly on your compatible device or in the Tread® app. Available in selected regions with a paid subscription to [Outdoor Maps](https://www.garmin.com/en-GB/p/893561/)+.

### SLOPE ANGLE

Colours depict the severity of a slope so a rider can easily see slopes that could be prone to an avalanche. Available in [selected regions](https://www.garmin.com/en-GB/p/893561/#additional) with a paid subscription to [Outdoor Maps](https://www.garmin.com/en-GB/p/893561/)+.
