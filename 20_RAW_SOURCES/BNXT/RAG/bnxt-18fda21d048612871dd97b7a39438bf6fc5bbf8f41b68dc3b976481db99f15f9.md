# Tread Owner's Manual - Viewing the Weather Forecast

# Viewing the Weather Forecast

NOTICE

Garmin® is not responsible for the accuracy or timeliness of the weather data.

Before you can use this feature, your device must be receiving weather data.
You can connect your device to the
Tread™ app to receive weather data ([Pairing Your Phone and Headset](https://www8.garmin.com/manuals/webhelp/GUID-16B1D74D-857B-4FFB-8DE2-A0960FE0D090/EN-US/GUID-883143FA-E7E4-4F4A-906A-4FF7FEEBE594.html)).

1. Select
    > Weather.

   The device shows the current weather conditions and a forecast for the next several days.
2. Select a day.

   The detailed forecast for that day appears.
