# THE 2025 OFFICIAL GUIDE IS ONLINE!

CORDOBA (Argentina) 27 February 2025 - The **2025 Official Guide** is now officially online. It is your go-to resource for everything related to the upcoming season of the **FIM Motocross World Championship**. Packed with in-depth information, the guide takes you through the evolution of this legendary series while providing key insights into the circuits that will host the world’s best motocross riders.

Inside, you will find **detailed profiles of the reigning champions** from **MXGP, MX2, WMX, and EMX**, alongside an exclusive look at the **2025 OAT Officially Approved Teams**. Additionally, the guide offers an array of key statistics, details on global TV coverage, and highlights of the championship’s official partners for the season ahead.

The **2025 edition** not only focuses on the **MXGP and MX2 World Championship classes**, but also delves into the **Monster Energy FIM Motocross of Nations, the Women’s Motocross World Championship, and the European Motocross Championships**. Readers can also familiarise themselves with the **updated FIM regulations** set to shape the upcoming season.
