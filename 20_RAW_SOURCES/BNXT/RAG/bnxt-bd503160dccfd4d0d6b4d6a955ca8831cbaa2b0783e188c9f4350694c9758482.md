# Provisional Regulations 2026 | Drupal

The Africa Eco Race offers several categories, each designed to meet the expectations of rally-raid enthusiasts — whether they are seasoned competitors, adventure seekers, or nostalgic for the great African epic journeys.
Each category has its own specific regulations, which you can download below.

[Auto / SSV / Truck](https://www.africarace.com/en/node/820#AutoSSVTruck)
:

This category includes all off-road vehicles competing in the race: cars, SSVs, and trucks.
    It is aimed at crews ready to take on a demanding mechanical and sporting challenge across African tracks, while embracing the true spirit of rally-raid.

[Download the Auto/SSV/Truck](https://www.africarace.com/sites/default/files/2025-11/AER26_RP_AUTO_FR_UK-V3.pdf)

---

[Bike / Quad](https://www.africarace.com/en/node/820#BikeQuad)
:

A legendary and historic category, motorbikes embody the very essence of African rally-raid.
    It brings together solo riders in search of adventure, self-challenge, and pure navigation, all within a spirit of camaraderie and endurance.

[Download the Motorbike Regulations](https://www.africarace.com/sites/default/files/2025-11/AER26_RP_MOTO_FR_UK-V3.pdf)

---

[Raid auto/bike](https://www.africarace.com/en/node/820#Raidautobike)
:

Designed for those seeking to experience the rally without the pressure of competition, the Raid category allows participants to follow the official course at their own pace.
    It’s the ideal choice for living the adventure in an accessible yet authentic way.

Download the raid Regulations – Coming Soon

[Historic](https://www.africarace.com/en/node/820#Historic)
:

The Historic category pays tribute to the legendary vehicles and motorbikes that shaped the history of African rally-raid.
    Reserved for vintage or faithfully restored machines, it invites participants to relive the golden age of the Dakar through a truly authentic adventure.

[Download the Historic regulation](https://www.africarace.com/sites/default/files/2025-09/AER26_RP_HISTORIC_FR_UK-V2.pdf)
