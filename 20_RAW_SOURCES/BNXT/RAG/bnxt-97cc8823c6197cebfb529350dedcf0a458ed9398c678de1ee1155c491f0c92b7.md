# Yamaha announces 2027 WR enduro range with significant 450F changes

Published on September 13th, 2026 by Nye Davis

#### **Yamaha confirms significant updates to its flagship WR450F for 2027, including motor mods, YZ-inspired chassis refinements, and at last, a hydraulic clutch.**

Team blue hasn't made a big fuss about its latest off-road collection – the press release for next year's bikes more or less stopped at confirming both bikes will continue in the line-up – but a closer look reveals a handful of alterations worthy of our attention.

The 250 goes unchanged for 2027, but the flagship 450 now boasts a hydraulic clutch (what's taken you so long Yamaha?) which the firm says offers a lighter pull than its cable-operated predecessor.

That familiar YZ-derived reverse-head motor has also been revised for 2027, with lightly fettled camshaft timing, said to suppress low-rpm torque, delivering a buttery smooth bottom end reducing the tendency to flame out.

The chassis, too, has gone under the knife, receiving flex optimisation treatment based on the latest YZ frame. These changes include a thickened upper section of the downtube, a narrower lower section and asymmetric engine mounts, all of which Yamaha reckons improves response, comfort and controllability.

Oddly enough, the MY27 spec sheet suggests a fully fuelled kerb mass of 116kg – three kilograms lighter than the outgoing model – though Yamaha doesn't outline where the fat has been trimmed.

There’s also a revised rear brake and a larger rear sprocket as standard. Both the big 450 and its little brother continue to feature Yamaha’s nifty Power Tuner app, which, alongside allowing users to fiddle with engine characteristics, also includes an ECU anti-theft lock system.

Pricing for both machines has come up an extra hundred quid, taking the 450 to £9400 and the 250 to £8950. You thrifty bargain hunters out there will be happy to hear that leftover 2026 stock has been discounted by a grand, making the outgoing bikes £1100 cheaper than their respective 2027 replacements, if you can find one.
