# Electric drive module

#### Efficient and scalable powertrain system for electric and hybrid vehicles

The electric drive module integrates the next-generation separate motor-generator and power electronics in a single system, which helps to achieve synergy effects. The new power electronics is specially optimized for attachment to the electric motor. The module thus serves as an electric powertrain for (plug-in) hybrid and electric vehicles.

## compact

Electric motor and power electronics in a single powertrain unit

## versatile

Ready for deployment in diverse powertrain topologies

## scalable

The performance of the system can be adapted to different vehicle types and payloads

## cost benefits

By integrating the power electronics and electric motor in a single powertrain unit, it is possible to save components and expensive connecting cables.

Drag to rotate

Easy-to-integrate powertrain solution with a modular design

Since the electric motor and power electronics are permanently attached to one another, the electric powertrain is highly versatile, enabling deployment in diverse powertrain topologies.

The inverter converts direct current supplied by the battery into alternating current required by the electric motor. Through its electronic control and monitoring of the electric motor, the inverter provides a reliable on-demand power supply for the electric powertrain. The electric motor, with its permanent magnet excitation, converts electrical energy into mechanical energy. In deceleration mode, the separate motor-generator charges the vehicle battery by converting mechanical energy into electrical energy.

## Integration in purely electric as well as hybrid powertrain solutions

The electric drive module is connected directly ti the drive shaft via a transmission, enabling integration in existing vehicle designs.

By integrating the module in the axle with a suitable transmission, it is possible to implement purely electric as well as hybrid solutions in this configuration.
