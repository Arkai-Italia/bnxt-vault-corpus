# 701 ENDURO | 2026 - The perfect combination | Husqvarna Indonesia

### 01. Precisely engineered

#### Fuel pump

The latest-generation 701 Enduro features a higher-pressure fuel pump, which plays a vital role in fuel atomization and provides a more direct connection between the throttle and rear wheel. The pump also allows for easy replacement of the fuel filters.

#### Frame

The lightweight chromium-molybdenum steel trellis frame is updated to be slightly stiffer, without compromising its renowned handling characteristics. Detailed feedback from the terrain is transferred to the rider thanks to the precisely calculated torsional rigidity and longitudinal flex. Additionally, the center stand brackets have been relocated on the frame for improved ease of installation.

#### Subframe

The self-supporting rear subframe, with its integrated 3.4-gallon (13-liter) fuel tank, is perfectly positioned for optimal weight distribution. Manufactured using a thermoplastic rotational molding process, the single-piece construction of the polyamide tank ensures a low weight.

#### WP forks

The WP XPLOR front forks offer 265 mm of wheel travel and are specifically engineered for dual sport riding. Featuring an open cartridge design with a spring in each leg, compression and rebound settings can be fine-tuned with 30 clicks each using the clearly marked dials located on top of each fork leg.

#### WP shock

The WP XPLOR rear shock offers adjustable compression and rebound settings for a customized set-up. Connected to a linkage system with offroad geometry, and offering 250 mm of rear wheel travel, the shock ensures comfort and stability across every type of terrain.

#### Airbox

The airbox features a intake snorkel for improved air flow into the engine. The updated version also reduces the number of parts used to construct the airbox while retaining the same, failproof air filter from the previous generation.
