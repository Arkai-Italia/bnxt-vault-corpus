The World famous hard enduro rallye presents the Romaniacs Prolog, where competitors have to prove their endurance, riding skills and experience in front of thousands of spectating fans, television crews, online Internet watchers, journalists and media.
 The stars of this sport stand out in these extreme races within Sibiu’s town centre where the artificial obstacles produce an amazing motorcycle show. Unpredictable obstacles and famous contestants, amaze us with their abilities in extreme track conditions, they keep the audience in a breathtaking trance throughout the entire race.
 Andy Fazakas and the ''Crazy Bike Club Sibiu'',  aways pleasantly surprise us with spectacular designed obstacles, extreme elements, and difficult challenges for the Competitors to race over.

 The extreme show of motorcycle competing on ["Bulevardul Coposu"](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Red+Bull+Romaniacs+hard+enduro+rallye+-+Prolog/@45.7924632,24.1489494,17z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m5!3m4!1s0x474c678ffe576269:0x53f9a4e60cb44f74!8m2!3d45.7924595!4d24.1511381)close to the centre of **[Sibiu's](http://www.sibiu.ro/)** mediaeval centre.

Competitors display their off-road riding abilities and talents directly against man made obstacles – a difficult competition, where only the very best of Competitors can win, but all Competitors are given the opportunity to qualify, to get into, and then attempt to win the ultimate prize, being on the top step of the podium and becoming **Red Bull Romaniacs Prolog Champions**.
