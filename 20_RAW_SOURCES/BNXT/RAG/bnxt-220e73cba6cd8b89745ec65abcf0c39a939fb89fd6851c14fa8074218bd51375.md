* BFGoodrich All-Terrain T/A KDR2+

The **BFGoodrich®** All Terrain T/A KDR2+’s tread pattern was carefully conceived with emphasis on its ability to clear sand when driving over dunes. To achieve this, the design is uniquely open, most notably along the shoulders. This allows the tire to generate greater traction, while limiting the risk of getting stuck on softer or looser ground. The **BFGoodrich®** All Terrain T/A KDR2+ will be available in a choice of two compounds (soft and medium) and two sizes:

16 inches (245/80-16), for all-wheel drive vehicles

17 inches (37/12.5-17), for two-wheel-drive buggies.

* BFGoodrich Mud-Terrain T/A KM3

The Mud-Terrain T/A KM3 is available to the general public. It was developed – like all **BFGoodrich® Tires** – with the benefit of experience acquired in the field. At home on sand and mud alike, and designed to both scale the steepest slopes and cope effectively with dunes, its combination makes it the perfect companion for amateur competitors.

* BFGoodrich Mud-Terrain T/A KM3 SSV

This version of the **BFGoodrich®**KM3 is designed for recreational and competitive Side by Side use. After being successfully put through its paces on a variety of cross-country rallies, the **BFGoodrich®**KM3 SSV is the tire of choice for numerous participants on the 2022 Dakar Rally, while also being available for purchase. This means users of these new **BFGoodrich®** products can enjoy the full benefit of the brand’s latest technologies, while running on the same tires as Dakar competitors when they venture off-road.

**BFGoodrich® Tires** is once again an official supplier to the Dakar in 2026, an eloquent illustration of the American brand’s unrelenting commitment.

From the start to the finish of the 47th Dakar, **BFGoodrich®**’s technicians will be on hand to analyse the tyres of the brand’s partner teams and advise on how to maximize their potential.

**BFGoodrich®**’s status as official supplier to the world’s second-biggest motorsport event mirrors its acclaimed expertise and the dominant position it enjoys in the all-terrain tyre market.

As official supplier to the 2026 Dakar, **BFGoodrich®** will offer all competitors a comprehensive range of services thanks to the **BFGoodrich® Dakar Service Center** which will operate daily from 3pm to 11pm. The **BFGoodrich® Dakar Service Center** is dismantled once the evening’s work has been completed, allowing **BFGoodrich®**’s staff to drive through the night to the next day’s bivouac.

The **BFGoodrich® Dakar Service Center**: a premium service at overnight halts for all competitors

**BFGoodrich®** will also provide a tyre fitting and removal service at the event’s overnight bivouacs at the **Dakar Service Center**. The facility comprises a truck workshop manned by around 13 experienced fitters and it is estimated that some 3,200 fitting/removal operations will be carried out in the course of the rally (cars & bikes) thanks to the 5 workshops :

* Welcome workshop
* Bar turning workshop
* Assembly/disassembly workshop
* Inflation workshop
* Tyre arrival/departure area
