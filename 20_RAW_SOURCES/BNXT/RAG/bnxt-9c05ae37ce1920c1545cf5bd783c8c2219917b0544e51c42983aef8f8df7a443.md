## Ceremony and reception space, in-house catering and event coordination available

**MILWAUKEE —** Engagement season is upon us, and the team at 1903 Events is here to help youtoast to the next chapter of your lives. Recently awarded the “Best of Weddings” award from [The Knot](https://www.theknot.com/marketplace/wedding-reception-venues-milwaukee-wi/current-winner?sort=featured) for 2020, 1903 Events offers couples complimentary private menu tastings, customizable menus, onsite event coordination and venue spaces that provide the perfect backdrop for your nuptials.

Guests who book a 2020 or 2021 wedding with 1903 Weddings at the Harley-Davidson Museum by April 30, 2020 will receive a complimentary sixth hour of hosted bar package.

Call 877-436-8738 or [click here](https://1903events.com/contact/) to contact 1903 Events. The team at 1903 Events has the resources for every wedding because 1903 Events knows you want your special day to be as unique as your love story.

Plus, read below to learn about the mouth-watering specials newly installed Executive Chef Wendy Wagner has been cooking up for January at MOTOR Bar & Restaurant.

January specials
SANDWICH OF THE MONTH
Tony Soprano Sandwich – Shaved smoked prime rib, provolone and house-made giardiniera on a toasted hoagie bun with a side of jus ($14.95).

MAC OF THE MONTH
Stick To Your Rigatoni – A hearty and zesty mac with rigatoni pasta, blue cheese, Parmesan and crushed kettle chip crust ($11.95).

DESSERT OF THE MONTH
Cookies and Cream Chocolate Mousse – Layers of chocolate mousse, whipped cream and Oreo cookie crumbles ($6.95).

COCKTAIL OF THE MONTH
Cinnamon Twist – Cure your holiday hangover with our Cinnamon Twist cocktail – Johnnie Walker Red Label, lemon juice and cinnamon simple syrup topped with ginger beer. Join us Monday-Friday from 4-6pm for buy one, get one free during Happy Hour. ($9).

**Mac Mondays**
Mondays are best spent with a bowl of MOTOR’s delicious homemade Mac n’ Cheese. Enjoy one of our Mac plates at a discounted price or create your own custom Mac with special toppings!

**Taco Tuesday**Join us every Tuesday for our weekly chef’s choice taco special for $10. And don’t forget, H-D Museum admission is just $10 on Tuesdays. And kids are just two for $10, too!

**Friday Night Fish Fry**Every Friday, our All-You-Can-Eat Wisconsin Fish Fry is available all day, served with golden fried cod, crispy French fries, fresh jicama coleslaw, homemade Southern-style cornbread and tartar sauce for only $14.95.

NEW Friday Offering: Fried Walleye with crispy smashed potatoes, campfire broccoli, fresh jicama coleslaw, artisan roll, whipped honey butter and tartar sauce for $18.95 (not all you can eat).

**Bulleit Bourbon presents BBQ & Blues**Every Saturday, take in live performances from a rotating lineup of blues musicians, plus all-you-can-eat, hickory-smoked BBQ chicken, pulled pork, smoked brisket and grilled sausage links for $29.99. Add an order of Memphis-style, dry-rubbed ribs for just $5 more. All platters come with French fries, jicama coleslaw, BBQ baked beans and made-from-scratch cornbread.

**Build-Your-Own Bloody Mary Bar Sundays**
Available all day – We give you vodka and our signature Bloody Mary mix in a pint glass and you pile it high with all your favorite toppings!

**Happy Hour**
Monday-Friday, 4-6 p.m.
BOGO specials for the beer and cocktail of the month, plus $1 off select appetizers.

About MOTOR® Bar & Restaurant
The only Harley-Davidson Museum® restaurant, MOTOR® Bar & Restaurant offers guests riverfront dining and Midwest camaraderie with a dose of genuine Harley-Davidson® attitude. MOTOR™ takes diners on a culinary road trip of great American food and drink from its location in downtown Milwaukee. Guests can enjoy burgers, BBQ, seasonal favorites and a whole lot more.
