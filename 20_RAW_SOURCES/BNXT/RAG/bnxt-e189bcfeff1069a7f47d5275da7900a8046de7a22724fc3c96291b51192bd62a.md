### WHO IS THIS FOR?

The Estrela Adventure Rally Tour is for riders who want a proper Adventure Rally Challenge abroad without the stress of organising hotels, logistics and support.  It’s designed specifically for adventure bikes and riders who want a week of epic riding, great company and premium evenings.

Surface and tyres:  Mostly easy going trails and gravel tracks, with tarmac sections to get from one area to another, suitable for big adventure bikes with dual sport or knobbly tyres.

### TAKE ON THE CHALLENGE

Escape the impending winter and prolonge the adventure season on a fully supported, seven day roadbook adventure along some of the best off-road tracks in Europe.  Expect scenic, winding mountain passes, wide open gravel roads with far reaching views, and everything in between!

Each day will be a new roadbook, available via the RallyMoto digital App, so there is minimum kit required.  Each evening we will review your ride, acknowledge any ‘Zero Heroes’ (those who’ve navigated perfectly and not racked up any speeding penalties), and enjoy a bit of banter over an evening meal.

Take note that this is a navigation adventure, not a speed event!

### where you’ll ride

* **Picos De Europa:** Big scenery and mountain passes straight off the ferry
* **Douro Valley**:  Vineyard ribbons and big river views.
* **Serra Da Estrela**:  Home to the highest peak in Portugal, and riddled with amazing trails.
* **Manteigas – Piodao:** Iconic historic villages set deep within the national park.
* **Serra Do Caramulo:**  Remote tracks and relentless twisties.

Every night ends in a hand-picked hotel with dinner together and a briefing for tomorrow’s roadbook.
