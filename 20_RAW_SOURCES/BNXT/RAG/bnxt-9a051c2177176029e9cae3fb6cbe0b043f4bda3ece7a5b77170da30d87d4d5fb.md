# Tread - Overland Edition Tread XL - Overland Edition Owner's Manual - Parking Near Your Destination

# Parking Near Your Destination

Your device can help you find a parking spot near your destination. When you approach some destinations, the device automatically prompts you to search for parking.

1. Select an option:
   * When the device prompts you, select
     Yes to search for nearby parking.
   * If the device does not prompt you, select
     Where To > Categories > Parking, and select
      > My Destination.
2. Select a parking location, and select
   Go! > Add as Next Stop.

The device guides you to the parking area.
