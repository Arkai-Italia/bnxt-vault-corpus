# Rights

###### **The American Motorcyclist Association wants to make the United States the best place in the world to be a motorcyclist.**

That’s why the AMA Government Relations team works tirelessly to protect and promote the interests of American motorcyclists at local, state, federal, and even international levels. Supporting pro-motorcycling legislation, working to defeat anti-rider regulations, and advocating for the interests of motorcyclists of all types are just a few of the ways the AMA fights every day to protect your rights as a motorcycle rider.
