# Tread - SXS Edition Owner's Manual - Syncing inReach Data

# Syncing inReach Data

You can sync data from your
[explore.garmin.com](http://www.explore.garmin.com) account to your device using the
Tread® app. After you make edits to your data, such as plan changes, contacts, preset messages, or quick text messages, you must sync them to your device using the mobile app.

Open the
Tread app.

Changes made to data such as collections, waypoints, routes, and tracks update automatically.

You can now sync your data to your
Tread SXS Edition device.
