# Raven joins centenary 6DAYS party with fresh special-edition Ren goggles

Published on September 6th, 2026 by Nye Davis

#### **Goggle specialist Raven has been announced as a main partner for this year’s centenary edition of the 6DAYS FIM Enduro of Nations in Portugal – and is marking the occasion with a new limited-edition goggle.**

The Swedish in-house brand of gear and kit giant 24MX is celebrating the 100th running of the world’s largest enduro with its glossy new Raven Ren 6DAYS Edition goggles.

Raven first partnered with the event in 2025, launching a limited-edition Rysen goggle for the Italian edition. The company says its expanded involvement this year reflects its ‘growing commitment to enduro’.

The new specs are described by the firm as its ‘next generation of race-focused eyewear’. Sounds suitably flash we’d say, and at a UK retail price of £49.99, the Rens shouldn’t break the bank, either. Raven’s existing Edge model is also available at a hefty discount at the time of penning this.

**Here’s what Raven has to say about the new model:**

*“Developed and field-tested with professional riders, Ren is built for motocross and enduro riders who need clear vision, secure fit and fast adaptability when conditions change.*

*At the centre of the goggles is a pre-curved, injection-moulded high-impact lens. The construction provides a clear field of vision while helping protect the rider’s eyes from roost, rocks and flying debris. UV400 protection and an anti-scratch coating help maintain visibility through demanding riding conditions.*

*A dustproof frame and triple-layer, moisture-wicking foam create a close seal against dust and silt while helping manage sweat during long days on the bike.*

*When conditions change, the Rapid Quick-Lens System allows riders to swap lenses within seconds. The goggles are also compatible with roll-off systems for maintaining visibility in wet and muddy race conditions.*

*A 48mm silicone-backed strap provides a secure grip against the helmet, helping keep the goggles firmly in position over rough terrain.*

*Each set comes with a bonus interchangeable lens and a custom 6DAYS 100 commemorative goggle pouch.”*

Among the pros currently using Raven goggles are Zach Pichon (pictured on his TM above) and Kyron Bacon, alongside Junior rider Will Dennett and 2026 world champions Alberto Elgari and Pietro Scardina.

The limited-edition 6DAYS Rens are already available to buy through 24MX, before making their official appearance at the 100th 6DAYS in Portugal next month.

*“We are proud to continue our collaboration with the 6DAYS and the FIM, and to be part of an event that means so much to enduro riders around the world,” said Davide Rinaldi, Head of Partnerships at Raven.*

*“Reaching 100 editions is a massive achievement and something the whole enduro community can celebrate.”*

F.I.M. Marketing & Digital Director Fabio Muner added:

*“We are delighted to welcome Raven for the second consecutive year as a Main Partner of the FIM 6DAYS, and especially for this historic 100th Edition.*

*“We truly appreciate their continued commitment to the event and are proud to celebrate this milestone together with the launch of the limited-edition Raven Ren 6DAYS goggle – a unique product for a truly unique edition.”*
