# **DAKAR 2026 STAGE-WINNER SARA PRICE TO RACE FOR DEFENDER IN 2026**

## **<u>Key points</u>**

- **American rally driver, motocross champion and four-time Dakar stage-winner Sara Price completes the Defender driver line-up for the 2026 Dakar**

- **The British brand Defender will enter three-cars into the Dakar in 2026, the halo event of the FIA World Rally-Raid Championship (W2RC)**

- **In 2024, California’s Sara Price made history as the first American woman and thirdever woman to win a stage in the Dakar**

- **Sara joins Dakar legend Stéphane Peterhansel and talented Rokas Baciuska to complete world-class Defender driver line-up into the ‘Stock’ category of Dakar and W2RC 2026**

Defender has signed Sara Price to compete in the 2026 edition of the world’s most epic long-distance off-road race. Signing Sara sees Defender complete its world-class driver line-up ahead of the adventure brand making its competitive manufacturer team debut in the 2026 Dakar, an extreme offroad test of human and machine. Sara joins fourteen-time Dakar winner Stéphane Peterhansel and promising talent Rokas Baciuska, in a trio of competition-spec Defender D7X-R rally cars in the ‘Stock’ category for production-based cars.

In 2024, Sara made her Dakar debut in the SSV class, becoming the first American woman and third woman ever to win a Dakar stage. She went on to finish fourth overall in the rally and won the title for best rookie. Sara now joins Defender as the brand embarks on its inaugural year competing in the Dakar as a works entry.

**Sara Price said** : _“To join the works Defender team in its competitive debut at the Dakar will be a huge honour, and I cannot wait to get started with the team. The Dakar is the pinnacle of rally-raid competition and I’m extremely proud to have competed in this competition before. Defender is an iconic vehicle with extreme capabilities, as well as a brand fuelled by purpose, so to drive for this British adventure brand at such a huge moment in its history is an amazing accomplishment for me. The testing and preparation work that is going behind the scenes is insanely impressive to see and I’m excited to get behind the wheel. As a born racer, I’m very competitive and working with Defender I know that we are aiming high and won’t rest until we succeed.”_

**Mark Cameron, Managing Director, Defender, said** : _"Wheels are well and truly in motion for our Dakar debut. Testing is going well, and we have now completed our driver line-up. Signing the brilliantly talented Sara Price is a huge moment for Defender. We have a powerful, capable and durable 4x4 in the Defender Dakar D7X-R, and our skilled and strong-minded drivers will showcase both Defender and explore the limits of human endurance as they navigate the desert dunes and arid plains of the Dakar in January.”_

It comes as Defender continues to progress preparations for competing in the 2026 World Rally-Raid Championship (W2RC) with successful tests held in a variety of off-road environments. The brand will make its manufacturer team debut at the Dakar where all three drivers will compete, Peterhansel and Baciuska then also competing in the remaining rounds of the 2026 W2RC.

# **About Defender**

# **Defender Rally**

Defender embraces the impossible, and Defender Rally embodies this to the extreme, demonstrating the ultimate capability, reliability and endurance in the most challenging of environments.

In 2025 Defender became the official car partner of the Dakar, the partnership set to continue in 2026, 2027 and 2028. From 2026, Defender will also compete in the FIA World Rally-Raid Championship (W2RC), entering the ‘Stock’ category – this being the first of a three-year programme.

Defender’s capability is designed to take on the most challenging of conditions, with Dakar being the ultimate test. The competition car retains the same tough, purpose-engineered D7x body architecture as the Defender, based on a lightweight aluminium monocoque construction to create a stiff body structure, in addition to featuring the 4.4-litre twin turbo V8 engine from Defender OCTA to provide exceptional driveability.

While Defender will not compete in Dakar until 2026, it played a key role at the 2025 rally as the event’s official vehicle partner. A fleet of 20 Defenders supported the event, which began in Bisha, Saudi Arabia, and ended in Shubaytah, transporting race officials and VIP media. A further six highly-specialised Defender recce vehicles will be used by Dakar organisers to plan the routes for the 2026, 2027 and 2028 events.
