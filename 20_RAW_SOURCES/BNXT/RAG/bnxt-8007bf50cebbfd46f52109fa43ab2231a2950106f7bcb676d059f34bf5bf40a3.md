## Broad visibility for moving specialists who join host of long-term partners

**Zug, Switzerland**– Infront, a Wanda Sports Group company and the exclusive [marketing partner](https://www.infront.sport/en/services/sponsorship) of Stade Toulousain, has secured moving specialists DMAX as the new official supplier for the French rugby club.

The agreement will see DMAX's logo printed on the club's shorts during all [Heineken Champions Cup](https://www.epcrugby.com/champions-cup/) matches, enjoying pan-European presence.

Stade Toulousain, which holds long-term partnerships with Nike, Airbus and Peugeot, also continues to attract new sponsors after AKKA, the engineering and technology consulting group for the airline industry, preceded DMAX in signing up as a new official partner in 2019.
