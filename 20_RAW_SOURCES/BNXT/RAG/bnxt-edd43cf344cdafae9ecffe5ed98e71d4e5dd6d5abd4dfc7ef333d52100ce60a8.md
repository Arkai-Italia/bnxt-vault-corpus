# BirdsEye Direct Satellite Imagery

BirdsEye direct satellite imagery provides downloadable, high-resolution map imagery, including detailed satellite imagery and raster-based topographical maps. You can download
BirdsEye direct satellite imagery directly to your device while it is connected to a
Wi‑Fi® network.
