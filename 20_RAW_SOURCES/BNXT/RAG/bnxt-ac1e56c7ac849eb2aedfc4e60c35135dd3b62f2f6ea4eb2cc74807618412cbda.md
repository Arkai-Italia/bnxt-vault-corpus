# XEF 250 Trail

XEF 250 Trail

Fantic TL 250 is the perfect combination of power and ease of riding.

The advanced technology and the continuous research for improvement, make it ideal to ride on the most difficult enduro tracks, but it will permit your skills to develop for an easier ride.

Nothing will ever seem unreachable, relying completely on the performance and intuitive chassis of a motorcycle designed and built entirely in Italy.

An exciting enduro bike, easy and with very low maintenance. Updated aesthetics, inspired by the Fantic Racing models.

**ENGINE**

The **new Fantic TL 250** is equipped with a 250cc 4-stroke liquid-cooled engine with **electronic fuel injection** and **a brand new ECU**, **Arrow exhaust and silencer.**

**CHASSIS**

The CrMo steel perimeter frame of the TL 250 has been optimized in terms of rigidity thanks to the placement of new reinforcements for the benefit of performance.

The **TL 250 is equipped with a FANTIC FRS 43mm USD racing-derived fork**, and **FANTIC FRS 250 Piggyback monoshock** - preload and hydraulic adjustment.

The swingarm is made of aluminum with variable section and removable rear sub-frame.

Enduro tires 90/90x21 - 120/90x18 mounted on aluminum rims. Powerful and modular 260mm front and 220mm rear wave brake disks.

**EQUIPMENT**

The **TL 250 is the bike for those who want to challenge themselves on technical off-road enduro trails,** with similar components to the Racing models.

The equipment includes 43mm Red Fantic FRS front fork, FSR rear suspension with piggy back, preload and hydraulic adjustment, aluminum swingarm, anodized rear brake master cylinder protection, CNC machined triple clamps, racing brake system with adjustable reach, black anodized silencer, handguards, and lightweight ergal crown.

The new front mask mounts a full-LED headlight.
