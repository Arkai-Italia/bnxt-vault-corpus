# Challenges - RallyMoto

# **RallyMoto Challenges**

### Challenges are typically remote events that run in the RallyMoto digital app, and take place over a number of months.

You can ride the routes in your own time, picking the better days to ride, terms of the weather or getting together with your riding mates..

Everything is scored and there is a leaderboard so you compare your results with the other riders.

# ICE BREAKER CHALLENGE

Keep your wheels rolling all winter long!  This remote event was the first of its kind, with multiple roadbook routes throughout the UK to ride over the winter months.  Typically from December 1st – March 1st.

Find out more on the Ice Breaker Challenge [here](https://rallymoto.co.uk/ice-breaker-challenge-2026/).

# CANNONBALL CAFE CHALLENGE

A mega challenge consisting of over 2400kms of 100% tarmac routes across the UK.  Riders had 24 separate roadbooks to choose from.

See the 2025 results [here.](https://rallymoto.co.uk/2025-cannonball-cafe-challenge-league-table/)
