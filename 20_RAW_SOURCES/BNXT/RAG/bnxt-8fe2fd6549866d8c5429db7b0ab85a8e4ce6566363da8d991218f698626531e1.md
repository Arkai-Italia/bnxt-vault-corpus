# Galfer expands Triumph partnership for 2026 6DAYS

Published on October 9th, 2026 by Enduro21 Team

**Galfer will support the Triumph JET Race Service at the 2026 6DAYS FIM Enduro of Nations, while also backing a strong roster of riders including Brad Freeman, Nathan Watson and Rachel Gutish.**

The braking specialist will provide technical support and components to riders using the Triumph JET Race Service in Grândola, Portugal, including its Shark and Wave discs and Racing brake pads. Enduro21 previously put Galfer’s Shark discs to the test. Read our full review [here](https://enduro21.com/en/news/tested-long-term-testing-galfer-shark-brake-discs-for-enduro).

Galfer is already an original equipment supplier for Triumph’s enduro range, providing brake discs for both production models and the Triumph Racing Factory Team. The renewed partnership extends that relationship into the 2026 6DAYS.

Among the riders supported by Galfer this year are Brit duo Brad Freeman and Nathan Watson, American Women’s World Trophy rider Rachel Gutish and Australian Joshua Strang. The supported line-up also includes Joulie Roussaly and Keo Yoyon with Team France, Morgan Lesiardo and Samuele Bernardini with Team Italy, Yago Martínez, Alex Puey and Marc Sans with Team Spain, and Max Alhinn and Michael Persson with Team Sweden.

Galfer joined the FIM Quality Product (FQP) label programme in late 2024, gaining the internationally recognised seal of approval for products and services used both on and off the track.
