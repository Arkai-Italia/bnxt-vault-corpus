# Tread - SXS Edition Owner's Manual - Addressable LED Wiring Considerations

# Addressable LED Wiring Considerations

You can wire addressable LED lights to your
Tread® Audio Box. For more information, see the manual for your addressable LED lights.

Compatible addressable LED types:

* WS2812B
* WS2813

| Addressable LED | LED Wire Color and Function |
| --- | --- |
| Addressable LED 1 | Red: +5V Power  White: Ground  Green with a black stripe: Data |
| Addressable LED 2 | Red: +5V Power  White: Ground  Green with a black stripe: Data |
